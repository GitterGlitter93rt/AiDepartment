HVAC replacements, plumbing replacements, electrical upgrades, roofing, pools, outdoor living projects, garage door replacements, generators, and restoration-related reconstruction all generate significant quoted work across the home-service trades — and nearly all of them share the same gap: a consistent process for following up after the estimate is sent.

## The Workflow

The estimate goes out, gets acknowledged, creates a salesperson task, triggers scheduled follow-up, generates a customer response, leads to a real human conversation, and results in either a sale, a move into nurture, or an honest closed-lost status if the customer went another direction.

## AI Assistance

AI can summarize the original job history, draft contextual follow-up messages, surface which estimates have gone stale, help organize the reasons a deal is delayed, create tasks automatically, and maintain a consistent follow-up cadence without requiring manual tracking.

## Segmentation

Not every open estimate is in the same place — some were just quoted, some are actively engaged, some are waiting on financing or timing, some are waiting on another decision entirely, some have gone quiet, some represent a future project rather than a current one, and some are genuinely closed-lost. Treating all of these the same way wastes the real value still sitting in your pipeline.

## Human Role

Pricing, diagnosis, design, technical questions, negotiation, customer objections, and any scope changes all stay with your team — automation supports the administrative follow-up around those conversations, not the conversations themselves.
