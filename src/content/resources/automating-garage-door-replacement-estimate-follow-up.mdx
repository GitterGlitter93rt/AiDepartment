A door replacement estimate involves more decisions than a repair — style, materials, opener options, insulation, budget, and timing all factor in, which means the homeowner usually needs more than a single conversation before they're ready to commit. Structured follow-up keeps that opportunity active during the decision window instead of letting it go quiet.

## The Workflow

The estimate goes out, gets acknowledged, creates a follow-up task, triggers a helpful reminder, leads to a real conversation, and results in either a decision or a move into nurture if the customer needs more time — a consistent path instead of one that depends on a salesperson remembering to check back.

## AI Assistance

AI can summarize the estimate history so the follow-up conversation picks up where the original visit left off, draft contextual communication, identify which quotes have gone stale, create reminders, and support task management for your sales team.

## Human Responsibility

Product recommendations, measurements, installation conditions, pricing, safety considerations, and negotiation all stay with the person who actually visited the property — automation handles the reminders and administrative follow-up around that conversation, not the conversation itself.
