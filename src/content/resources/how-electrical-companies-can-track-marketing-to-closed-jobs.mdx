Not all electrical leads are equal — a simple outlet repair inquiry and a $15,000 panel-and-generator project can come through the exact same form, and a marketing report that treats them the same is measuring the wrong thing. Marketing should ultimately be evaluated based on the jobs and projects it actually produces, not the volume of calls or forms it generates.

## The Full Chain

The measurement worth building runs from marketing source to inquiry, to a scheduled appointment, to an estimate, to a closed job, and finally to the revenue that job represents. Each stage is a real checkpoint where you can see whether a source is producing genuine business or just inbound noise.

## Building the Data

Call tracking, consistent form data, a CRM or field-service platform that preserves the original lead source as the opportunity moves through your process, closed-job reporting tied back to that source, and offline conversion feedback where your ad platforms support it all need to work together — and the lead-source preservation step is usually where this breaks down first.
