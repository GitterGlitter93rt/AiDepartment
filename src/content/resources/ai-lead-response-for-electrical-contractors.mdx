A routine service call, a generator installation, an EV charger request, and a commercial project bid can all arrive through the same phone line or contact form — but they need very different follow-up, and treating them identically means the higher-value opportunities often get the same generic handling as a simple service ticket.

## Lead Types

Service calls, panel upgrades, generators, EV chargers, remodels, commercial projects, new construction, and routine maintenance inquiries are all common inbound categories, each with its own urgency, sales cycle, and follow-up needs.

## The Workflow

An inquiry gets acknowledged, moves through basic classification to identify which category it falls into, gets routed appropriately, leads to an appointment or an estimate, and reaches the right human professional — a technician for a service call, an estimator for a larger quoted project.

## AI Assistance

Information collection, routing, scheduling, reminders, follow-up, and CRM task creation are all reasonable for AI-assisted workflows to handle consistently, freeing your team from the administrative overhead of manually sorting every inbound inquiry.

## Human Responsibility

Technical diagnosis, electrical safety, code compliance, pricing, engineering, and permitting decisions all require qualified professionals — automation's role stops at getting the right information to the right person quickly.
