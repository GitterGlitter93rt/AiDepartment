A same-day drain clog gets resolved in one visit. A water heater replacement, a repipe, a sewer project, or a larger remodel usually doesn't — these bigger opportunities often take more than one conversation to close, and that gap is exactly where inconsistent follow-up quietly costs a plumbing business real revenue.

## Relevant Estimate Types

Water heaters, repiping, sewer work, remodels, fixture packages, larger replacements, and commercial projects all share the same pattern: a technician identifies the opportunity, an estimate goes out, and the customer needs time to decide — time your follow-up process either supports or wastes.

## The Workflow

A structured sequence handles this consistently: the estimate goes out, gets acknowledged, creates a task, triggers scheduled follow-up, generates a customer response, leads to a real human conversation, and results in either booked work or a move into longer-term nurture if the timing isn't right yet.

## AI Assistance

AI can summarize the original job notes so nothing gets lost between the technician's visit and the follow-up call, draft contextual messages, create follow-up reminders at sensible intervals, prioritize which stale estimates need attention first, and keep CRM status accurate without someone manually updating it after every interaction.

## Human Responsibility

Pricing and any technical recommendation about what work is actually needed stay with your qualified plumbers — automation handles the administrative follow-up around the decision, not the decision itself.
