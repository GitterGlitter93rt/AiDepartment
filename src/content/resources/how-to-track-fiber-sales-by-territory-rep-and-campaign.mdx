A fiber territory launch usually runs several acquisition channels at once — door-to-door reps, local marketing, referrals, and sometimes an outsourced sales partner, all working the same geography simultaneously. Without clear attribution, it's genuinely difficult to know which of those channels is actually producing activated, paying customers versus which one just looks busy.

## Establish Identity

The foundation is consistent tracking of address, customer, representative, territory, and lead source for every contact — captured the same way regardless of which channel generated it, so the data can actually be compared apples to apples later.

## Follow the Lifecycle

From there, the lifecycle worth tracking runs from lead to appointment, to order, to installation, to activation. Each of those stages is a real checkpoint where a prospect can stall out, and knowing where that's happening — rather than just knowing the final activation count — is what actually helps you fix the process.

## Management Reporting

With that lifecycle data in place, real questions become answerable: which territories are converting well and which aren't? Which reps are producing activated customers, not just signed forms? Which campaigns are generating installations, not just leads that never went anywhere? And where in the handoff between sales and installation are things actually breaking down?
