Leasing inquiries don't respect business hours, but leasing teams have limited availability — someone browsing listings at 9 p.m. isn't going to wait until morning if a competing property responds first. AI-assisted workflows can improve response speed, deliver approved information, qualify interest, and coordinate scheduling, while any sensitive housing decision stays firmly with people.

## Inquiry Sources

Property websites, listing portals, phone calls, email, advertising, and referrals all generate leasing inquiries, and each deserves the same basic response standard regardless of channel.

## Immediate Response

A workflow can acknowledge the inquiry, collect contact information, identify which property or unit the person is interested in, provide approved factual information, ask about scheduling preference, and offer approved showing availability — enough to move the conversation forward without requiring a person to be immediately available.

## Showing Scheduling

The path runs from inquiry to approved information, to scheduling, to a confirmation, to a reminder, to the actual showing, and into human follow-up afterward.

## AI Guardrails

Any information the system provides — availability, amenities, pricing, policies, location, showing instructions — needs to come from verified property data. AI should never invent availability, guess at pricing, overstate features, imply a concession that isn't actually being offered, or state a policy that isn't your real policy.

## Human Responsibility

Application decisions, exceptions to standard policy, disputes, fair-housing-sensitive situations, legal questions, and unusual accommodation requests all require a person's judgment and accountability — automation handles the scheduling and information layer, not the actual leasing decision.
