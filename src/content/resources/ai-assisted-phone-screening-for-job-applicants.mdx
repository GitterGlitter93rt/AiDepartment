High-volume employers — home services companies, dealer groups, solar and field-sales organizations, restoration businesses, skilled trades, and multi-location employers generally — often need to screen far more applicants than a recruiting team can realistically call individually. AI-assisted phone screening can gather consistent, job-related information from every applicant before a human ever gets involved, without pretending to make the actual hiring decision.

## Example Workflow

An application is received, gets acknowledged, the candidate receives an invitation for an approved screening call, an AI-assisted phone conversation happens, a transcript is generated, a structured summary is produced, a human recruiter reviews it, an interview decision gets made, and scheduling follows from there.

## Appropriate Questions

Depending on the role, reasonable screening questions might cover relevant work experience, interest in the specific role, availability, work location, any job-related licenses or certifications, schedule requirements, willingness to perform clearly described job duties, and a chance for the candidate to ask their own questions. What should never appear here are medical questions, anything touching protected characteristics, or personal questions unrelated to the job itself.

## What the AI Produces

The useful outputs are a transcript, a concise summary, structured responses to the actual questions asked, flags for any missing information, suggested follow-up questions for the recruiter, and an updated workflow status — all information that helps a human make a faster, better-informed decision.

## What AI Should Not Do

The system should never be positioned as independently deciding to hire or reject a candidate, determining compensation, assessing suitability based on protected information, or handling sensitive employment issues on its own.

## Candidate Experience

Candidates deserve to know what they're dealing with: the system should identify itself appropriately, explain the purpose of the screening clearly, offer a path to reach a human, and never pretend to be a human recruiter.
