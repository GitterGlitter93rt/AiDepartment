Solar sales organizations often generate a large volume of leads — through paid media, referrals, the website, events, door-to-door canvassing, and partner relationships — and then lose a meaningful share of that volume to inconsistent follow-up. High lead volume with weak follow-up doesn't produce more sales. It produces more acquisition cost spent on leads that never get a real conversation.

## Where Leads Come From

Each source has a slightly different follow-up expectation. A paid-media lead who just filled out a form expects a fast response. A referral expects a warmer, more personal touch. A door-to-door lead has already had a real conversation with a rep and expects that relationship to continue, not restart from zero with a stranger.

## Immediate Response

Regardless of source, the first layer should confirm the inquiry, assign it to the right rep, offer scheduling where appropriate, and notify the right person internally so nothing sits unclaimed. This is the difference between a lead that gets worked within the hour and one that gets worked whenever someone happens to notice it.

## Multi-Step Follow-Up

From there, a structured sequence — SMS, email, a phone call, a task assigned to the salesperson, and a longer-term nurture track for anyone not ready to move forward yet — keeps the lead warm without depending entirely on one person's memory and discipline.

## Human Role

Customer-specific economics, financing conversations, system recommendations, objection handling, closing, and any consumer questions that require real judgment all stay with your sales team. Automation's job is to make sure they're having those conversations with leads who are actually still engaged, not chasing ones that went cold three weeks ago.

## Compliance Guardrail

None of this should come at the expense of consent and communication rules. Automated calls, texts, and emails should respect consent, honor opt-outs, follow applicable telemarketing regulations, and stay within your company's own communication policy. This is not a framework for mass unsolicited outreach — it's a framework for following up reliably with people who already expressed real interest.
