Generators, panel upgrades, EV chargers, lighting projects, remodels, and commercial bids all involve quoted work that a homeowner or business doesn't decide on the spot. When follow-up depends entirely on an individual estimator remembering to check back in, a meaningful share of that quoted work simply goes quiet.

## The Workflow

A structured cadence handles this consistently: the estimate goes out, gets acknowledged, creates a scheduled task, triggers follow-up, generates a customer response, leads to a real conversation with the estimator, and results in either the sale or a move into longer-term nurture if the customer isn't ready yet.

## AI Assistance

AI can summarize the original site-visit notes, draft approved follow-up messages appropriate to the type of project, surface which quotes have gone stale and need attention, create tasks automatically, and keep pipeline status organized without requiring manual upkeep.

## What Stays With Estimators

The actual technical scope, pricing, and any recommendation about what electrical work is needed remain squarely with your qualified estimators — automation supports the process around that judgment, not the judgment itself.
