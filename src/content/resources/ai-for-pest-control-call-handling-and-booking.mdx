A pest control business fields a lot of repetitive calls — "do you treat ants," "can someone come this week," "what's your process for termites" — mixed in with genuinely urgent, high-intent service requests. AI-assisted intake is well-suited to the repetitive part, as long as it's designed to recognize when a call needs a real person instead.

## What AI Can Handle

Collecting customer information, the property address, a general pest category, a preferred appointment window, whether the caller is an existing customer, answers to common approved questions, and basic booking workflows are all reasonable for structured intake to manage — this is the bulk of routine call volume for most pest control businesses.

## What Should Escalate

Safety concerns, chemical exposure questions, unusual infestations, anything with a medical dimension, property-specific treatment advice, disputes, and commercial compliance issues should all route to a qualified person immediately. These aren't edge cases to handle eventually — they're situations where a wrong or generic answer could actually matter.

## Missed Calls

A missed call in this business is often a missed booking that goes straight to the next company in a search result. An immediate recovery workflow — a quick callback or text the moment a call goes unanswered — recovers a meaningful share of that lost opportunity.

## Booking Integration

The full path looks like: a call or form comes in, moves through intake, gets matched to an approved service type, gets scheduled, receives a confirmation, and flows into your technician's actual workflow — a clean, consistent path from first contact to a booked appointment on the schedule.
