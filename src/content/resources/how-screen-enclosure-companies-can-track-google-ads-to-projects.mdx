A form submission or a phone call is a signal, not a business outcome. For a screen enclosure company, the real measurement chain runs from a Google Ads click through the lead, a site visit, an estimate, a signed project, and finally revenue — and skipping any of those steps in your reporting leaves you optimizing for the wrong thing.

## Building the Chain

This requires several pieces working together: website tracking to see what happens after the click, call tracking for phone-driven inquiries, UTM parameters applied consistently, CRM source fields that don't get overwritten as the lead moves through your process, recorded appointment outcomes, estimate status, signed-project status, and — where your platforms support it — offline conversion feedback that tells Google which leads actually became customers.

## Management Questions Worth Answering

With that chain in place, real questions become answerable: which campaigns are producing actual site visits, not just clicks? Which are producing estimates? Which are producing signed projects? Which specific keywords are generating a lot of volume but poor-quality inquiries? Which geographic markets are producing genuinely better opportunities than others?
