A high-value landscaping or outdoor-living lead rarely closes in a single conversation. It typically involves a consultation, a site evaluation, a design discussion, and often more than one estimate revision before a homeowner signs. AI's real value here isn't in any of those human interactions — it's in the gaps between them, where follow-up quietly succeeds or fails.

## Lead Sources

Google Ads, organic search, referrals, social media, home shows, and direct website inquiries are all common entry points, each deserving the same basic follow-up discipline even though the tone of outreach might differ.

## The Workflow

A lead arrives, gets acknowledged, moves through qualification, leads to a consultation, receives consistent follow-up, results in a design or estimate, and eventually a decision — with AI supporting every step that isn't the actual design or sales conversation itself.

## AI Assistance

Scheduling, reminders, summarizing notes from a site visit or consultation, drafting follow-up communication, keeping CRM records current, and flagging stale leads that need attention are all reasonable for automation to handle reliably.

## Human Responsibility

Design, site conditions, drainage, material selection, pricing, project scope, and construction feasibility all require your team's real expertise — a property's specific conditions can't be evaluated by a system that's never seen the site.
