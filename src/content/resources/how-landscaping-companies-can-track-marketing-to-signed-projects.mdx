A landscaping business running both Google Ads and Meta campaigns needs to know more than which one produces more leads — it needs to know which one produces serious consultations, real estimates, and signed projects. Those are very different outcomes, and a source that looks strong on lead count can look weak once you measure all the way to a signed contract.

## The Full Chain

The chain worth tracking runs from marketing source to lead, to a consultation, to an estimate, to a signed project, and finally to revenue. Calls, forms, paid media, and organic search all feed into this, but only if the source information survives the trip through your CRM and proposal or estimate system.

## Where This Breaks Down

The most common failure point isn't the initial tracking — it's source preservation. A lead captured cleanly with the right UTM data often loses that context the moment it's handed to a designer or salesperson working in a separate estimate tool. Fixing that handoff is usually worth more than adding any new tracking pixel.
