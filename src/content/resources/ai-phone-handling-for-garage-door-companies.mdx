A garage door call is almost always high-intent — a door that won't open, a spring that snapped, a car trapped inside. Customers in that situation call more than one company and book whichever one actually answers first. AI-assisted answering and missed-call recovery exist to make sure that's consistently you.

## Intake

A quick intake step collects customer information, the address, a general description of the issue, whether it's a door or opener problem, preferred timing, and whether the situation is a genuine emergency — enough to route the call correctly without slowing the customer down.

## Workflow

A call comes in, moves through intake, reaches approved scheduling or routing, gets confirmed, and hands off to a technician — a fast, consistent path from first contact to a booked appointment.

## Missed Calls

When a call is missed, an immediate acknowledgment to the customer, a callback workflow, and a dispatch notification recover a real share of what would otherwise become a lost job to a faster-responding competitor.

## Human Escalation

Safety issues, a trapped vehicle or person, structural problems, diagnosis, pricing, and unusual repair conditions all need a technician's real judgment immediately — automation's job is to get the call answered and routed fast, not to make any of these calls itself.
