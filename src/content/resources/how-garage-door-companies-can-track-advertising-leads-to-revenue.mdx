A garage door company's leads split into two very different outcomes — a quick repair visit and a full replacement project — and a marketing report that only counts clicks or calls can't tell you which campaigns are producing which. Real measurement should distinguish between ad clicks, inbound calls, booked service, and the revenue that comes from repairs versus replacements.

## The Full Chain

The chain worth tracking runs from campaign to call or form, to a booking, to the actual service visit, to either a repair or an estimate for replacement, to a completed job and its revenue. Repair and replacement jobs have very different values, and lumping them together in reporting hides which campaigns are actually driving your higher-value work.

## Building the Data

Google Ads, Local Services Ads where you use them, call tracking, your CRM or field-service platform, consistent source fields, and offline conversion feedback all need to work together — and specifically tracking whether a completed job was a repair or a replacement is what makes this reporting actually useful for budget decisions.
