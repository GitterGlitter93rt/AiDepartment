Plumbing demand is often immediate — a customer with an overflowing toilet or a burst pipe isn't going to wait around. An unanswered call in that moment doesn't just delay the job, it frequently means the customer simply calls the next plumber on the list. AI-assisted answering and missed-call recovery exist specifically to close that gap.

## Inbound Call Workflow

A well-designed call flow identifies the customer, captures the general service type, gauges urgency, moves toward scheduling or routing, confirms the appointment, and hands off to a person or technician for anything that needs real judgment — all without making the caller wait through an unnecessarily long phone tree.

## Missed-Call Workflow

When a call still goes unanswered — busy dispatch, after-hours, a brief gap in coverage — an immediate acknowledgment and callback request, paired with an internal notification and routing back to a person, recovers a meaningful share of calls that would otherwise just go to a competitor.

## Appropriate AI Tasks

Collecting contact information, gathering a basic problem description, scheduling within approved appointment categories, providing basic business information, and notifying dispatch are all reasonable for AI-assisted intake to handle reliably and consistently.

## Human Tasks

Diagnosis, safety judgment, pricing, code questions, specific repair recommendations, and emergency judgment calls all require a qualified plumber or dispatcher — this is where the actual expertise your business is built on comes into play, and automation should never attempt to substitute for it.
