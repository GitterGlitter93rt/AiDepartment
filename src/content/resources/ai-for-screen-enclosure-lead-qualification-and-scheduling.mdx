A new-construction enclosure inquiry and a small repair request aren't the same conversation, but they often arrive through the exact same contact form. AI-assisted intake can sort out that difference early, gather the information that actually matters, and get a site visit scheduled with the right person — without pretending to evaluate the property itself.

## Capture Useful Information

A short intake step can collect contact information, the property location, the type of project, whether there's an existing structure involved, whether it's a new enclosure or a repair, the customer's desired timeline, and their preference for how a consultation happens. This is enough to route the lead correctly without requiring the customer to fill out a lengthy form.

## Route Appropriately

From there, a lead moves through basic qualification, gets assigned to the right salesperson or estimator, gets a site visit scheduled, receives a reminder, and flows into your estimate workflow — a clear, consistent path instead of an ad hoc process that varies by who happens to pick up the inquiry.

## Scheduling

Calendar availability, confirmations, reminders, rescheduling, and internal notifications all reduce the friction of actually getting a site visit booked and kept — a surprising number of lost opportunities in this industry come down to a site visit that never got properly scheduled in the first place.

## Human Judgment

Structural feasibility, engineering requirements, permit requirements, final pricing, and site-specific scope decisions all require your estimator's actual eyes on the property. AI-assisted intake gets the right information to the right person quickly — it doesn't make any of those judgment calls itself.
