## 1. Company

**Brand:** Your AI Department

**Primary domain:** YourAIDepartment.ai

**Marketing domain:** HireAnAIDepartment.com

HireAnAIDepartment.com will function as a marketing and campaign domain that directs prospective customers to the primary brand.

---

## 2. Core Idea

Every company will need an AI capability, but most small and mid-sized businesses do not have the expertise, personnel, or internal systems required to build one.

Your AI Department serves as an outsourced AI strategy, training, implementation, automation, and support function.

The company will not position itself as merely another AI agency. It will help clients build practical AI capabilities throughout their organizations.

---

## 3. Core Positioning

**We build AI departments for growing businesses.**

We help leadership teams understand AI, train employees, identify valuable opportunities, implement useful systems, automate repetitive work, and manage ongoing AI adoption.

The company sells measurable business improvement rather than AI hype.

---

## 4. Target Customers

Initial target customers include:

- Small and mid-sized businesses
- Companies with approximately 10–500 employees
- Owner-operated businesses
- CEOs and executive leadership teams
- Companies that know AI is important but do not know where to begin
- Companies already experimenting with AI but lacking structure, governance, or measurable results

Priority industries may include:

- Automotive and collision repair
- Professional services
- Law firms
- Insurance
- Construction and contracting
- Manufacturing
- Restaurants and hospitality
- Healthcare and dental practices
- Real estate
- Accounting and finance

Industry priorities will be refined using keyword research, customer interviews, competitive analysis, and sales results.

---

## 5. Customer Problems

Prospective clients commonly face the following problems:

- Leadership does not understand which AI tools matter.
- Employees use AI inconsistently or not at all.
- The company lacks an AI strategy.
- Management cannot identify which workflows should be automated.
- Employees waste time on repetitive administrative work.
- AI usage creates security, privacy, and compliance concerns.
- The company has purchased AI tools without achieving measurable adoption.
- Sales leads are not answered or followed up with quickly enough.
- Marketing, CRM, and customer service systems are disconnected.
- The company lacks internal expertise to manage AI implementation.

## 6. Initial Services

### AI Consulting

Strategic guidance for leadership teams regarding AI adoption, tool selection, workflow redesign, governance, implementation, and measurement.

### AI Readiness Assessment

A structured evaluation of a company's leadership, employees, workflows, systems, data, governance, and opportunities.

The assessment is primarily a lead-generation and diagnostic product that leads into consulting, training, implementation, and managed services.

### Executive AI Training

Private coaching, executive workshops, and leadership education focused on practical business use of AI.

### AI Implementation

- Custom assistants

- CRM automation

- Email and SMS workflows
- Customer service tools

- Reporting and analysis systems
- Department-specific workflows

### AI-Powered Marketing

## 8. Sales Funnel

The initial customer journey is expected to be:

1. Prospect discovers the company through search, content, advertising, referral, or outreach.
2. Prospect visits a service or industry landing page.
3. Prospect completes an AI assessment or schedules a strategy call.
4. The company identifies operational problems and AI opportunities.
5. The prospect receives an assessment, scorecard, or preliminary roadmap.
6. The company proposes training, consulting, implementation, marketing, or managed services.
7. The client enters an ongoing Managed AI Department relationship when appropriate.

Your AI Department will distinguish itself by combining:

- Employee education
- Hands-on implementation

Competitors frequently provide only training, only consulting, or only technical implementation.

## 11. Business Principles

1. Sell business outcomes, not AI terminology.
2. Diagnose before prescribing.
3. Do not automate a broken process without first understanding it.
4. Make recommendations understandable to executives and employees.
5. Focus on measurable time savings, revenue growth, cost reduction, and risk management.
6. Document important company decisions.
7. Keep the brand broader than any individual AI tool.
8. Use education as a pathway to consulting and implementation.
9. Build recurring client relationships whenever ongoing value can be delivered.
10. Avoid exaggerated promises and unsupported ROI claims.
