# Your AI Department — Vertical Transformation Strategy

Status: Strategic Direction

## Core Principle

Your AI Department should not limit itself to generic AI consulting.

Long-term, the company should identify expensive, repeatable workflows inside valuable industries and help organizations transform those workflows using the appropriate combination of:

- AI
- automation
- software
- integrations
- messaging
- workflow orchestration
- analytics
- employee training
- governance
- human review

The business problem comes first.

The technology is selected based on what best solves the problem.

## Technology-Agnostic Model

Your AI Department should remain technology-agnostic.

Depending on the engagement, the correct solution may involve:

- configuring an existing specialized platform
- integrating multiple existing systems
- improving an existing workflow
- implementing automation
- building a custom AI-assisted workflow
- creating custom software where justified
- coordinating specialized vendors
- working alongside internal IT, security, legal, operations, or data teams

The company should not imply that custom development is always preferable.

Sometimes the highest-value recommendation will be to purchase, configure, or integrate an existing specialized platform.

## From Services to Repeatable Systems

Transformation services are the initial business model.

Over time, repeated successful workflow patterns may become reusable intellectual property, implementation frameworks, accelerators, or software products.

Conceptual progression:

Consulting
→ Workflow Discovery
→ Implementation
→ Repeated Pattern
→ Standardized Framework
→ Reusable Components
→ Potential Vertical Product

Do not create a software product simply because it is technically possible.

A repeatable product should emerge from a validated business problem.

## Insurance Example

Property and casualty insurance demonstrates the type of vertical opportunity Your AI Department should study.

Specialized insurance technology companies have built platforms around communication, claims workflows, service operations, messaging, routing, document handling, analytics, and AI-assisted workflows.

Hi Marley is one example of a specialized P&C insurance communication and workflow platform.

Hi Marley should be treated as an industry reference/example rather than a company Your AI Department claims to represent, partner with, or compete directly against.

Do not state that a specific insurance carrier uses Hi Marley unless that relationship has been independently verified from a reliable source.

## Human Review and Governance

Insurance is a regulated and high-consequence environment.

AI-assisted workflows should explicitly define:

- human ownership
- escalation thresholds
- approved data usage
- sensitive information handling
- validation requirements
- professional judgment boundaries
- auditability
- vendor review
- security requirements

Do not imply AI replaces adjusters, underwriters, licensed professionals, legal review, or other accountable decision-makers.

## Example Insurance Communication Workflow

Conceptual example:

Policyholder Contact
→ Identity / Context
→ Intent Classification
→ Relevant Claim or Service Workflow
→ Routine / Approved Request?
→ AI-Assisted Response or Workflow
→ Human Escalation Where Required
→ Follow-Up / Document Collection
→ Core System Updated
→ Outcome Tracked

This is a conceptual transformation pattern, not a promise that every insurer should implement the same architecture.

## Long-Term Business Model

Your AI Department may eventually operate across two complementary models:

### 1. Business Transformation Services

Assess
→ Discover
→ Prioritize
→ Design
→ Implement
→ Integrate
→ Train
→ Measure
→ Manage

### 2. Vertical Systems / Reusable IP

When the same business problem appears repeatedly across clients or an industry, the company may standardize:

- workflow frameworks
- reusable integrations
- implementation accelerators
- templates
- governance models
- dashboards
- AI-assisted workflow components
- software products where justified

The second model should emerge from validated demand created through the first.

## Strategic Position

The company should be comfortable saying:

"Sometimes we build it. Sometimes we configure existing technology. Sometimes we integrate specialized vendors. Our job is to solve the business problem."

This technology-agnostic position is core to the Your AI Department business-transformation model.
