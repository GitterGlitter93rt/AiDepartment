# MODULE 37 - FINANCIAL SERVICES INDUSTRY PLAYBOOK

Status: Working Draft - Internal Sales Training
Evidence Status: Workflow complete; segment-specific regulatory/market evidence requires verified sourcing before final PDF.

## Purpose

Teach YAD salespeople how financial advisory, wealth-management, lending, and related financial-services firms handle prospects, appointments, onboarding, documents, client communication, CRM, reporting, and employee AI adoption while licensed/fiduciary/credit/investment decisions remain under qualified human and compliance control.

Core question:

> Where are prospect follow-up, onboarding, document collection, client-service capacity, CRM discipline, reporting, and AI governance consuming unnecessary time or reducing visibility?

---

# 14. OBJECTIONS

## "AI CAN'T GIVE FINANCIAL ADVICE"

> Agreed. We're not trying to move regulated advice or suitability decisions to an uncontrolled AI system. We focus on the business/admin workflow around the professional.

## "COMPLIANCE WON'T ALLOW IT"

> Compliance needs to be involved. The first step may be identifying low-risk workflows and approved tools rather than automating client advice.

# 16. FIELD CHEAT SHEET

Signals: multi-advisor firm, active hiring, CRM, document-heavy onboarding, marketing/referral program.

Top hooks: licensed-staff capacity; onboarding docs; CRM follow-up; reporting; AI governance.

Human rule: advice, investment, credit, fiduciary, regulatory decisions remain qualified/compliant human responsibilities.

# MODULE 37 CORE RULE

Financial services can benefit from AI around information and administration, but trust and regulation are central. YAD should make qualified professionals more capable without pretending software replaces regulated judgment.
