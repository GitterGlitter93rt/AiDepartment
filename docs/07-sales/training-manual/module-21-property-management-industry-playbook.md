# MODULE 21 - PROPERTY MANAGEMENT INDUSTRY PLAYBOOK

Status: Working Draft - Internal Sales Training
Vertical Priority: 12
Evidence Status: Workflow complete; market-size/statistical evidence should be added only after source verification.

## Purpose

Teach YAD salespeople how property-management companies acquire owners and tenants, handle leasing inquiries, route maintenance, communicate with residents and owners, process documents, manage renewals, and report performance.

The rep diagnoses business workflow, not landlord-tenant law.

Core question:

> Where are leasing opportunities, owner leads, maintenance requests, employee time, resident communication, and management visibility leaking across the property-management workflow?

# 18. OBJECTIONS

## "OUR PMS ALREADY DOES ALL OF THAT"

> Good. Then we should use it. What still happens outside the PMS in inboxes, spreadsheets, personal phones, or manual reports?

## "MAINTENANCE NEEDS A HUMAN"

> Emergencies and judgment absolutely need the right human. We can still improve intake, work-order creation, reminders, status updates, and routing around that decision.

## "WE CAN'T AUTOMATE TENANT SCREENING"

> We don't need to. Screening and housing decisions require careful legal/compliance controls. There are many other workflow areas we can improve.

# 20. FIELD CHEAT SHEET

Best signals: active owner-acquisition ads, multiple properties, large portfolio, hiring, online leasing, multiple systems.

Top hooks: owner-lead follow-up; leasing response; maintenance communication; employee capacity; PMS integration.

Best first question:

> What happens to an owner prospect who doesn't connect on the first callback?

ROI tools: Lead Response, Employee Capacity, Reporting/Admin.

Employee-safe: systems handle repetitive communication and routing; property managers handle relationships, exceptions, legal and operational judgment.

---

# 21. CERTIFICATION

Rep must understand owner vs tenant journeys, PMS/CRM distinction, maintenance escalation, fair-housing/legal boundaries, advertiser sourcing, capacity analysis, and phone/walk-in scripts.

# MODULE 21 CORE RULE

Property management is a communication and coordination business wrapped around legal and operational responsibilities. YAD improves the repetitive system around managers while housing, legal, safety, and consequential decisions remain with qualified humans.
