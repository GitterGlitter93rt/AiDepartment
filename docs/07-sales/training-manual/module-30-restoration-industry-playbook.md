# MODULE 30 - RESTORATION INDUSTRY PLAYBOOK

Status: Working Draft - Internal Sales Training
Vertical: Water / Fire / Mold Restoration
Evidence Status: Workflow complete; industry benchmarks require verified sources before final PDF.

## Purpose

Teach YAD salespeople how restoration companies capture urgent calls, respond after hours, schedule emergency work, communicate with property owners, manage insurer/document workflows, coordinate jobs, and measure marketing.

Core question:

> When property damage creates urgent demand, how reliably does the company capture the call, dispatch the right human team, keep the customer informed, process documents, and track the opportunity to revenue?

# 4. MISSED / AFTER-HOURS CALLS

Best hook:

> What happens when a water-loss call hits at 2 AM and the on-call person is already handling another emergency?

Potential support: intelligent routing, immediate acknowledgment, basic intake, CRM/job record, escalation, callback tracking.

Safety/emergency decisions remain human.

# 12. WALK-IN

---

# 13. ROI EXAMPLE

Use Missed Call ROI with actual emergency-call volume, legitimate-opportunity %, close rate, and average job revenue. Never assume every missed call is a lost loss.

---

# 14. FIELD CHEAT SHEET

Signals: 24/7, active Google Ads, multiple crews, property-manager/plumber partnerships, multi-location.

Top hooks: emergency calls; surge capacity; referral attribution; document admin; customer status.

Employee-safe: automation supports dispatch/admin; qualified restoration professionals control field/safety decisions.

---

# 15. CERTIFICATION

Rep must understand urgent restoration lead flow, insurer/admin boundaries, source attribution, surge handling, and safety/human review.

# MODULE 30 CORE RULE

Restoration is an emergency-response business. YAD improves the information and customer workflow around the response without substituting AI for qualified field judgment or insurance/legal decisions.
