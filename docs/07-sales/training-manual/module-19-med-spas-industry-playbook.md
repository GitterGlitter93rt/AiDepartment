# MODULE 19 - MED SPA INDUSTRY PLAYBOOK

Status: Working Draft - Internal Sales Training
Vertical Priority: 10
Evidence Status: Core workflow approved; AmSpa market statistics require final source re-verification before publication.

## Purpose

Teach YAD salespeople how medical spas generate treatment inquiries, respond to paid social/search leads, schedule consultations, manage no-shows, follow up unbooked prospects, reactivate clients, support front-desk capacity, and measure marketing while protecting clinical judgment and patient privacy.

The rep diagnoses business workflow, not medical treatment.

Core question:

> Where are treatment inquiries, consultations, schedule capacity, follow-up, recurring-client value, employee time, and marketing attribution leaking between first interest and completed treatment?

- skin treatments

- memberships

# 19. OBJECTIONS

## "OUR CLIENTS EXPECT WHITE-GLOVE HUMAN SERVICE"

> They should get it. We're looking at the routine work around the coordinator - immediate acknowledgment, reminders, CRM updates, scheduling, reporting - so humans have more time for the relationship.

## "WE CAN'T HAVE AI GIVING TREATMENT ADVICE"

> Agreed. Clinical questions and treatment decisions should route to qualified providers. We can still improve non-clinical intake, scheduling, follow-up, and business reporting.

## "OUR META AGENCY HANDLES LEADS"

> Great. Can you trace those leads through consultation show, treatment, and revenue, and who owns the follow-up once the lead enters the practice?

# 20. ROI EXAMPLE - CONSULTATION NO-SHOW

HYPOTHETICAL:

- 250 consultations/month
- 8% no-show / late-cancel exposure
- $300 selected consultation/slot business-value metric
- 20% realistically recovered through better confirmation/reschedule/follow-up

Exposed = 20
Scenario recovered = 4
Illustrative monthly schedule value = $1,200

Use actual scheduling and revenue data; do not imply treatment or revenue is guaranteed.

---

# 21. FIELD CHEAT SHEET

Best signals: active Meta ads, aggressive offers, multi-location, memberships, online booking, high review volume, active coordinators.

Top hooks: paid-lead speed; no-shows; unbooked consultation follow-up; reactivation; attribution.

Best first question:

> What happens to a paid treatment inquiry that doesn't book during the first conversation?

ROI tools: Lead Response, No-Show, Employee Capacity.

Employee-safe: automate routine admin around coordinators/providers, not the human clinical relationship.

---

# 22. CERTIFICATION

Rep must understand med-spa lead sources, consultation/no-show economics, CRM/DM workflow, reactivation, privacy/clinical boundaries, advertiser sourcing, conservative ROI, and phone/in-person scripts.

# MODULE 19 CORE RULE

Med spas often spend aggressively to create attention. YAD's role is to make the business system after the ad more measurable and consistent while clinical decisions, patient safety, and treatment relationships remain under qualified human control.
