# MODULE 34 - STAFFING / RECRUITING INDUSTRY PLAYBOOK

Status: Working Draft - Internal Sales Training
Evidence Status: Workflow complete; market benchmarks require verified sourcing before final PDF.

## Purpose

Teach YAD salespeople how staffing/recruiting firms acquire employers, source candidates, schedule interviews, manage ATS/CRM workflows, reactivate databases, automate repetitive coordination, and report performance while hiring decisions and anti-discrimination compliance remain human.

Core question:

> Where are employer leads, candidate pipelines, recruiter time, interview scheduling, database reactivation, and reporting leaking between job order and successful placement?

---

# 5. ATS / CRM

Questions:

- Does every candidate enter ATS?
- Is source tracked?
- Can stale candidates be found?
- Can employer pipeline be seen?
- Are calls/emails logged?
- Is interview scheduling integrated?
- What data is entered twice?

# 6. DATABASE REACTIVATION

Potential value in old:

- candidates
- former placements
- clients
- job orders
- silver-medalist candidates

Use skills/availability/location filters and appropriate consent.

Do not call every historical candidate an active placement opportunity.

- scheduling
- reminders
- resume summaries
- ATS notes
- follow-up drafting
- candidate status updates
- job-description formatting
- report assembly

# 8. DOCUMENT / RESUME WORK

Potential support:

- resume parsing
- structured skill extraction
- summarization
- duplicate detection
- document classification

Any automated ranking/screening must be carefully reviewed for employment law, bias, transparency, and jurisdiction-specific requirements.

Safer starting point: organize information for human recruiters rather than autonomously reject candidates.

# 15. OBJECTIONS

## "AI SHOULDN'T HIRE PEOPLE"

> Agreed. The safer value is often around organization, scheduling, summaries, reminders, search support, and recruiter administration while humans make employment decisions.

## "OUR ATS HAS AI"

> Great. Which workflows are actually improving, and what still remains manual or disconnected?

---

# 16. ROI EXAMPLE - RECRUITER CAPACITY

HYPOTHETICAL:

- 10 recruiters
- 3 repetitive admin hours/week
- 48 weeks
- 40% addressable
- $42 loaded hourly cost

Annual repetitive = 1,440 hours
Addressable = 576
Potential capacity value = $24,192/year

Capacity, not payroll savings.

---

# 17. FIELD CHEAT SHEET

Signals: large recruiter team, many job postings, active hiring clients, large database, multiple offices.

Top hooks: recruiter capacity; ATS admin; reactivation; scheduling; reporting.

Best first question:

> What admin keeps recruiters from recruiting?

ROI tools: Employee Capacity, Reactivation, No-Show, Reporting.

Human rule: hiring/rejection/employment decisions remain under qualified human review and legal/compliance controls.

# MODULE 34 CORE RULE

Staffing firms create value through recruiter judgment and relationships. YAD should make the information and coordination system around recruiters more efficient without outsourcing employment decisions to opaque automation.
