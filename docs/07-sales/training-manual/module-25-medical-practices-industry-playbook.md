# MODULE 25 - MEDICAL PRACTICES INDUSTRY PLAYBOOK

Status: Working Draft - Internal Sales Training
Vertical Priority: 16
Evidence Status: Workflow complete; specialty-specific benchmark evidence must be added only after verification.

## Purpose

Teach YAD salespeople how medical practices handle patient inquiries, referrals, scheduling, no-shows, reminders, document intake, repetitive administrative work, patient communication, reporting, and AI adoption while preserving clinical judgment, patient privacy, and healthcare compliance.

Core question:

> Where are patient inquiries, referral workflows, schedule capacity, administrative time, and communication breaking down around the clinical work - and what can improve without moving medical judgment out of qualified human hands?

---

# 17. OBJECTIONS

## "AI CAN'T PRACTICE MEDICINE"

> Agreed. We're not trying to move diagnosis or treatment decisions away from qualified clinicians. We focus on the administrative system around them.

## "HIPAA MAKES AI IMPOSSIBLE"

> HIPAA and privacy requirements constrain tool selection and data handling. They don't mean every workflow is impossible. We evaluate approved vendors, BAAs, access, and what can be done without exposing PHI.

## "OUR EHR ALREADY DOES THIS"

> Good. Then we'd use what it already does. What remains manual outside the EHR?

# 18. ROI EXAMPLE - REFERRAL ADMIN CAPACITY

HYPOTHETICAL:

- 5 administrative employees
- 3 hours/week each on repetitive referral/document chasing
- 48 weeks
- 40% addressable
- $34 loaded hourly cost

Annual repetitive hours = 720
Addressable = 288
Potential annual capacity value = $9,792

This is potential capacity, not payroll savings.

# 19. FIELD CHEAT SHEET

Best signals: multi-provider practice, high call/referral volume, visible hiring, multiple locations, online scheduling, specialty referral dependence.

Top hooks: scheduling calls; no-shows; referral aging; document processing; staff capacity.

Best first question:

> Which administrative referral/scheduling task consumes the most employee time today?

ROI tools: No-Show, Document Processing, Employee Capacity, Reporting/Admin.

Employee-safe: systems reduce repetitive admin around clinicians/staff; clinical judgment stays human.

# 20. CERTIFICATION

# MODULE 25 CORE RULE

Medical practices are not a place for casual AI promises. YAD improves administrative throughput, scheduling, referral handling, reporting, and employee capacity while qualified clinicians retain full responsibility for medical judgment and patient care.
