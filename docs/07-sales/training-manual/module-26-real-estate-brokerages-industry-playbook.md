# MODULE 26 - REAL ESTATE BROKERAGES INDUSTRY PLAYBOOK

Status: Working Draft - Internal Sales Training
Vertical Priority: 17
Evidence Status: Workflow complete; external market statistics should be source-verified before final PDF.

## Purpose

Teach YAD salespeople how real-estate brokerages and teams generate buyer/seller leads, route inquiries, nurture long-cycle prospects, manage agent follow-up, support transaction/admin workflows, reactivate databases, and measure marketing while preserving licensed-agent, fair-housing, contract, and fiduciary responsibilities.

Core question:

> Where are buyer/seller inquiries, long-term nurture, agent capacity, CRM discipline, and marketing attribution leaking between first contact and a closed transaction?

# 5. LONG-TERM NURTURE

Real-estate leads often are not ready today.

Potential statuses:

- 0-30 days
- 30-90 days
- 3-6 months
- 6-12+ months
- waiting on financing / move / lease / life event

CRM should preserve future timing and next actions.

Ask:

> What happens to a good lead that says they're six months away?

# 19. OBJECTIONS

## "REAL ESTATE IS RELATIONSHIP-BASED"

> Absolutely. The goal is to give agents more time for the relationship while the system handles reminders, tracking, summaries, and routine nurture support.

## "OUR CRM ALREADY NURTURES"

> Great. Is it actually used consistently, and can you tie the nurture source through to appointments and closings?

## "AI CAN'T NEGOTIATE FOR OUR CLIENTS"

> We agree. Negotiation, representation, contract and licensed decisions stay with the agent/broker.

# 20. ROI EXAMPLE - LONG-TERM LEAD FOLLOW-UP

HYPOTHETICAL:

- 800 leads/month
- 20% are longer-term legitimate prospects
- 15% of that segment loses desired follow-up
- 4% incremental closing scenario over appropriate long horizon
- $4,000 selected brokerage-side revenue metric per closing

Long-term = 160
Weak-follow-up exposure = 24
Scenario closings = 0.96 over relevant cohort/horizon
Illustrative value = $3,840

Do not present as monthly guaranteed revenue; real-estate cycles are long and cohort-based.

# 21. FIELD CHEAT SHEET

Best signals: active Meta/portal ads, large team, ISA/inside sales, multiple offices, CRM, open houses, active hiring.

Top hooks: speed-to-lead; long-term nurture; seller follow-up; agent capacity; source-to-close attribution.

Best first question:

> What happens to a good lead that isn't ready for six months?

ROI tools: Lead Response, Reactivation, Employee Capacity.

Employee-safe: systems remember/nurture; agents build relationships, advise, negotiate and close.

# MODULE 26 CORE RULE

Real estate is won through relationships, timing, and follow-up. YAD should help the brokerage remember and measure thousands of relationships more consistently while licensed agents retain the human work of representation, advice, negotiation, and trust.
