# MODULE 31 - PEST CONTROL INDUSTRY PLAYBOOK

Status: Working Draft - Internal Sales Training
Evidence Status: Workflow complete; industry benchmarks require source verification before final PDF.

## Purpose

Teach YAD salespeople how pest-control companies generate urgent and recurring leads, schedule technicians, sell service plans, follow up estimates, retain/reactivate customers, and manage route/customer communication while licensed/safety decisions remain human.

Core question:

> Where are inbound pest leads, recurring-plan opportunities, route capacity, customer communication, and marketing attribution leaking between first inquiry and retained customer?

Employee-safe: systems support CSRs/technicians; licensed treatment decisions stay human.

# MODULE 31 CORE RULE

Pest control combines urgent leads with recurring relationships. YAD helps the company capture and retain those relationships while qualified professionals remain responsible for treatment and safety.
