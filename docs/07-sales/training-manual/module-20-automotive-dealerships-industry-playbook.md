# MODULE 20 - AUTOMOTIVE DEALERSHIPS INDUSTRY PLAYBOOK

Status: Working Draft - Internal Sales Training
Vertical Priority: 11
Evidence Status: Core workflow approved; NADA 2025 industry figures require final publication re-verification.

## Purpose

Teach YAD salespeople how dealerships generate vehicle and service leads, use BDC/sales teams, schedule appointments, follow up unsold opportunities, reactivate databases, manage service-lane communication, and measure marketing while preserving human responsibility for sales, credit, F&I, compliance, and repair decisions.

Core question:

> Where are vehicle leads, service opportunities, BDC capacity, follow-up, database value, and marketing attribution leaking between first contact and sold vehicle or completed repair order?

---

# 20. OBJECTIONS

## "WE ALREADY HAVE A CRM AND DMS"

> You should. The question is whether lead response, follow-up, service workflows, attribution, and reporting are actually connected across them.

## "OEM REQUIRES OUR SYSTEMS"

> Then replacement may be off the table. We'd evaluate what can be configured or integrated around the required stack.

## "WE HAVE A BDC"

> Good. We're not trying to replace it. We're looking at how much repetitive admin can become consistent so the BDC spends more time actually connecting with shoppers.

# 21. ROI EXAMPLE - INTERNET LEAD FOLLOW-UP

HYPOTHETICAL:

- 2,000 leads/month
- 10% become stale without desired contact cadence
- 30% of those remain legitimate opportunities
- 5% incremental sale scenario
- $2,500 selected dealer gross/revenue contribution metric

Stale = 200
Addressable = 60
Scenario additional sales = 3
Illustrative value = $7,500/month

Use dealership-selected economic metric; do not imply vehicle gross or revenue figures.

# 22. FIELD CHEAT SHEET

Best signals: high ad volume, large BDC, multiple rooftops, aggressive offers, active service marketing, many vendors.

Top hooks: internet response; stale leads; appointment show; BDC capacity; source-to-sale / source-to-RO attribution.

Best first question:

> What happens after an internet lead doesn't answer the first call?

ROI tools: Lead Response, No-Show, Employee Capacity, Reactivation.

Employee-safe: give BDC/advisors better systems; sales/F&I/service judgment stays human.

# 23. CERTIFICATION

Rep must explain vehicle-sales and service-lane journeys, diagnose CRM/BDC/service issues, understand DMS integration caution, use advertiser sourcing, quantify conservatively, and preserve F&I/credit/service decision boundaries.

# MODULE 20 CORE RULE

Dealerships rarely need another random tool. They need the large existing vendor stack to produce a coherent customer journey and measurable business outcome. YAD should improve the system around the BDC, sales team, service advisors, and management rather than pretending technology replaces them.
