# MODULE 32 - VETERINARY INDUSTRY PLAYBOOK

Status: Working Draft - Internal Sales Training
Evidence Status: Workflow complete; veterinary market benchmarks require verified sourcing before final PDF.

## Purpose

Teach YAD salespeople how veterinary practices handle new-client inquiries, urgent calls, scheduling, reminders, no-shows, recall/recheck communication, repetitive front-desk work, client updates, and reporting while veterinarians and qualified staff retain clinical responsibility.

Core question:

> Where are pet-owner inquiries, appointment capacity, reminders, client communication, and staff time leaking around the veterinary care workflow?

---

- high phone volume
- emotional/urgent callers
- scheduling
- no-shows
- preventive reminders
- repeat clients
- front-desk workload
- prescription/refill request routing
- medical records
- reviews
- practice-management systems
- client communication

---

# 2. BUSINESS MODEL

Potential services:

- wellness
- vaccines/preventive
- diagnostics
- surgery
- dentistry
- urgent care
- specialty care
- pharmacy
- boarding/grooming where offered

Do not assume appointment or patient lifetime value.

---

# 4. PHONE / URGENT INQUIRIES

Potential support:

- call routing
- basic intake
- scheduling
- immediate acknowledgment
- human escalation

AI must not independently diagnose urgency or tell owners how to treat an animal unless content/workflow is explicitly approved by qualified veterinary leadership and safely escalated.

---

# 5. NO-SHOW / SCHEDULING

Questions:

> What is the no-show / late-cancel rate?

> What happens to an empty appointment slot?

> How easy is rescheduling?

Use No-Show ROI with practice data.

---

# 6. PREVENTIVE / RECHECK REMINDERS

Potential workflows:

- recheck reminders
- preventive care reminders
- appointment reminders
- overdue client reactivation where medically appropriate and practice-approved

Clinical timing must follow veterinarian/practice rules.

---

# 7. FRONT-DESK CAPACITY

Repetitive tasks:

- scheduling
- records requests
- refill-request routing
- estimate/payment questions
- appointment reminders
- basic FAQs
- client updates
- call notes

Ask:

> What does your front desk answer repeatedly that doesn't require medical judgment?

---

# 8. PRACTICE MANAGEMENT / CRM

Questions:

- Online booking?
- Calls logged?
- Reminders automatic?
- New-client leads tracked?
- Inactive clients segmented?
- Referral source tracked?
- What remains manual?

Do not promise integration before reviewing the veterinary PMS.

---

# 9. CLIENT COMMUNICATION

Potential approved communication:

- appointment confirmation
- pickup readiness
- administrative status
- records/document reminders
- review requests

Medical results, diagnosis, treatment changes, and emergencies require veterinary human involvement.

# 14. WALK-IN

> Hi, I'm [Rep] with Your AI Department. We work with practices on scheduling, reminders, front-desk capacity, client communication, reporting, and responsible AI. Who oversees practice operations?

Respect treatment/privacy areas.

---

# 15. OBJECTIONS

## "PET OWNERS NEED A PERSON"

> Absolutely, especially for medical concerns. We focus on routine scheduling, reminders, records/admin routing, and reporting around the clinical team.

## "AI CAN'T TRIAGE OUR CASES"

> Then it shouldn't. We can improve non-clinical workflows and route medical questions to qualified staff.

---

# 16. ROI EXAMPLE

Use No-Show or Employee Capacity ROI using the practice's appointment categories and loaded labor. Do not value clinical outcomes or assume all missed slots can be recovered.

---

# 17. FIELD CHEAT SHEET

Signals: busy multi-doctor practice, urgent care, ads, online booking, multiple locations, hiring.

Top hooks: missed calls; no-shows; front-desk capacity; reminders; PMS workflow.

Employee-safe: systems handle repetitive admin; veterinarians/techs handle medicine and client judgment.

# MODULE 32 CORE RULE

Veterinary care is human clinical work. YAD improves the administrative and communication system around that care so staff can spend more time with animals and owners.
