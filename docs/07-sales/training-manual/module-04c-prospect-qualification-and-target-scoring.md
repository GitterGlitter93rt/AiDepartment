# MODULE 4C - PROSPECT QUALIFICATION AND TARGET SCORING

Status: Working Draft - Internal Sales Training

## Purpose

Not every business should receive the same amount of prospecting effort.

Your AI Department salespeople should use visible business signals to prioritize companies where a meaningful economic opportunity is more likely to exist.

One of the strongest public signals is active paid advertising.

A company that is actively paying to generate demand has already demonstrated willingness to invest in customer acquisition. That does not prove the company is profitable, does not reveal exact advertising spend, and does not prove that its campaigns are good. It does, however, make questions about lead response, missed calls, CRM, follow-up, attribution, conversion, and customer acquisition economics more relevant.

The objective is to create a better prospect list before the rep ever calls or walks through the door.

---

# 1. ACTIVE PAID ADVERTISING IS A HIGH-VALUE PROSPECTING SIGNAL

When a company is actively running Google or Meta advertising, the rep should think:

AD SPEND
->
LEADS / CALLS / INQUIRIES
->
RESPONSE
->
QUALIFICATION
->
FOLLOW-UP
->
SALE
->
REVENUE

The advertiser may have opportunities at any point in that chain.

This fits the YAD Growth Systems philosophy because advertising performance should not be evaluated only inside the ad platform. The full acquisition system may include landing pages, call tracking, CRM, speed to lead, qualification, follow-up, sales conversion, offline conversions, revenue attribution, and reporting.

The rep is not assuming the advertiser has a problem.

The ad activity simply provides a credible reason to investigate.

---

# 2. WHAT ACTIVE ADS CAN TELL US

Active advertising may indicate:

- the business wants more customers
- ownership is willing to invest in growth
- lead handling has measurable economic importance
- missed calls may be more expensive because acquisition money was spent to create the opportunity
- attribution matters
- landing-page conversion may matter
- follow-up may matter
- CRM discipline may matter
- customer acquisition cost may matter
- the company may be more receptive to measurable ROI discussions

Active ads do NOT prove:

- exact monthly ad spend
- profitability
- return on ad spend
- lead quality
- conversion rate
- agency quality
- whether the company is satisfied with marketing
- that YAD can definitely improve performance

Never invent those facts.

---

# 3. FAST CANDIDATE-FINDING WORKFLOW

This is one of the simplest ways a rep can build a stronger prospect list.

## METHOD A - GOOGLE INDUSTRY + LOCATION SEARCH

Search Google using the service / industry plus a target city or market.

Examples:

> HVAC Jacksonville FL

> emergency plumber St Augustine FL

> roofing company Orlando FL

> personal injury lawyer Tampa FL

> collision repair Jacksonville FL

> cosmetic dentist Ponte Vedra FL

> med spa Orlando FL

Look for businesses appearing in clearly labeled Sponsored / Ad placements.

Those companies become potential high-priority prospects because they are actively competing for customer acquisition in that market at that moment.

Record:

- company
- website
- phone
- city / service area
- advertised service
- offer / CTA
- landing page used
- whether the ad emphasizes phone call, form, quote, consultation, appointment, financing, emergency service, etc.

Then ask:

> What happens after this paid prospect clicks or calls?

That question creates the YAD prospecting hypothesis.

### IMPORTANT

One Google search is only a signal.

Search ads can vary by:

- location
- time
- keyword
- auction conditions
- campaign settings
- device and other factors

Do not conclude that a company never advertises simply because it did not appear in one search.

Do not estimate the company's spend from its ad position.

---

## METHOD B - META / FACEBOOK AD LIBRARY

Use Meta's public Ad Library to find currently running advertising across Meta technologies.

A rep can search by:

- business / page name
- advertiser
- brand
- relevant keyword when useful

For a known prospect, search its business/page name and inspect its active ads.

Look for:

- what service is being pushed
- current offers
- lead-generation ads
- consultations
- quote requests
- financing offers where applicable
- geographic campaigns
- seasonal offers
- repeated creative themes
- whether the CTA drives to a form, website, call, message, or booking flow

Do not infer exact advertising spend from the number of ads or creatives.

### EXAMPLE

A rep finds an HVAC company's Facebook Page in Meta Ad Library and sees active ads promoting AC replacement financing.

Potential hook:

> I saw you guys are actively promoting replacement financing right now. When somebody responds to one of those ads but does not book on the first contact, what happens next?

That is researched relevance.

It is not a claim that the company has bad follow-up.

---

## METHOD C - GOOGLE ADS TRANSPARENCY CENTER

Google's Ads Transparency Center can also be used to research advertisers by advertiser name or website / domain where available.

This can help confirm that a business is publishing ads through Google even if it does not appear during one manual search.

Use this as another research signal, not as a substitute for discovery.

# 4. CROSS-PLATFORM PRIORITY SIGNAL

A business advertising on more than one platform may deserve even more attention.

Example:

Active Google Search ads
+
Active Meta ads
+
High-value service
+
Prominent phone / form CTA
+
Fast-response industry
=
strong YAD prospecting candidate.

Why?

The business is investing across multiple acquisition channels, so downstream problems involving:

- response speed
- missed calls
- CRM
- follow-up
- attribution
- appointment setting
- sales conversion
- reporting

may have meaningful economic leverage.

Again:

This is a reason to investigate.

It is not proof that anything is broken.

---

# 5. THE PAID-AD PROSPECTING HYPOTHESIS

Example:

HVAC company
+
active Google advertising
+
24/7 emergency service
+
phone-heavy customer journey
=
strong reason to investigate after-hours handling, missed calls, speed to lead, call tracking, CRM attribution, and booked-job economics.

Possible opener:

> I noticed you guys are actively advertising around emergency HVAC. When one of those paid calls comes in after hours and the office cannot grab it immediately, what happens next?

The statement about advertising must be based on something the rep actually observed.

Do not exaggerate by saying:

> You guys are spending a fortune on Google.

unless the prospect has actually provided spend data.

---

# 6. WHY PAID LEADS CAN CREATE A DOUBLE-LOSS PROBLEM

When a business pays to create an opportunity and then fails to handle it effectively, two forms of economic exposure may exist:

1. The acquisition investment used to create the opportunity.
2. The potential customer value attached to the opportunity.

This makes paid advertisers particularly useful prospects for discussions involving:

- missed calls
- after-hours response
- speed to lead
- CRM routing
- follow-up
- appointment setting
- attribution
- call tracking
- landing pages
- conversion tracking
- customer acquisition cost

Do not call the advertising spend "wasted" until the account and downstream sales process have actually been audited.

Use:

> advertising spend worth auditing

or:

> customer-acquisition investment touching a possible lead-handling problem

# 7. YAD PROSPECT PRIORITY SCORE - STARTING MODEL

This is an internal prioritization tool, not a public ROI score.

Add points for visible signals.

## PAID ACQUISITION SIGNALS

+4 - Active Google paid-search / high-intent sponsored advertising signal

+3 - Active Meta / Facebook / Instagram advertising signal

+1 - Active on more than one paid channel

## ECONOMIC VALUE SIGNALS

+2 - High-value customer, job, case, treatment, contract, or recurring account economics

+2 - Lead / intake / estimate volume appears operationally important

## URGENCY / WORKFLOW SIGNALS

+1 - Emergency / after-hours / 24-7 service model

+1 - Appointment, estimate, consultation, or intake-heavy buying process

+1 - Multiple locations or service territories

+1 - Visible growth / hiring / expansion signal

+1 - Strong phone dependence

+1 - Website uses prominent lead forms, booking, quote requests, or consultation CTAs

## INITIAL PRIORITY BANDS

### TIER A - 9+ POINTS

High-priority prospect.

Research first, then call and/or include in a field route.

### TIER B - 6-8 POINTS

Good prospect.

Include in normal outbound cadence and nearby field routes.

### TIER C - 3-5 POINTS

Possible fit, but less evidence of immediate economic leverage.

### TIER D - 0-2 POINTS

Low public evidence of fit. Do not automatically reject; deprioritize unless another strong reason exists.

This scoring system should be improved over time using actual YAD conversion data.

# 8. ADVERTISING SHOULD NOT BE THE ONLY QUALIFICATION SIGNAL

Do not make the mistake of targeting only companies running ads.

A business may generate substantial volume through:

- referrals
- organic search
- Google Business Profile
- partnerships
- repeat customers
- insurance relationships
- dealer relationships
- professional referrals
- sales teams
- outbound prospecting

Those businesses may still have major opportunities involving:

- employee capacity
- workflow automation
- reporting
- integration
- customer communication
- CRM
- AI training
- internal knowledge systems

Paid advertising is a strong Growth Systems signal.

It is not the entire YAD market.

# 10. HOW PHONE REPS USE THE SCORE

Before dialing, the rep should know:

- Is the company visibly advertising?
- On what channel?
- What service / offer is being promoted?
- What customer action does the ad request?
- What happens after that action?
- What is the most plausible leak to ask about?

Example note:

> Tier A. HVAC. Active Google ads for emergency AC + replacement. 24/7 service. Two locations. Hook: after-hours paid calls / speed to lead. Backup: replacement-estimate follow-up.

That is much better than:

> HVAC company - call and pitch AI.

---

# 11. HOW FIELD REPS USE THE SCORE

Field routes should favor clusters of higher-scoring prospects.

Example route:

- 2 scheduled meetings
- 4 Tier A walk-ins
- 5 Tier B walk-ins
- 3 existing follow-up stops

all within the same geographic area.

If a rep sees that a company is actively promoting a specific offer, they can use that information carefully in the opening.

Example:

> I saw you guys are advertising roof inspections right now. I had one question about what happens to the homeowner after the inspection if they do not sign immediately.

Do not walk into the business claiming you know its advertising economics.

# 12. BUILDING A DAILY ADVERTISER PROSPECT LIST

A rep or sales coordinator can build a target list by market.

Example assignment:

Market:
Jacksonville, Florida

Vertical:
HVAC

Step 1:
Search several high-intent Google terms such as:

- AC repair Jacksonville FL
- emergency HVAC Jacksonville FL
- AC replacement Jacksonville FL

Step 2:
Record businesses appearing in Sponsored placements.

Step 3:
Visit their websites and identify:

- service promoted
- CTA
- phone / form / booking path
- 24/7 claim
- number of locations
- financing / offer

Step 4:
Search those businesses in Meta Ad Library.

Step 5:
Record whether active Meta ads exist and what they promote.

Step 6:
Score the prospect.

Step 7:
Assign:

- primary hook
- backup hook
- phone priority
- field-route priority

Step 8:
Load the research into the CRM.

This turns public ad activity into a repeatable B2B prospecting system.

---

# 13. DO NOT CREATE FAKE LEADS TO TEST THEM

Do not submit fake quote forms, fake appointments, fake legal inquiries, fake patient inquiries, or other deceptive customer requests simply to test response time.

That wastes the prospect's employees' time and can damage the YAD brand.

Use public signals and honest sales outreach.

If a prospect later authorizes YAD to perform a structured mystery-shop or response-time test as part of an engagement or audit, follow the approved scope and compliance requirements.

# 15. THE BEST-PROSPECT CONCEPT

A particularly strong YAD prospect often has several characteristics at once:

They already invest in growth.

+

Their customers / jobs are financially meaningful.

+

Their buying journey depends on fast response or consistent follow-up.

+

Their team handles meaningful administrative volume.

+

Their systems are not fully connected or measured.

=

A potentially strong YAD business case.

Paid advertising helps us find more businesses that may fit this profile.

It does not replace discovery.

# MODULE 4C PASS STANDARD

A rep passes when they can:

- use Google industry + location searches to identify visible Sponsored advertisers
- use Meta Ad Library to research active ads by business / page / advertiser
- use Google Ads Transparency Center as an additional advertiser research source
- identify strong public qualification signals
- verify visible ad activity without inventing spend
- explain why advertisers may have greater downstream ROI leverage
- choose a relevant hook from the advertised service
- distinguish public observation from internal business fact
- assign a preliminary prospect priority score
- use that score for phone and field prioritization
- avoid fake mystery-shopping or deceptive lead submissions
- recognize strong non-advertiser prospects as well
- update the CRM with useful prospecting intelligence

The objective is not simply more dials or more doors.

The objective is more conversations with businesses where solving a workflow problem could have meaningful economic value.
