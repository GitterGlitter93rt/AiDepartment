# MODULE 41 - FINAL SALES CERTIFICATION

Status: Working Draft - Internal Sales Training

## Purpose

Your AI Department certification exists to answer one question:

> Can this salesperson represent YAD in front of a real business owner without damaging the prospect, the client, the company, or the delivery team?

Certification is competency-based.

Reading the manual is not certification.

Tenure is not certification.

Activity volume is not certification.

A certified rep must demonstrate:

- YAD doctrine
- business economics
- responsible ROI
- discovery
- CRM knowledge
- phone prospecting
- in-person prospecting
- target qualification
- hook selection
- story / visualization selling
- objection handling
- competitive positioning
- proposal / closing discipline
- vertical understanding
- claim / risk discipline
- clean CRM and delivery handoff behavior

---

# 1. CERTIFICATION LEVELS

## LEVEL 1 - YAD SALES FOUNDATIONS CERTIFIED

Rep may conduct supervised prospecting after demonstrating:

- YAD explanation
- doctrine
- employee-safe language
- basic CRM understanding
- basic business economics
- compliant cold-call and walk-in behavior

## LEVEL 2 - YAD PHONE & FIELD SALES CERTIFIED

Rep may independently cold call and conduct in-person prospecting after passing:

- phone roleplay
- gatekeeper
- walk-in roleplay
- hook selection
- objections
- CRM logging

## LEVEL 3 - YAD DIAGNOSTIC SELLER CERTIFIED

Rep may independently conduct discovery and build business-case scenarios after passing:

- workflow mapping
- financial diagnosis
- ROI calculators
- capability mapping
- technical limitation handling
- stakeholder / next-step control

## LEVEL 4 - YAD VERTICAL SPECIALIST

Rep earns separate endorsements for selected industries after demonstrating:

- industry economics
- customer journey
- top leaks
- vertical hooks
- vertical objections
- field behavior
- relevant ROI tools
- industry risk boundaries

## LEVEL 5 - YAD FULL-CYCLE SALES CERTIFIED

Rep may own opportunities through approved proposal / close after demonstrating all prior levels plus:

- proposal quality
- commercial discipline
- executive objections
- competitive positioning
- delivery handoff

Final authority for pricing/contract signatures remains subject to company policy and role permissions.

---

# 2. RECOMMENDED FINAL SCORE

Working recommendation:

- 85% overall or better
- no critical section below 75%
- zero hard-fail events
- manager signoff

YAD may adjust the numeric standard after the certification program is piloted.

Hard-fail rules matter more than a high average score.

---

# 3. WRITTEN / KNOWLEDGE EXAM - 20%

Rep should demonstrate understanding of:

- what YAD sells
- YAD Method
- five business leaks
- theoretical vs recoverable value
- loaded labor / capacity
- CRM
- capability labels
- build vs buy
- human-in-loop
- pricing authority
- case-study rules
- statistics/evidence rules
- proposal quality
- delivery handoff

Question types:

- multiple choice
- scenario judgment
- short answer
- identify-the-bad-claim

Do not make the exam a trivia contest.

---

# 4. PHONE PROSPECTING PRACTICAL - 15%

Rep receives three unseen company scenarios.

Must demonstrate:

- research hypothesis
- opener
- hook
- gatekeeper handling
- owner conversation
- discovery transition
- next step
- CRM note

At least one scenario should involve an active Google/Meta advertiser.

---

# 5. IN-PERSON FIELD PRACTICAL - 15%

Rep must demonstrate:

- professional entry
- no fake appointment
- front-desk respect
- body language
- 30-second owner conversation
- one-question discipline
- leave-behind use
- exit
- follow-up

Hard fail for:

- entering restricted space
- reading/photographing private information
- refusing to leave
- lying to gatekeeper

---

# 6. DISCOVERY / WORKFLOW PRACTICAL - 15%

Rep receives a simulated business with incomplete information.

Must discover:

- business model
- lead/customer journey
- workflow
- failure point
- frequency
- cause
- current workaround
- systems
- human-review requirements
- desired state
- urgency

The examiner should intentionally give irrelevant information to test whether the rep can stay focused.

---

# 7. ROI / FINANCIAL DIAGNOSIS PRACTICAL - 15%

Rep receives a dataset containing:

- verified values
- client estimates
- missing values
- one misleading high assumption

Rep must:

- source-label inputs
- choose correct calculator
- distinguish exposure vs realistic recovery
- create conservative scenario
- mark missing data
- explain ROI/payback if investment is provided

Rep fails if they manipulate assumptions to make the project look attractive.

---

# 8. OBJECTIONS / COMPETITIVE PRACTICAL - 10%

Rep must handle randomized objections such as:

- AI isn't ready
- we have ChatGPT
- we have a receptionist
- we have a CRM
- we have IT
- we have an agency
- customers want humans
- employees can do it
- too expensive
- send me info
- think about it
- guarantee ROI
- will it integrate?
- why not SaaS?
- we can build internally

Scoring:

CLARIFY
->
RESPOND
->
QUESTION

Not rebuttal memorization.

---

# 9. PROPOSAL / CLOSING PRACTICAL - 10%

Rep must present:

- problem
- current state
- desired state
- business case
- assumptions
- scope
- exclusions
- investment
- measurement
- next decision

Examiner introduces:

- price challenge
- scope creep
- new integration request
- missing stakeholder

Rep must not invent concessions or promises.

---

# 10. HARD-FAIL EVENTS

A rep cannot pass certification if they:

1. Invent a statistic.
2. Invent a case study/client/result.
3. Guarantee revenue or ROI.
4. Present theoretical opportunity as guaranteed recovery.
5. Promise an unverified software integration.
6. Promise a security/compliance outcome without review.
7. Position YAD as a way to fire/replace staff.
8. Give unauthorized legal advice.
9. Give medical/clinical advice.
10. Give investment/financial/insurance advice outside proper licensed roles.
11. Recommend unsafe autonomous decisions.
12. Lie to a gatekeeper or prospect.
13. Invent pricing, discount, financing, payment terms, or package.
14. Ignore a do-not-contact / leave request.
15. Misrepresent a hypothetical scenario as a real customer result.
16. Recommend AI simply because YAD can build it when a simpler solution is clearly better.

These rules protect YAD's reputation and clients.

---

# 11. VERTICAL SPECIALIST EXAM

For each vertical endorsement, rep receives a random company and must answer:

1. How does this business make money?
2. What are five likely workflow leaks?
3. What public signals make it a good prospect?
4. What are two hooks?
5. What is the best first discovery question?
6. Which ROI worksheet may apply?
7. What existing software category is likely?
8. What should remain human?
9. What is a vertical-specific objection?
10. When should YAD not sell?

Rep should be certified only for verticals they actually understand.

- Collision Repair

Plus at least two additional YAD verticals.

# 13. CRM CERTIFICATION

Rep must demonstrate:

- account creation / update
- exact notes
- stage selection
- next task/date
- source / hook where required
- decision-maker
- business problem
- financial inputs / source labels
- objection
- closed-lost reason
- handoff documentation

A deal that only exists in the rep's memory is not an acceptable pipeline.

---

# 14. FIELD KIT CERTIFICATION

Rep must know when to use:

- business card
- one-page YAD overview
- industry field card
- Core ROI Field Kit
- tablet / demo
- current approved QR / next-step link

They should not carry or dump the full master manual on a cold prospect.

---

# 15. DEMO DISCIPLINE

If a prospect requests a demo, the rep must distinguish:

- approved live demo
- illustrative demo
- concept visualization
- capability requiring technical validation

Never imply a staged/demo workflow is already deployed for a real customer if it is not.

---

# 16. EVIDENCE CERTIFICATION

Rep must take one statistic and explain:

- source
- sample/population
- date
- limitation
- approved wording
- forbidden wording

Example:

ServiceTitan residential-services survey:

Approved:

> In a 2025 survey of 1,000+ residential service contractors, 64% said phone calls were the dominant communication channel.

Forbidden:

> 64% of your HVAC customers only call.

---

# 17. CASE-STUDY CERTIFICATION

Rep must understand:

REAL CASE STUDY - VERIFIED

versus

HYPOTHETICAL BUSINESS SCENARIO.

They must never blur them.

---

# 18. EMPLOYEE-SAFE CERTIFICATION

Examiner says:

> So how many people can I get rid of?

Rep must redirect to:

- workload
- capacity
- repetitive tasks
- human judgment
- overtime
- growth
- hiring deferral where defensible

The rep should never celebrate headcount elimination as the primary outcome.

---

# 19. PROFESSIONAL / SENSITIVE VERTICAL TEST

Rep receives one of:

- law
- medical
- dental
- veterinary
- insurance
- financial services
- accounting
- assisted living

Must identify:

- sensitive data
- professional judgment
- human review
- technical/security review
- things YAD should not automate blindly

This is required even if the rep's main territory is home services.

---

# 20. FINAL PANEL

Recommended final certification panel:

- sales manager
- senior sales / founder representative
- delivery / technical representative

Why delivery is included:

A rep can sound persuasive while making technically irresponsible promises.

Delivery should have veto authority on technical/promise-discipline failures.

Track:

- certification level

# 22. RECERTIFICATION

Recommended triggers:

- major offer change
- pricing model change
- new vertical
- new regulated workflow
- material product/capability change
- repeated claim/promise errors
- long inactivity

Periodic recertification should also confirm evidence and scripts remain current.

Suggested working cadence:

Annual full review + targeted quarterly roleplays.

Final cadence may be changed by management.

---

# 23. CERTIFICATION IS NOT PERMISSION TO IMPROVISE COMMERCIAL TRUTH

Even a senior certified rep may not invent:

- new offers
- pricing
- integration guarantees
- results
- case studies
- partnerships
- credentials
- compliance claims

Certification increases responsibility.

It does not remove governance.

---

# 24. GRADUATION SCENARIO

Final scenario should combine:

- active advertiser prospect
- gatekeeper
- decision-maker
- weak initial hypothesis
- hidden real pain
- employee concern
- CRM issue
- missing ROI input
- competitor/IT objection
- technical integration question
- pricing objection

Rep must adapt and reach the correct next step.

The ideal final scenario cannot be passed by memorizing one script.

---

# 25. FINAL YAD SALES STANDARD

A fully certified YAD salesperson should be able to walk into or call an unfamiliar business and:

1. Understand enough of the business model to ask intelligent questions.
2. Form a hypothesis without pretending it is fact.
3. Find the real workflow problem.
4. Quantify it conservatively.
5. Explain the problem visually and financially.
6. Protect employees and human judgment.
7. Recommend the simplest reliable solution.
8. Handle objections without fighting.
9. Explain why YAD is or is not the right fit.
10. Advance to the correct commercial next step.
11. Document everything cleanly.
12. Hand delivery a deal that matches what the client was actually sold.

# MODULE 41 CORE RULE

YAD certification is not a test of whether a salesperson can sell AI.

It is a test of whether they can diagnose a business responsibly, communicate value, protect trust, and sell only what Your AI Department can credibly deliver.
