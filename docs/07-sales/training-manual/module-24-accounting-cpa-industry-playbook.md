# MODULE 24 - ACCOUNTING / CPA INDUSTRY PLAYBOOK

Status: Working Draft - Internal Sales Training
Vertical Priority: 15
Evidence Status: Workflow complete; external industry benchmarks should be added only after source verification.

## Purpose

Teach YAD salespeople how accounting and CPA firms acquire clients, onboard them, collect documents, perform recurring administrative work, communicate deadlines, assemble reports, manage internal knowledge, and adopt AI while preserving professional accounting, tax, audit, and financial judgment.

Core question:

> Where are client leads, document collection, repetitive administration, reporting time, employee capacity, and knowledge access leaking across the accounting firm's workflow?

# 8. SEASONAL CAPACITY

Tax and accounting firms may face sharp seasonal workload.

Ask:

> During your busiest eight weeks, what repetitive work consumes staff capacity before professionals even begin the actual accounting/tax work?

- increase professional capacity
- avoid some temporary admin pressure

Do not promise reduced professional staffing.

# 18. OBJECTIONS

## "ACCOUNTING REQUIRES PROFESSIONAL JUDGMENT"

> Agreed. We're looking at the repetitive workflow around that judgment - document intake, reminders, routing, summaries, reporting, and internal knowledge.

## "WE CAN'T PUT FINANCIAL DATA INTO CHATGPT"

> Sensitive data should not go into an unapproved tool. Any AI workflow needs a security/data review and appropriate vendor controls.

## "WE ALREADY HAVE PRACTICE-MANAGEMENT SOFTWARE"

> Good. What still lives outside it in inboxes, spreadsheets, or manual reports?

# 19. ROI EXAMPLE - DOCUMENT CHASING CAPACITY

HYPOTHETICAL:

- 8 employees
- 2 hours/week each on repetitive missing-document/admin follow-up
- 48 weeks
- 45% addressable
- $45 loaded hourly cost

Annual repetitive hours = 8 x 2 x 48 = 768
Addressable = 345.6
Potential annual capacity value = $15,552

This is capacity, not guaranteed payroll savings.

# 20. FIELD CHEAT SHEET

Best signals: growing firm, many staff, tax/accounting advisory mix, active hiring, multi-office, visible tech stack.

Top hooks: busy-season admin; document collection; onboarding; reporting; AI governance.

Best first question:

> What repetitive client/document task consumes the most time during your busiest season?

ROI tools: Employee Capacity, Document Processing, Reporting/Admin, AR Efficiency.

Employee-safe: give accountants/CPAs more time for judgment/client work; no professional replacement pitch.

# 21. CERTIFICATION

# MODULE 24 CORE RULE

Accounting firms create value through professional judgment. YAD should remove avoidable administrative friction around that judgment, not pretend AI can replace the responsibility of qualified accounting and tax professionals.
