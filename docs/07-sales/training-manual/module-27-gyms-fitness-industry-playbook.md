# MODULE 27 - GYMS / FITNESS INDUSTRY PLAYBOOK

Status: Working Draft - Internal Sales Training
Vertical Priority: 18
Evidence Status: Workflow complete; membership-industry benchmarks require verified sourcing before final PDF.

## Purpose

Teach YAD salespeople how gyms, fitness studios, personal-training businesses, and multi-location fitness operators generate leads, schedule tours/trials, convert memberships, reactivate dormant prospects/members, sell training, support front-desk capacity, and measure marketing.

Core question:

> Where are membership leads, trials, tours, follow-up, member retention, staff capacity, and marketing attribution leaking between first interest and long-term member value?

# 5. ACTIVE-ADVERTISER TARGETING

Meta Ad Library is especially useful.

Search Google:

- gym + city
- personal training + city
- fitness studio + city
- Pilates / HIIT / CrossFit-style category + city as appropriate

Opener:

# 17. OBJECTIONS

## "FITNESS IS PERSONAL"

> Absolutely. Coaches and salespeople build the relationship. We're looking at the system around them - lead routing, reminders, CRM tasks, reporting, and repetitive member communication.

## "OUR SOFTWARE ALREADY DOES AUTOMATION"

> Great. What still happens manually or inconsistently outside it?

# 18. ROI EXAMPLE - TOUR NO-SHOWS

HYPOTHETICAL:

- 300 tours/trials booked monthly
- 20% no-show
- 25% of no-shows realistically recoverable
- 30% join rate among recovered shows
- $500 selected first-year/member contribution metric

No-shows = 60
Recovered shows = 15
Scenario joins = 4.5
Illustrative value = $2,250

Use actual membership economics and realistic cohort timing.

# 19. FIELD CHEAT SHEET

Best signals: active Meta ads, trial offers, multiple locations, large sales/front desk, PT programs, visible hiring.

Top hooks: paid-lead speed; tour no-show; stale lead nurture; reactivation; attribution.

Best first question:

> What happens to a paid lead that doesn't book or answer the first time?

ROI tools: Lead Response, No-Show, Reactivation, Employee Capacity.

Employee-safe: automation supports membership team/coaches; relationships and coaching remain human.

# 20. CERTIFICATION

Rep must understand lead-to-member journey, trials/no-shows, CRM, reactivation, advertiser sourcing, conservative member-value ROI, and phone/walk-in scripts.

# MODULE 27 CORE RULE

Fitness is a recurring-revenue relationship business. YAD improves the system that captures, follows up, schedules, reactivates, and measures members while coaches and staff remain responsible for the human experience.
