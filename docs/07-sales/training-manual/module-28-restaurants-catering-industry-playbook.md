# MODULE 28 - RESTAURANTS / CATERING INDUSTRY PLAYBOOK

Status: Working Draft - Internal Sales Training
Vertical Priority: 19
Evidence Status: Core workflow approved; National Restaurant Association benchmark figures require final source re-verification.

## Purpose

Teach YAD salespeople how restaurants, caterers, food trucks, and event-food businesses acquire customers, handle phone/orders/reservations, respond to catering inquiries, follow up quotes, manage repeat customers, request reviews, and reduce repetitive admin while food safety and operational decisions remain human.

Core question:

> Where are catering opportunities, reservations/orders, customer communication, repeat-customer value, staff capacity, and marketing visibility leaking around the food operation?

---

# 16. WALK-IN

Avoid meal rush times when possible.

> Hi, I'm [Rep] with Your AI Department. I don't have an appointment. We work with restaurants/caterers on catering leads, quote follow-up, customer reactivation, reviews, staff capacity, and marketing attribution. Who handles catering sales or operations?

Do not enter kitchens/food-prep areas uninvited.

---

# 17. OBJECTIONS

## "WE'RE TOO BUSY FOR ANOTHER SYSTEM"

> Then another system may be wrong. We'd first see if your existing POS/order/booking tools can be connected or configured better.

## "FOOD IS A HUMAN BUSINESS"

> Absolutely. The goal is to give staff more time for guests and events while routine lead tracking, reminders, FAQs, and reporting become more consistent.
