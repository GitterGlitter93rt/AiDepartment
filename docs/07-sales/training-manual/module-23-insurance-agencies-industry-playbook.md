# MODULE 23 - INSURANCE AGENCIES INDUSTRY PLAYBOOK

Status: Working Draft - Internal Sales Training
Vertical Priority: 14
Evidence Status: Workflow complete; carrier/agency market statistics require verified sourcing before final PDF.

## Purpose

Teach YAD salespeople how independent/captive insurance agencies generate prospects, gather quote information, follow up, handle renewals, cross-sell, manage service requests, process documents, and measure marketing while preserving licensed-agent responsibility, carrier rules, privacy, and state regulatory requirements.

Core question:

> Where are quote opportunities, renewals, service requests, employee capacity, cross-sell opportunities, and marketing visibility leaking across the agency workflow?

# 17. OBJECTIONS

## "WE ALREADY HAVE AN AMS"

> Good. What still happens outside it in spreadsheets, inboxes, producer phones, or manual follow-up?

## "ONLY A LICENSED AGENT CAN ADVISE CLIENTS"

> Agreed. We're not trying to replace licensed judgment. We improve intake, routing, reminders, documentation, CRM, and reporting around the producer.

## "OUR CARRIER CONTROLS THE SYSTEM"

> Then we work within those constraints and only integrate/configure what is technically and contractually permitted.

# 19. FIELD CHEAT SHEET

Best signals: active ads, multiple producers, commercial lines, purchased leads, visible hiring, multiple offices.

Top hooks: quote follow-up; paid lead response; renewal workflow; CSR capacity; attribution.

Best first question:

> What happens to a quote that doesn't bind on the first conversation?

# 20. CERTIFICATION

Rep must understand agency pipeline, AMS/CRM, quote/renewal workflows, licensed-agent boundaries, privacy, advertiser sourcing, and conservative ROI.

# MODULE 23 CORE RULE

Insurance agencies run on follow-up, retention, and service. YAD can make the operating system around licensed agents more consistent and measurable, but coverage advice, binding, suitability, and regulated decisions stay with qualified professionals.
