# Your AI Department Sales Training Manual

Status: Active Internal Training System
Version: Working Draft

## Purpose

This directory is the source of truth for the internal Your AI Department sales training manual, field playbook, ROI selling system, industry playbooks, printable sales forms, roleplay academy, coaching system, and certification standards.

The training system is intentionally modular so salespeople can learn, practice, use, and later search the material without needing to read one enormous document front-to-back every time they need an answer.

## Training architecture

1. Master Sales Training Manual - complete internal sales bible.
2. Field Sales Playbook - condensed day-to-day selling guide.
3. Industry Field Cards - short vertical-specific quick-reference guides.
4. ROI Calculator Forms - printable/fillable worksheets used during financial diagnosis.
5. Video Training - short demonstrations and roleplays linked from the final manual where practical.
6. Roleplay Academy - practice system for phone, field, discovery, ROI, objections, and closing.
7. Sales Management & Coaching - CRM governance, call/field review, deal reviews, forecasting, coaching, and handoff quality.
8. Final Certification - competency gates before reps advance in authority.
9. Evidence Review Register - approved/sourced evidence, pending verification, limitations, and final-publication QA.

## Approved doctrine

- Business problem first. Technology second.
- Sell business outcomes, not AI terminology.
- Diagnose before prescribing.
- Do not position AI as a way to fire or replace staff.
- Position around employee capacity, productivity, service, growth, reduced repetitive work, reduced overtime where appropriate, and better systems around good people.
- Never invent statistics, customer results, case studies, integrations, ROI, or guarantees.
- Separate theoretical exposure, realistically recoverable opportunity, and ROI/payback.
- Prefer client data over external benchmarks where reliable data is available.
- Use the simplest reliable solution that solves the business problem.
- Keep appropriate human oversight in sensitive or high-impact workflows.
- Do not promise integrations, security/compliance outcomes, delivery timelines, pricing concessions, or technical scope beyond approved truth.
- A salesperson is allowed to conclude that a prospect does not need YAD.

## Current production status

The written curriculum is now substantially built from foundational doctrine through vertical specialization, coaching, and final certification.

Before the final polished Master PDF is treated as publication-ready, complete these production passes:

1. Evidence verification - reopen all sources in `evidence-review-register.md`, replace/reject pending statistics, and assign review dates.
2. Commercial truth reconciliation - confirm the current assessment flow, current scheduling link, Managed AI Department pricing structure if finalized, discount authority, payment terms, and any changed offers.
3. Cross-linking - connect each vertical chapter to relevant ROI forms, objection sections, CRM concepts, and roleplay drills.
4. Field-card production - condense each priority vertical into a 1-2 page quick-reference field card.
5. Field Sales Playbook - extract the approximately 30-50 page daily-use version from the Master Manual.
6. Video scripts / recordings - produce the short training demos identified in Module 39 and link them where practical.
7. Visual production - create revenue-leak funnels, current-vs-future workflows, CRM backbone, employee-capacity visuals, attribution chains, human-in-loop diagrams, and industry story cards.
8. Editorial QA - remove duplication, standardize terminology, check page flow/navigation, and validate every internal cross-reference.
9. Final PDF assembly - approved YAD visual identity, hyperlinked table of contents, bookmarks, page numbers, module navigation, industry tabs, Evidence Cards, and printable/mobile readability.
10. Pilot certification - test the curriculum on real reps, capture confusion/failure points, then revise before broad rollout.

## Visual / PDF direction

Final PDF output should use the approved visual identity in `docs/01-brand/visual-identity.md` and should be highly skimmable, using diagrams, business-system visuals, ROI indicators, callout boxes, worksheets, scenario illustrations, Say This / Don't Say This panels, and end-of-module field summaries.

Final navigation should include continuous page numbering, hyperlinked TOC, PDF bookmarks, module numbers, vertical navigation, and searchable headings.

The final system should include a complete Master Sales Training Manual plus smaller field-ready assets. Generated PDFs are deliverables; editable logic/content and source discipline remain documented in this repository.
