# Your AI Department Sales Training Manual

Status: Active Internal Training System
Version: Working Draft

## Purpose

This directory is the source of truth for the internal Your AI Department sales training manual, field playbook, ROI selling system, and printable sales forms.

The training system is intentionally built in modules so salespeople can learn and use the material without needing to read one enormous document front-to-back.

## Training architecture

1. Master Sales Training Manual - complete internal sales bible.
2. Field Sales Playbook - condensed day-to-day selling guide.
3. Industry Field Cards - short vertical-specific quick-reference guides.
4. ROI Calculator Forms - printable/fillable worksheets used during financial diagnosis.
5. Video Training - short demonstrations and roleplays linked from the final manual where practical.
6. Roleplay and Certification - competency checks before a salesperson advances.

## Approved doctrine

- Business problem first. Technology second.
- Sell business outcomes, not AI terminology.
- Diagnose before prescribing.
- Do not position AI as a way to fire or replace staff.
- Position around employee capacity, productivity, service, growth, reduced repetitive work, reduced overtime where appropriate, and better systems around good people.
- Never invent statistics, customer results, case studies, integrations, ROI, or guarantees.
- Separate theoretical exposure, realistically recoverable opportunity, and ROI/payback.
- Prefer client data over external benchmarks where reliable data is available.
- Use the simplest reliable solution that solves the business problem.
- Keep appropriate human oversight in sensitive or high-impact workflows.
