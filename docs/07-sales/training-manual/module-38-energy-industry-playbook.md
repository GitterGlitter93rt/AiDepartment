# MODULE 38 - ENERGY INDUSTRY PLAYBOOK

Status: Working Draft - Internal Sales Training
Evidence Status: Workflow complete; subsector-specific energy benchmarks/regulatory evidence require verified sourcing before final PDF.

## Purpose

Teach YAD salespeople how growth-oriented energy businesses such as solar, energy-efficiency contractors, distributed-energy providers, and related service organizations generate leads, schedule assessments/site visits, prepare proposals, follow up, manage project documents, communicate status, and report performance while engineering, utility, financial, safety, and regulatory decisions remain under qualified human control.

Core question:

> Where are qualified leads, site assessments, proposals, document workflows, customer communication, and employee capacity leaking between first inquiry and completed energy project?

---

# 1. SEGMENT THE VERTICAL FIRST

Energy is broad.

Potential YAD prospects may include:

- residential/commercial solar installers
- battery/storage installers
- energy-efficiency contractors
- energy consulting/service firms
- EV charging infrastructure providers
- distributed-energy businesses
- commercial energy project developers

Utility/grid operators and highly regulated infrastructure require different enterprise/risk review and should not be treated like ordinary SMB sales targets.

# 3. ACTIVE-ADVERTISER TARGETING

For consumer-facing energy companies, use Google/Meta Ad Library.

Potential searches:

- solar installer + city
- commercial solar + city
- EV charger installer + city
- energy efficiency contractor + city

Opener:

> I noticed you're actively advertising [solar/EV/energy service] in [market]. Once a paid lead comes in and doesn't move forward on the first conversation, what happens next?

Never infer spend or subsidy eligibility.

# 6. PROJECT DOCUMENT WORKFLOW

Potential documents:

- proposals
- utility/interconnection forms
- permits
- engineering docs
- financing docs
- customer approvals
- equipment documentation
- inspections

AI may classify/extract/route/administer approved workflows.

It does not approve engineering, utility, tax, financial, or legal requirements.

---

# 7. CUSTOMER STATUS COMMUNICATION

Long project cycles can generate repetitive questions.

Potential approved milestones:

- site assessment scheduled
- design review pending
- permit submitted/received
- utility step
- equipment scheduled
- installation date
- inspection/activation status

Automation should reflect verified system data and route exceptions to humans.

# 15. OBJECTIONS

## "OUR PROJECTS ARE TOO REGULATED / TECHNICAL"

> The technical and regulatory decisions stay with qualified people. We're looking at the repeatable business workflow around leads, proposals, documents, communication, and reporting.

## "INCENTIVES CHANGE ALL THE TIME"

> Then we should not automate claims about incentives without approved current data and human review.

---

# 16. ROI EXAMPLE - PROPOSAL FOLLOW-UP

Use Unsold Estimate ROI using the company's actual signed-project value, addressable pipeline, and realistic incremental close scenario. Do not include tax credits/incentives as guaranteed customer value.

---

# 17. FIELD CHEAT SHEET

Signals: active ads, many sales reps, project coordinators, financing, long proposal cycles, multiple markets.

Top hooks: proposal follow-up; paid lead speed; project document admin; customer status; CRM-to-project handoff.

Employee-safe: systems support sales/project teams; engineering, safety, utility, regulatory and financial decisions remain human.

# MODULE 38 CORE RULE

Energy projects often involve long, document-heavy handoffs between sales and delivery. YAD improves the business system around those handoffs while qualified professionals retain responsibility for technical, financial, utility, regulatory, and safety decisions.
