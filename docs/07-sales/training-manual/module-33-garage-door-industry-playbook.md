# MODULE 33 - GARAGE DOOR INDUSTRY PLAYBOOK

Status: Working Draft - Internal Sales Training
Evidence Status: Workflow complete; industry benchmarks require verified sourcing before final PDF.

## Purpose

Teach YAD salespeople how garage-door service/install companies capture urgent calls, schedule technicians, follow up replacement estimates, manage paid search, and increase office/technician capacity.

Core question:

> Where are urgent service calls, replacement opportunities, technician capacity, follow-up, and marketing attribution leaking between the first inquiry and completed job?

---

# 1. WHY GARAGE DOOR FITS YAD

- urgent stuck/broken door calls
- high-intent Google search
- phone-heavy intake
- same-day service
- repair vs replacement
- opener replacement
- estimate follow-up
- route/scheduling
- local reviews
- field-service systems

# 3. ACTIVE-ADVERTISER TARGETING

Search:

- garage door repair + city
- garage door opener repair + city
- garage door replacement + city

Opener:

> I noticed you're actively advertising garage-door repair in [market]. When one of those paid calls comes in and the office can't answer live, what happens next?

---

# 4. MISSED CALL / SPEED

Potential YAD support: call routing, missed-call SMS, CRM capture, scheduling, callback tasks, after-hours handling where appropriate.

---

# 5. REPLACEMENT ESTIMATE FOLLOW-UP

Ask:

> What happens to a full-door/replacement estimate that doesn't close on the first visit?

Pipeline: Estimate -> Follow-Up -> Questions/Financing -> Won/Lost/Future.

---

# 6. SCHEDULING / ROUTE CAPACITY

Potential automation: confirmations, technician ETA, rescheduling, customer reminders.

Technician safety/repair decisions remain human.

---

# 7. CRM / ATTRIBUTION

Ideal:

SOURCE -> CALL/FORM -> BOOKING -> TECH VISIT -> SALE -> REVENUE

Ask:

> Can Google Ads be tied to actual completed repair/replacement revenue?

---

# 8. EMPLOYEE CAPACITY

Look for repetitive scheduling, calls, estimate reminders, CRM notes, review requests, reporting.

# 11. COLD CALL

> Hey [Name], [Rep] with Your AI Department. Quick question: when an urgent garage-door repair call comes in and everybody is already tied up, what makes sure that opportunity gets recovered?

---

# 12. WALK-IN

> Hi, I'm [Rep] with Your AI Department. We help service contractors with missed calls, scheduling, estimate follow-up, CRM, employee capacity, and marketing attribution. Who oversees operations here?

---

# 13. ROI

Use Missed Call ROI for repair leads or Unsold Estimate ROI for replacement jobs, using actual customer values and legitimate-opportunity percentages.

---

# 14. SAFETY

Automation does not diagnose springs, doors, electrical openers, structural conditions, or other repair/safety issues. Qualified technicians decide.

---

# 15. FIELD CHEAT SHEET

Signals: active Google Ads, same-day/emergency service, multiple trucks, replacement installs, strong reviews.

Top hooks: missed calls; replacement follow-up; scheduling; attribution; office capacity.

Employee-safe: systems support CSRs/techs; field safety/repair remains human.

# MODULE 33 CORE RULE

Garage-door companies are another speed-driven home-service business. YAD helps them capture and follow up the opportunity without substituting automation for skilled technician judgment.
