# MODULE 29 - E-COMMERCE INDUSTRY PLAYBOOK

Status: Working Draft - Internal Sales Training
Vertical Priority: 20
Evidence Status: Workflow complete; category-specific benchmarks require source verification before final PDF.

## Purpose

Teach YAD salespeople how e-commerce brands acquire traffic, convert shoppers, recover abandoned intent, handle customer service, retain buyers, manage product/content workflows, connect advertising to revenue, and increase employee capacity.

Core question:

> Where are paid traffic, conversion, abandoned intent, customer service, retention, employee time, and attribution leaking between the ad impression and profitable repeat customer?

---

# 1. WHY E-COMMERCE FITS YAD

Potential characteristics:

- high paid-media spend
- Meta / Google / TikTok or other channels
- landing/product pages
- conversion-rate optimization
- carts/checkouts
- email/SMS
- customer support
- reviews
- repeat purchases
- large product catalogs
- Shopify / other commerce platform
- fulfillment / inventory systems
- analytics fragmentation
- creative testing

---

# 2. BUSINESS MODEL

Important metrics may include:

- revenue
- gross margin
- average order value
- conversion rate
- customer acquisition cost
- repeat purchase
- refund / return rate
- contribution margin
- lifetime value

Do not optimize to ROAS alone without understanding margin and attribution.

# 4. ACTIVE-ADVERTISER TARGETING

Meta Ad Library is particularly useful.

Research:

- active creatives
- offers
- landing pages
- product categories
- frequency of creative testing signals
- Google Shopping/Search presence
- email/SMS capture

Opener:

> I noticed you're actively running Meta campaigns for [product]. I'm not calling to tell you to buy more ads. Can you trace that paid traffic through contribution/repeat value, or are you mostly optimizing to platform-reported ROAS?

Never infer spend.

---

# 5. CONVERSION RATE / LANDING PAGE

Potential issues:

- slow mobile page
- weak product explanation
- generic landing page
- poor trust signals
- complicated checkout
- weak offer alignment
- no structured testing

YAD can support landing pages, CRO, tracking, creative testing, and analytics where offered.

Do not promise conversion uplift.

# 18. IN-PERSON / OFFICE OPENER

For brands with offices/warehouses:

> Hi, I'm [Rep] with Your AI Department. I don't have an appointment. We work with e-commerce teams on paid-media attribution, conversion workflows, customer support, reactivation, reporting, and employee capacity. Who oversees growth or e-commerce operations?

Do not enter warehouses/operations areas uninvited.

---

# 19. OBJECTIONS

## "WE ALREADY HAVE KLAVIYO / SHOPIFY AUTOMATIONS"

> Good. The question is whether the whole system is measured and whether support, attribution, reporting, and retention still have manual gaps.

## "OUR AGENCY HANDLES THE ADS"

> Great. We may complement them by connecting acquisition to CRM/customer data, repeat purchase, support, and contribution economics.

## "AI CONTENT WILL HURT OUR BRAND"

> Then AI should support ideation/drafting while brand owners approve final content. Maximum automation is not the objective.

---

# 20. ROI EXAMPLE - SUPPORT CAPACITY

HYPOTHETICAL:

- 2,500 tickets/month
- 35% repetitive status/FAQ tickets
- 50% of repetitive volume realistically addressable
- 4 minutes saved/ticket
- $30 loaded support labor/hour

Addressable tickets = 437.5
Hours = 29.17
Monthly capacity value = ~$875
Annual = ~$10,500

Use actual handle time and staffing. Capacity is not guaranteed payroll savings.

---

# 21. FIELD CHEAT SHEET

Best signals: active Meta ads, visible Google Shopping, significant catalog, high support volume, subscription/repeat products, active hiring.

Top hooks: attribution beyond ROAS; abandoned checkout; support capacity; retention; reporting.

Best first question:

> Can you trace paid acquisition to profitable repeat customers rather than just platform-reported conversions?

ROI tools: Advertising Efficiency, Employee Capacity, Reactivation, Final ROI.

Employee-safe: AI handles repetitive analysis/support drafts; humans own brand, exceptions, product, and financial decisions.

---

# 22. CERTIFICATION

Rep must understand e-commerce funnel, CAC/AOV/margin distinction, attribution limitations, support automation, retention, advertiser sourcing, and conservative capacity/ROI math.

# MODULE 29 CORE RULE

E-commerce has abundant data but not always abundant clarity. YAD should connect advertising, conversion, customer value, support, and retention into a measurable system rather than optimizing one dashboard in isolation.
