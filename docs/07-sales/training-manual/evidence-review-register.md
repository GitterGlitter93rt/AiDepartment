# SALES TRAINING MANUAL - EVIDENCE REVIEW REGISTER

Status: Active Internal QA Register
Purpose: Prevent unverified, stale, or overgeneralized statistics from entering the final sales manual, field cards, scripts, decks, or rep training.

## Evidence rule

No external statistic becomes approved sales language until the source has been reviewed for:

- original source / publisher
- study or dataset type
- sample / population
- geography
- data year
- publication date
- exact claim
- limitation
- approved rep wording
- forbidden overgeneralization
- next review date

Client-specific ROI should prefer verified client data over industry benchmarks.

# A. SOURCED / DRAFT-VERIFIED EVIDENCE

These sources have been accessed during the current manual build. They still require a final editorial citation check before publication, but the underlying source/claim has been located.

## A1. ServiceTitan - 2025 Residential Services Report

Source: ServiceTitan press release / Residential Services Report.
Population: 1,000+ residential service contractors in HVAC, electrical, plumbing, roofing, and related trades in the United States.
Claims used in manual:

- 64% reported phone calls as the dominant communication channel.
- Among contractors with $10M+ annual revenue, 47% reported that following up on unsold estimates resulted in 11-15% of income.

Approved wording:

> In a 2025 ServiceTitan survey of more than 1,000 residential service contractors, 64% said phone calls remained their dominant communication channel.

> In the same cross-trade study, nearly half of contractors above $10M in annual revenue reported meaningful income associated with following up on unsold estimates.

Forbidden:

> 64% of HVAC customers only use the phone.

> Automated follow-up will increase roofing/plumbing/HVAC revenue by 15%.

Limitations: vendor research; cross-trade rather than vertical-exclusive.
URL: https://www.servicetitan.com/press/residential-industry-report-2025
Final citation check: REQUIRED.

# D. FINAL EVIDENCE CARD TEMPLATE

Every approved statistic should be converted to this structure:

## Claim

Exact statistic / finding.

## Source

Publisher and original URL.

## Original research?

Yes / No / Derived.

## Population / sample

Who or what was measured?

## Geography

US / state / global / other.

## Data year

When was the underlying data collected?

## Publication date

When was it published?

## Limitation

What should the salesperson understand?

## Approved wording

Sentence the rep may use.

## Forbidden wording

The overclaim the rep may not make.

## Review date

When should the claim be refreshed?

---

# E. CLIENT DATA OVERRIDES BENCHMARKS

If a prospect can provide reliable:

- call logs
- lead data
- close rates
- customer values
- employee compensation
- ad spend
- no-show rates
- estimate pipeline
- revenue attribution

use that instead of an industry average whenever practical.

External evidence should help a rep know what to investigate.

It should not replace discovery.

# FINAL RULE

A smaller number we can defend is more valuable than an impressive statistic we cannot explain.
