# MODULE 36 - MANUFACTURING INDUSTRY PLAYBOOK

Status: Working Draft - Internal Sales Training
Evidence Status: Workflow complete; subsector-specific benchmarks require verified sourcing before final PDF.

## Purpose

Teach YAD salespeople how manufacturers handle B2B sales/RFQs, quoting, customer communication, documents, production reporting, internal knowledge, quality/admin workflows, and system integration while engineering, safety, quality, and production decisions remain under qualified human control.

Core question:

> Where are RFQs, sales follow-up, document processing, employee knowledge, reporting, and cross-system data movement consuming capacity or reducing visibility across the manufacturer?

---

Questions:

# 10. EMPLOYEE CAPACITY

Look for:

- report assembly
- RFQ data entry
- quote admin
- SOP search
- meeting summaries

- quality-document admin

Ask:

---

# 11. SAFETY / QUALITY / ENGINEERING BOUNDARY

AI does not independently approve:

- engineering design
- product safety
- quality release
- machine operation
- process parameters
- maintenance safety
- regulatory compliance
- material substitutions
- production changes

Qualified humans and validated systems remain responsible.

Never enter production areas without authorization/PPE/safety compliance.

---

# 17. OBJECTIONS

## "OUR PROCESSES ARE TOO TECHNICAL FOR AI"

> The technical decisions may be. We're often looking at document intake, routing, search, reporting, CRM, and repetitive admin around those decisions.

## "WE CAN'T RISK HALLUCINATIONS"

> Agreed. High-consequence outputs need validation and controlled source data. Some workflows may be ordinary automation rather than generative AI.

---

# 18. ROI EXAMPLE - ENGINEERING/ESTIMATING CAPACITY

HYPOTHETICAL:

- 12 technical employees
- 2 repetitive admin hours/week
- 48 weeks
- 35% addressable
- $60 loaded hourly cost

Annual repetitive = 1,152 hours
Addressable = 403.2
Potential annual capacity = $24,192

Not payroll savings; value depends on how capacity is redeployed.

---

# 19. FIELD CHEAT SHEET

Signals: ERP+CRM, RFQ volume, multiple facilities, engineers doing admin, complex documents, hiring.

Top hooks: RFQ processing; quote follow-up; technical employee capacity; system integration; knowledge/reporting.

Best first question:

> What repetitive work surrounds your RFQ/quote process that doesn't require engineering judgment?

ROI tools: Employee Capacity, Document Processing, Reporting/Admin.

Human rule: engineering, quality, safety, production decisions remain qualified/human.

# MODULE 36 CORE RULE

Manufacturing AI should begin in the business and information workflows around production, not with uncontrolled automation of production itself. YAD earns credibility by protecting engineering, quality, and safety authority.
