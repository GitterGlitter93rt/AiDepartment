# MODULE 17 - ELECTRICAL INDUSTRY PLAYBOOK

Status: Working Draft - Internal Sales Training
Vertical Priority: 8
Evidence Status: Core workflow approved; labor-stat publication citation should be rechecked before final PDF.

## Purpose

Teach YAD salespeople how residential/light-commercial electrical contractors generate calls, schedule technicians, sell larger projects, follow up estimates, coordinate customer communication, use skilled labor, and measure marketing.

The rep diagnoses business workflow, not electrical safety or code.

Core question:

> Where are urgent calls, high-value project opportunities, technician capacity, estimate follow-up, and marketing visibility leaking between the first inquiry and completed electrical revenue?

# 4. SAFETY BOUNDARY

AI must not independently make safety-critical electrical decisions.

Qualified electricians / appropriate professionals remain responsible for:

- diagnosis
- electrical safety
- code compliance
- installation decisions
- load / system design
- permits
- inspections

YAD can improve the business process around those decisions.

---

# 5. CALL / AFTER-HOURS WORKFLOW

Questions:

> What happens to a new electrical service call if nobody answers live?

> How are after-hours inquiries handled?

> Do missed calls create an automatic callback task or acknowledgment?

Potential solutions: routing, SMS acknowledgment, CRM capture, scheduling, AI phone handling for defined non-safety intake, human escalation.

# 7. ESTIMATE FOLLOW-UP

High-value quoted jobs may include panel/service upgrades, generators, rewires, EV charging, larger commercial work.

Ask:

> What happens to a $5,000-$20,000 type project estimate that doesn't close during the first conversation?

Do not insert those values unless the contractor confirms their range; use as hypothetical language only.

Potential CRM pipeline: Estimate Sent -> Follow-Up -> Financing/Questions -> Won/Lost/Future.

---

# 8. TECHNICIAN CAPACITY

Skilled electrical labor should not be consumed by avoidable admin.

Look for:

- repeated note entry
- estimate follow-up drafting
- customer ETA messages
- job summaries
- parts / material request admin
- photo/document organization
- report assembly

Prior research anchor: BLS 2025 electrician median pay was approximately $63,190. Final source/date must be reverified before publication.

Use actual client loaded labor for ROI.

# 16. OBJECTIONS

## "OUR TECHNICIANS HANDLE THEIR OWN FOLLOW-UP"

> Good. Can management see whether it happened consistently, and could the system handle reminders/admin while the technician handles the actual customer conversation?

## "WE CAN'T AUTOMATE ELECTRICAL WORK"

> Agreed. We're not trying to automate electrical judgment. We're looking at the business workflow around intake, scheduling, follow-up, communication, and reporting.

# 17. ROI EXAMPLE - ESTIMATE FOLLOW-UP

HYPOTHETICAL:

- 40 larger estimates/month
- 15 remain unsold
- 50% still addressable
- 7% incremental close scenario
- $7,500 average sold project

Addressable = 7.5
Scenario wins = 0.525
Illustrative monthly revenue = $3,937.50

Use client data; do not promise the outcome.

# 18. FIELD CHEAT SHEET

Best signals: emergency service, active ads, panel/generator/EV work, multiple trucks, financing, active hiring.

Top hooks: missed calls; high-value estimate follow-up; technician capacity; attribution; field-service CRM.

Best first question:

> What happens to a larger electrical estimate that doesn't close the first time?

ROI tool: Missed Call ROI or Unsold Estimate ROI.

Employee-safe position: better systems around licensed people; no replacement/safety positioning.

---

# 19. CERTIFICATION

Rep must explain the electrical customer journey, separate business automation from electrical judgment, diagnose call/estimate leakage, use advertiser sourcing, discuss CRM, quantify conservatively, and deliver phone/in-person scripts.

# MODULE 17 CORE RULE

Electrical contractors make money through skilled human judgment and execution. YAD improves the system that brings the right customer to that skilled labor and handles the repetitive work around it.
