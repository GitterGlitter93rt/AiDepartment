# MODULE 40 - SALES MANAGEMENT AND COACHING

Status: Working Draft - Internal Sales Training

## Purpose

A strong sales manual is not enough.

Your AI Department also needs a management system that answers:

- What should a rep do every day?
- What should be measured?
- How should calls and walk-ins be coached?
- How should deals be reviewed?
- How should the CRM be used?
- How should forecasts be built?
- How do managers detect overpromising before it becomes a delivery problem?
- How do new reps improve over 30, 60, and 90 days?

The objective is not maximum activity for its own sake.

The objective is a repeatable system that creates qualified business conversations and protects YAD credibility.

---

# 1. MANAGE OUTCOMES AND LEADING INDICATORS

Do not manage only:

- dials
- emails
- doors visited

Those are activity indicators.

Also measure:

- decision-makers reached
- meaningful conversations
- business problems identified
- financially quantifiable problems
- qualified opportunities
- next steps earned
- proposals presented
- closed business
- disqualified prospects

A rep who makes 100 poor calls is not automatically outperforming a rep who creates five high-quality diagnostic conversations.

---

# 2. CORE SALES FUNNEL

Recommended working CRM stages:

## 1. TARGET / PROSPECT

Account identified but no meaningful contact.

## 2. CONTACTED

Two-way contact or credible routing to the correct stakeholder.

## 3. DISCOVERY / QUALIFICATION

Business problem, decision-maker, urgency, and fit are being evaluated.

## 4. FINANCIAL DIAGNOSIS / BUSINESS CASE

Relevant volumes, values, failure rates, and scenario economics are being developed.

## 5. STRATEGY / SOLUTION DEFINITION

Next engagement or implementation direction is being defined.

## 6. PROPOSAL / DECISION

Scope, assumptions, investment, stakeholders, and decision process are active.

## 7. CLOSED WON

Agreement completed under current company process.

## 8. CLOSED LOST / DISQUALIFIED

No current deal; reason recorded.

## 9. DELIVERY HANDOFF

Won deal transferred with complete promise/scope record.

Final CRM configuration may use different labels, but stage definitions must remain clear.

---

# 3. NEVER ADVANCE A STAGE BECAUSE THE REP FEELS GOOD

Each stage should have evidence.

Example:

Discovery stage should usually include:

- identified problem
- prospect description of current workflow
- likely decision-maker / stakeholder
- next action

Financial Diagnosis should include:

- at least some defensible inputs or explicit missing-data plan
- business consequence
- source labels

Proposal should include:

- defined scope
- stakeholder / decision process
- pricing authority
- assumptions
- measurement plan

---

# 4. REQUIRED CRM NOTES

Bad note:

> Interested in AI.

Better:

> Owner says after-hours calls reach voicemail. Estimates 35-50 calls/month after hours but has not reviewed call logs. Average service customer estimated at $700. Uses ServiceTitan. Wants operations manager involved next. Next step: review 60 days of call data Thursday.

Track:

- exact prospect language
- current workflow
- pain
- frequency / volume
- economic inputs and source
- systems
- stakeholders
- objection
- next step
- date
- rep commitment

---

# 5. CLOSED-LOST / DISQUALIFICATION REASONS

Recommended categories:

- no meaningful pain
- economics too small
- no urgency
- budget / timing
- no decision access
- chose competitor
- built internally
- existing system sufficient
- technical infeasibility
- security/compliance constraint
- no response
- not a YAD-fit use case
- prospect demanded guarantees / unsafe scope
- do not contact

Managers should learn from losses rather than hiding them.

---

# 6. PHONE PROSPECTING METRICS

Potential leading metrics:

- calls attempted
- conversations
- decision-makers reached
- gatekeeper referrals
- qualified conversations
- meetings / next steps
- disqualifications

Conversion metrics:

- conversation / dial
- decision-maker / conversation
- qualified / decision-maker
- meeting / qualified

Track by:

- industry
- hook
- list/source
- rep

This tells us whether the problem is targeting, opening, discovery, or closing.

---

# 7. IN-PERSON FIELD METRICS

Track:

- businesses visited
- decision-makers identified
- decision-makers spoken to
- meaningful conversations
- return visits earned
- appointments booked
- financial opportunities identified
- disqualifications
- follow-up completion

Also track:

- route / geography
- vertical
- time block
- public qualification signal

Do not reward random driving and door-count theater.

---

# 8. ADVERTISER-SOURCED PROSPECT METRICS

Because active Google / Meta advertising is an important YAD qualification signal, managers should track:

- sourced from Google Sponsored search?
- sourced from Meta Ad Library?
- active service / offer advertised
- phone-heavy or form-heavy funnel
- multi-channel advertiser?
- meeting rate
- qualified-opportunity rate
- close rate

Over time YAD should learn whether active advertisers outperform generic cold lists by vertical.

---

# 9. HOOK PERFORMANCE

Every rep should log the opening angle used where practical.

Categories:

- missed call
- speed to lead
- unsold estimate
- employee capacity
- attribution
- CRM
- reporting
- reactivation
- no-show
- growth / scalability
- integration

Manager asks:

> Which hooks create qualified conversations, not merely longer calls?

---

# 10. WEEKLY DEAL REVIEW

For every meaningful opportunity ask:

1. What problem did the prospect say they have?
2. How does the workflow work today?
3. What is the evidence?
4. How often does the issue occur?
5. What is it worth?
6. Which inputs are verified vs estimated?
7. What should remain human?
8. What system / integration constraints exist?
9. Who is the decision-maker?
10. Who else influences the deal?
11. What is the next specific action?
12. What could kill the deal?
13. What has the rep promised?

If the rep cannot answer these, the deal may be less qualified than the CRM stage suggests.

---

# 11. THE PROMISE AUDIT

Before proposal and again before handoff, the manager should ask:

- Did we promise a result?
- Did we promise an integration?
- Did we promise a delivery date?
- Did we promise a specific technology?
- Did we promise pricing / discount / payment terms?
- Did we promise automation of a sensitive decision?
- Did we imply employee replacement?

Anything outside approved truth must be corrected before signature whenever possible.

---

# 12. FORECAST DISCIPLINE

Forecasts should reflect evidence, not rep optimism.

Possible working categories:

## PIPELINE

Qualified but substantial work remains.

## BEST CASE

Credible path to decision exists in forecast period but unresolved risk remains.

## COMMIT

Decision process, stakeholders, commercial terms, and next steps are sufficiently clear that the manager believes close is highly likely.

These are proposed internal categories and may be changed when YAD finalizes its CRM.

Never create false certainty to make the forecast look better.

---

# 13. DEAL HEALTH CHECK

Score 1-5:

- economic value
- pain frequency
- data quality
- urgency
- decision access
- feasibility
- measurability
- stakeholder alignment
- implementation readiness
- expansion potential

A large dollar opportunity with no decision access and poor data may still be weak.

---

# 14. DAILY COACHING RHYTHM

A manager does not need a one-hour meeting every day.

Potential 10-15 minute daily check:

- top priorities
- today's field/call target
- one deal blocker
- one skill focus
- yesterday's follow-up misses

Do not turn the daily into a status-meeting marathon.

---

# 15. WEEKLY COACHING RHYTHM

Recommended components:

- pipeline/deal review
- one recorded call / roleplay review
- field conversion review
- hook/vertical performance
- CRM hygiene
- one skill assignment

Manager should identify one or two improvements, not twenty.

---

# 16. MONTHLY REVIEW

Review:

- qualified pipeline created
- revenue / wins
- stage conversion
- vertical conversion
- advertiser-sourced performance
- calls vs field performance
- average deal cycle
- proposal conversion
- closed-lost reasons
- claim/promise errors
- training progress

Look for system problems, not only individual blame.

---

# 17. CALL REVIEW SCORECARD

Score 1-5 each:

- opener relevance
- tone
- listening
- discovery quality
- follow-up questions
- quantification
- YAD explanation
- employee-safe language
- objection handling
- next step
- claim discipline

Manager should quote specific moments from the call when coaching.

---

# 18. FIELD REVIEW SCORECARD

Score:

- appearance / professionalism
- respect for workplace
- front-desk handling
- opening relevance
- body language
- one-question discipline
- discovery
- use of field tools
- exit / follow-up
- CRM notes

Hard fail for refusing to leave or violating safety/privacy boundaries.

---

# 19. 30 / 60 / 90 DAY REP DEVELOPMENT

## FIRST 30 DAYS

Rep should master:

- YAD doctrine
- CRM fundamentals
- economics / ROI basics
- cold-call opener
- walk-in behavior
- top objections
- two core verticals
- CRM note standards

Focus on practice and supervised prospecting.

## DAYS 31-60

Rep should:

- run discovery
- use ROI worksheets
- handle broader objections
- work advertiser-sourced targets
- perform independent field routes
- specialize in 3-5 verticals
- participate in proposal meetings

## DAYS 61-90

Rep should:

- own qualified opportunities
- run deeper discovery
- present approved business cases
- manage multiple stakeholders
- pass final certification
- demonstrate clean handoffs

Progress depends on competency, not merely days employed.

---

# 20. PERFORMANCE PROBLEM DIAGNOSIS

If low meetings:

Check:

- list quality
- public signals
- opener
- gatekeeper skill
- call volume
- field route quality

If many meetings but few opportunities:

Check:

- discovery
- targeting
- vertical knowledge
- pain quantification

If many opportunities but few proposals:

Check:

- data collection
- next-step control
- stakeholder access
- feasibility

If many proposals but few wins:

Check:

- qualification
- value case
- proposal scope
- decision process
- price / risk
- competitor positioning

Do not solve every performance issue by demanding more dials.

---

# 21. COACH THE BEHAVIOR, NOT THE PERSONALITY

Use:

> You answered the integration question before asking what they actually needed connected. Next time, ask system + data + action before responding.

Avoid:

> You're just not technical enough.

Specific behavior can be trained.

---

# 22. CLAIM DISCIPLINE AUDIT

Managers should periodically sample:

- emails
- proposals
- CRM notes
- call recordings
- field collateral

Look for:

- unsupported statistics
- guarantees
- invented case studies
- employee replacement language
- unsupported integrations
- unapproved pricing
- stale evidence

One exaggerated claim can damage trust across the company.

---

# 23. EVIDENCE EXPIRATION

Every major external statistic should eventually have:

- source
- sample
- data year
- publication date
- limitation
- approved wording
- review date

Managers should not let reps keep using an old statistic because it sounds good.

---

# 24. PROPOSAL QUALITY GATE

Manager approval should confirm:

- problem defined
- workflow confirmed
- financial assumptions sourced
- scope clear
- exclusions clear
- technical feasibility reasonable
- sensitive/human decisions identified
- third-party costs identified where known
- success metrics defined
- decision-maker involved
- price approved

Interest alone does not make a proposal ready.

---

# 25. DELIVERY HANDOFF QUALITY

Sales must transfer:

- problem statement
- current workflow
- desired workflow
- client systems
- assumptions
- scope
- exclusions
- stakeholders
- promised timeline if approved
- pricing/commercial terms
- ROI model
- security/compliance issues
- human-review requirements
- success metrics

Delivery should never discover after signature that sales promised an impossible integration.

---

# 26. SALES / DELIVERY FEEDBACK LOOP

After implementation, delivery should tell sales:

- what assumption was wrong
- what integration was harder/easier
- what clients actually valued
- what adoption problems occurred
- what KPI improved
- what did not improve

Sales should update future discovery and claims based on real delivery evidence.

---

# 27. CASE STUDY ACQUISITION

At kickoff / close, where appropriate, establish permission/process to collect:

- baseline
- implementation
- 30/60/90-day metrics
- client quote / approval
- limitations

Do not wait six months and try to reconstruct the baseline from memory.

Only approved verified results become sales proof.

---

# 28. SALES MANAGER DASHBOARD

Recommended categories:

## ACTIVITY

calls, visits, follow-ups

## CONNECTION

decision-makers, meaningful conversations

## QUALIFICATION

qualified opportunities, financial diagnoses

## PIPELINE

value by stage, next-step coverage

## CONVERSION

meeting, proposal, win rates

## QUALITY

CRM hygiene, promise audit, roleplay score

## LEARNING

vertical/hook performance, lost reasons

---

# 29. DON'T CREATE A TOXIC SALES CULTURE

Do not reward:

- lying to get meetings
- fake urgency
- overpromising
- hiding lost deals
- manipulating CRM stages
- trashing competitors
- pressure that causes reps to invent claims

YAD should build a sales culture where credibility compounds.

---

# 30. MANAGER CERTIFICATION

A YAD sales manager should be able to:

- score calls consistently
- coach phone and field reps
- inspect ROI assumptions
- catch unsupported promises
- run a deal review
- diagnose funnel performance
- approve / reject proposal readiness
- coach vertical knowledge
- manage evidence freshness
- protect delivery handoff

# MODULE 40 CORE RULE

Sales management is not counting activity and demanding more of it.

It is building a system where the right prospects receive the right questions, qualified opportunities advance with evidence, reps improve through specific coaching, and nothing gets sold that delivery cannot responsibly fulfill.
