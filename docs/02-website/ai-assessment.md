In approximately 7-12 minutes, the assessment evaluates key areas of your business and produces an initial AI Department Score with personalized opportunity recommendations.

# HOW LONG DOES IT TAKE?

Target completion time:

Approximately 7-12 minutes.

The assessment uses branching logic so businesses only receive deeper questions when those questions are relevant.

For example:

Companies that advertise may receive additional marketing questions.

Companies with internal finance teams may receive additional administrative questions.

Companies with meaningful lead volume may receive deeper sales questions.

This keeps the assessment useful without making every prospect answer every possible question.

# REPORT INTEGRITY

Assessment results must be honest.

Do not:

- Invent ROI
- Guarantee savings
