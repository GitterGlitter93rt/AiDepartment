# Your AI Department — V1 Legal & Policy Pages

Status: Required Before Launch

This document defines the required legal/policy page architecture.

Final legal language should accurately reflect the actual website, vendors, data flows, business entity, and jurisdiction at launch.

Do not publish fabricated legal details.

## Required Routes

### Privacy Policy

Planned route:

/privacy/

Must address, as applicable:

- information submitted through contact forms
- assessment data
- business contact information
- scheduling information
- cookies and analytics
- advertising measurement technologies
- server/log information
- third-party processors
- data retention
- security
- user rights where applicable
- contact information
- policy updates

The final policy must reflect the actual production stack.

Do not claim that data is never shared with service providers if processors are used.

## Terms of Use

Planned route:

/terms/

Potential areas:

- website informational purpose
- intellectual property
- acceptable use
- no guarantee of business results
- no guarantee of AI output accuracy
- third-party services/links
- limitation language appropriate to the business
- governing terms
- changes to terms
- contact information

Final legal language should be reviewed against the actual business entity and applicable law before launch.

## Cookie / Tracking Disclosure

Determine whether this will be:

- a dedicated page
or
- incorporated into the Privacy Policy

The disclosure must reflect actual technologies deployed.

Potential future technologies may include:

- analytics
- advertising conversion tracking
- embedded scheduling
- security tools

Do not list technologies that are not actually installed.

## Contact Form Consent

Forms should include concise consent/disclosure language describing:

- why information is collected
- that the business may respond to the inquiry
- links to Privacy Policy and Terms where appropriate

Marketing consent must not be implied if the user is only submitting an inquiry.

## Assessment Data

The Privacy Policy should explain how assessment responses and contact information are handled.

Do not describe assessment answers as anonymous if they are connected to identifiable contact information.

## Calendly

Calendly is the approved scheduling provider but is not yet implemented.

Once implemented, the policy should accurately disclose scheduling-related processing and relevant third-party involvement.

## Stripe

Stripe is approved for the paid Executive AI Advisory Session but is not yet implemented.

Do not claim that Your AI Department stores payment-card information if payment is processed directly by Stripe and the implementation confirms that architecture.

## Copyright Notice

The site footer may use a standard factual copyright notice such as:

© [current year] Your AI Department. All rights reserved.

The year should be generated or maintained correctly.

Do not imply trademark registration unless registration actually exists.

## Footer

Required legal links:

- Privacy Policy
- Terms of Use

Cookie/tracking policy link may be added if implemented as a separate page.

## Legal Review

Before public launch, verify:

- correct legal/business entity name
- appropriate contact address/email
- jurisdiction
- actual third-party vendors
- actual tracking technologies
- actual form behavior
- actual scheduling implementation
- actual payment implementation

Do not treat placeholder language as final legal advice.
