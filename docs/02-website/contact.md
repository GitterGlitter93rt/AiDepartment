# WHAT HAPPENS NEXT

After you contact us:

1. We review your inquiry.

2. We determine the most appropriate next step.

3. A member of Your AI Department may contact you to gather additional context.

4. Qualified companies may be invited to a strategy call.

5. If appropriate, we may recommend an assessment, workshop, audit, implementation project, or ongoing engagement.

---

# WHAT TO EXPECT

Our first objective is to understand the business problem.

We may ask questions about:

- Revenue
- Employees
- Technology
- Lead generation
- Sales process
- Customer service
- Operations
- Administrative workload
- AI usage
- Business goals
- Current software
- Marketing spend
- Implementation priorities

The goal is to understand whether we can create meaningful value before recommending an engagement.

---

# NO PRESSURE SALES

We do not believe every company needs a large AI project.

In some cases, the right recommendation may be:

- Start with training
- Fix basic processes first
- Improve tracking
- Document workflows
- Delay implementation
- Use an existing software solution
- Continue evaluating options

A good recommendation is more important than forcing a sale.
