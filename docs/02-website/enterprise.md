## The Enterprise AI Transformation Framework

### 1. Discover

Understand business priorities, workflows, technology, data, and existing AI activity.

### 2. Map

Create an enterprise portfolio of AI and automation opportunities.

### 3. Prioritize

Rank initiatives based on value, feasibility, risk, and strategic importance.

### 4. Pilot

Test high-potential opportunities with defined success criteria.

### 5. Implement

Build, integrate, configure, or coordinate the required solution.

### 6. Adopt

Train employees and embed the technology into real workflows.

### 7. Measure

Compare outcomes against defined baselines.

### 8. Scale

Expand successful systems across appropriate departments or business units.

### 9. Optimize

Continue discovering and improving opportunities as technology evolves.
