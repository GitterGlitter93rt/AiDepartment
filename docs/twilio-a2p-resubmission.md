This CUSTOMER_CARE campaign sends non-promotional customer-care messages to people who contact Your AI Department and expressly opt in to SMS. ## Message flow / how end users consent — paste into Twilio

End users may opt in on our public website at https://youraidepartment.ai/sms-consent/. The user enters a mobile number and must affirmatively check a separate SMS consent checkbox that is unchecked by default. The disclosure identifies Your AI Department as a business brand operated by Catastrophic Solutions LLC, describes the customer-care messages, states that message frequency varies and message/data rates may apply, provides STOP and HELP instructions, states that consent is not a condition of purchase, and links to the Privacy Policy and Terms. Providing a phone number or submitting another website form without selecting SMS consent does not enroll the user in SMS messaging. Privacy Policy: https://youraidepartment.ai/privacy/ Terms: https://youraidepartment.ai/terms/

We may also collect verbal consent during a live conversation with a person who has contacted us. Before any SMS is sent, our representative reads the approved verbal consent script below and records the person's affirmative agreement. We do not solicit SMS opt-in by sending an SMS to a person who has not already consented.
