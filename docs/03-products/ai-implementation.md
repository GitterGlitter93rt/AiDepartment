# WHEN WE MAY RECOMMEND NOT BUILDING

We may recommend against implementation when:

- The business case is weak
- The process changes too frequently
- Data is insufficient
- Existing software already solves the problem
- Human judgment dominates the workflow
- Implementation cost exceeds likely value
- The organization is not ready to adopt the system

Not building something can be the correct recommendation.

# FAQ

## Can you build custom AI agents?

Yes.

When a custom agent is the appropriate solution, we can help design and implement it.

---

## Can you integrate AI with our existing software?

Often, yes.

Integration depends on the software, available APIs, permissions, and technical environment.

---

## Do we need to replace our existing systems?

Not necessarily.

We prefer to evaluate existing systems before recommending replacement.

---

## Can you automate repetitive employee work?

Potentially.

We first analyze the workflow to determine which parts are suitable for automation or AI assistance.

---

## Can you build an AI phone agent?

Yes.

AI phone systems may be appropriate for answering, qualification, routing, scheduling, after-hours coverage, and other defined workflows.

---

## Can AI completely replace employees?

Some workflows can become highly automated, while others require significant human involvement.

We evaluate the actual work rather than making broad assumptions about job replacement.

---

## How long does implementation take?

It depends on scope.

A focused workflow may be implemented relatively quickly.

Complex multi-system projects may require significantly more planning, development, testing, and deployment.

---

## Do you support systems after launch?

Yes.

Ongoing support may be included in the project or provided through a Managed AI Department engagement.

---

## Do you guarantee ROI?

No.

We can estimate potential value and measure results, but business outcomes depend on many factors.
