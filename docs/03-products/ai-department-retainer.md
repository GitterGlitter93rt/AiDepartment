# FAQ

## Is this the same as hiring a fractional Chief AI Officer?

It can include similar strategic responsibilities, but the Managed AI Department may also coordinate implementation, training, optimization, automation, and other execution work depending on the engagement.

---

## Do we need to complete Executive AI Strategy first?

Not always.

However, companies with many opportunities may benefit from structured discovery before beginning a long-term relationship.

---

## Is implementation included?

Some recurring implementation and optimization may be included depending on scope.

Large or complex projects may require separate project pricing.

---

## Can you work with our internal IT or development team?

Yes.

The Managed AI Department can coordinate with internal teams and outside vendors.

---

## Can you manage our marketing too?

Potentially.

Google Ads, Meta Ads, SEO, CRM, automation, and growth systems may be included or separately scoped depending on the client relationship.

---

## Can you train our employees?

Yes.

Training and AI adoption support may be part of the relationship.

---

## Are we locked into specific AI software?

No.

Our goal is to recommend technology based on business fit rather than forcing every client onto one platform.

---

## Do you guarantee savings or revenue increases?

No.

We measure opportunities and results where possible, but business outcomes cannot be guaranteed.
