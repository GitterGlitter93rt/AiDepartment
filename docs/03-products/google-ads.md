# THE PROBLEM

Many businesses spend heavily on Google Ads without knowing which campaigns, keywords, calls, or leads actually turn into profitable customers.

Common problems include:

- Poor conversion tracking
- Weak keyword targeting
- Wasted search-term spend
- Missing negative keywords
- Generic landing pages
- Slow lead response
- Poor CRM attribution
- Untracked phone calls
- Inconsistent follow-up
- High cost per qualified lead
- Campaigns optimized for clicks instead of revenue

Google Ads should not be managed in isolation.

It should be connected to the full customer acquisition system.

# CONVERSION TRACKING

One of the biggest problems in paid advertising is incomplete attribution.

A business may know that it generated 100 leads.

But leadership often cannot answer:

Which campaign produced the lead?

Which keyword produced the call?

Did the lead become a customer?

How much revenue did that customer generate?

Which campaigns produce profitable work?

We can help connect advertising data to business outcomes.

Potential systems include:

- Google Ads conversion tracking
- GA4
- Call tracking
- Form tracking
- CRM attribution
- Offline conversion imports
- Customer-value tracking
- Executive dashboards
