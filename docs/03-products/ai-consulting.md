# EMPLOYEE CAPACITY

AI does not have to eliminate a job to create value.

Sometimes the larger opportunity is helping existing employees accomplish more.

For example:

If five employees each spend eight hours per week on repetitive administrative work, that represents approximately:

1,920 hours per year.

If better systems could address even part of that workload, the company may be able to:

- Handle more customers
- Reduce overtime
- Improve service
- Increase sales activity
- Delay future hiring
- Reassign employees to higher-value work

The correct value depends on the actual workflow and business.

---

# AI AND WORKFORCE PLANNING

Growing companies often respond to workload by hiring.

Sometimes that is the correct decision.

But before adding another position, leadership should also ask:

Why is the workload increasing?

Which tasks require human judgment?

Which tasks are repetitive?

Could existing software be configured better?

Could systems be integrated?

Could AI assist the employee?

Could automation eliminate unnecessary steps?

Could the company increase capacity without increasing overhead proportionally?

AI consulting can help answer those questions before the company commits to additional fixed costs.

# WHO IT MAY NOT BE FOR

AI consulting may not be appropriate when:

- Leadership has no interest in changing existing processes
- The company cannot provide access to relevant business information
- There is no meaningful business problem to solve
- The company expects AI to fix fundamentally broken operations without organizational change
- Leadership expects guaranteed financial results

Good AI implementation requires realistic expectations and participation from the business.

# FAQ

## What does an AI consultant actually do?

An AI consultant helps a company identify where AI and automation may create value, evaluate technology options, prioritize opportunities, and develop an implementation strategy.

Your AI Department can also help implement recommended systems.

---

## Do we need to know what AI solution we want?

No.

In many cases, determining what should be implemented is part of the engagement.

---

## Can you help us identify tasks that could be automated?

Yes.

Workflow and automation opportunity identification are major parts of our consulting process.

---

## Can you help us determine whether AI could reduce hiring needs?

We can analyze workloads and workflows to determine whether automation or AI could increase employee capacity.

That does not mean every role can or should be replaced.

---

## Can you work with our existing software?

Yes.

We prefer to evaluate existing systems before recommending unnecessary replacements.

---

## Can you build the solutions you recommend?

Yes.

When appropriate, implementation can be handled as a separate engagement.

---

## Do you work with companies that have never used AI?

Yes.

A company does not need prior AI experience.

---

## Do you guarantee ROI?

No.

We can develop business cases and estimate potential impact when sufficient data exists, but business outcomes cannot be guaranteed.
