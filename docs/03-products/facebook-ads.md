# THE PROBLEM WITH LEAD-ONLY MARKETING

A campaign may appear successful because it generates:

- Impressions
- Clicks
- Form submissions
- Low-cost leads

But those metrics do not tell leadership whether the advertising created profitable customers.

The questions that matter are:

- Are the leads qualified?
- How quickly are they contacted?
- How many appointments are scheduled?
- How many appointments show?
- How many leads become customers?
- What revenue do those customers generate?
- What is the actual customer acquisition cost?
- Which campaigns and creative generate the best customers?

That is where our approach begins.
