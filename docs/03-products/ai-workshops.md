# WHAT AN AI WORKSHOP IS

An AI workshop is a focused working session designed to help a business understand where AI can be used practically.

It is not a generic presentation about artificial intelligence.

The workshop is built around:

- Your business
- Your workflows
- Your employees
- Your technology
- Your customers
- Your growth goals

The objective is to identify useful opportunities and create practical next steps.

# WORKSHOP VS TRAINING

AI Training is focused primarily on helping employees use AI more effectively.

AI Workshops are more collaborative and diagnostic.

A workshop may combine:

- Education
- Workflow analysis
- Opportunity discovery
- Strategy
- Planning

---

# WORKSHOP VS EXECUTIVE AI STRATEGY

A workshop can be a focused standalone engagement.

The Executive AI Strategy engagement is deeper and may include:

- Broader company analysis
- Multiple departments
- Technology review
- ROI analysis
- Prioritized roadmap
- 90-day plan
- 12-month plan

A workshop may lead into that deeper engagement if appropriate.

# WORKSHOPS AS A SALES ENTRY POINT

For companies that are interested in AI but not ready for a larger implementation, a workshop can be a practical first step.

It allows leadership to:

- Evaluate fit
- Build internal alignment

without immediately committing to a large implementation project.
