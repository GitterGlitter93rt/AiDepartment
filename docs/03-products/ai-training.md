# WHY AI TRAINING MATTERS

Many employees already use tools such as:

- ChatGPT
- Claude
- Microsoft Copilot
- Google Gemini
- AI features inside existing software

But informal adoption creates problems.

Employees may:

- Use AI inconsistently
- Share sensitive information
- Trust inaccurate answers
- Waste time with weak prompts
- Ignore valuable use cases
- Create inconsistent work
- Avoid AI because they do not understand it

Structured training helps companies adopt AI more effectively and with less risk.
