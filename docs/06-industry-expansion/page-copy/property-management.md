## Positioning Section

Heading:
Property Management Is a Communication Business

Copy:
A large portion of property management work happens between systems, people, and recurring requests. Leasing inquiries arrive after hours. Maintenance issues need classification and routing. Residents need updates. Vendors need information. Managers need visibility.

AI and automation can help organize and coordinate those workflows without pretending every resident, maintenance, financial, or property decision should be delegated to software.

## Key Opportunity Areas

### Leasing Inquiry Response

AI-assisted workflows can acknowledge inquiries, answer approved property questions, collect basic information, route leads, and coordinate next steps.

### Showing Scheduling

Connect qualified inquiries with approved showing availability, confirmations, reminders, and rescheduling workflows.

### Maintenance Request Intake

Collect structured information about the request, property, unit, urgency, contact information, and relevant context before routing it into the appropriate maintenance process.

### Vendor Coordination

Automation can support assignment, notifications, scheduling, status collection, and internal handoffs.

### Resident Communication

Support routine confirmations, maintenance updates, appointment reminders, policy information, and common questions while preserving access to people.

### Owner and Management Reporting

AI can assist with summaries, portfolio reporting, task visibility, operational dashboards, and recurring administrative workflows where the underlying data is reliable.

### Internal Knowledge

Help employees find approved procedures, property information, SOPs, vendor processes, and internal documentation.

## Human Judgment

Lease decisions, legal questions, resident disputes, fair-housing-sensitive issues, emergencies, property-specific decisions, financial decisions, and other high-context situations require appropriate human oversight.

Your AI Department does not provide legal, housing, or property-management compliance advice.
