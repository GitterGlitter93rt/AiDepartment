## Key Opportunity Areas

### Internal Knowledge

Help employees locate approved procedures, product information, operational documentation, and internal resources.

### Document Workflows

AI-assisted extraction, classification, summarization, and routing can reduce manual administrative work where appropriate controls exist.

### Customer Service Assistance

Support service teams with information retrieval, conversation summaries, knowledge assistance, and workflow recommendations while preserving human handling where required.

### Operations

Automate repetitive data movement, task creation, workflow routing, status updates, and internal reporting.

### Marketing Operations

AI can support research, campaign operations, approved content workflows, reporting, and analysis while maintaining appropriate review.

### Employee Productivity

Provide controlled AI assistance for drafting, analysis, summarization, knowledge retrieval, and administrative tasks.

### Analytics and Management Reporting

AI can assist managers in interpreting operational information when the underlying data is reliable and appropriate for use.
