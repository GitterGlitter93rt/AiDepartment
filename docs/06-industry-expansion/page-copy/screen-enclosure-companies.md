## Positioning Section

Heading:
The Opportunity Is Often Lost Between the Lead and the Estimate

Copy:
A company can generate strong demand and still lose valuable projects because calls are missed, site visits are delayed, estimates are not followed up, customer questions are scattered across text messages, or marketing sources cannot be connected to signed work.

AI and automation can improve those handoffs without removing the contractor, estimator, or project manager from decisions that require real-world judgment.

## Key Opportunity Areas

### Lead Response

Acknowledge new inquiries, recover missed calls, route opportunities, and create consistent first-touch follow-up.

### Site-Visit Scheduling

Coordinate consultations, measurements, estimates, and follow-up appointments more efficiently.

### Estimate Follow-Up

Create structured reminders, salesperson tasks, SMS/email follow-up, and longer-term nurture for unsold proposals.

### Customer Communication

Support permit-status updates, scheduling notices, material updates, project reminders, and routine questions while keeping human escalation available.

### Old Lead Reactivation

Re-engage unsold estimates and older inquiries through appropriately permissioned campaigns.

### Marketing Attribution

Connect Google Ads, organic search, calls, forms, estimates, signed projects, and revenue.
