## Positioning Section

A New Market Is More Than a Sales Territory

When new fiber infrastructure becomes available, providers and sales organizations need more than canvassing activity. They need systems that can identify opportunity, capture interested households, follow up consistently, coordinate installations, track outcomes, and show management which territories and representatives are producing results.

## Key Opportunity Areas

### Door-to-Door Lead Capture

Field representatives can capture interested prospects into a structured system tied to representative, territory, address, campaign, and next action.

### Automated Follow-Up

Permissioned leads can receive structured SMS, email, voice, and representative follow-up without relying entirely on individual salespeople remembering every prospect.

### Qualification

Workflows can help organize service availability, location information, customer interest, existing service, scheduling needs, and other relevant qualification data.

### Installation Scheduling

Sales handoff, installation booking, reminders, rescheduling, and no-show recovery can be connected into the customer-acquisition workflow.

### Territory and Rep Attribution

Management should be able to understand performance by territory, salesperson, campaign, lead source, appointment, installation, and activation where the underlying systems make that data available.

### Customer Communication

Automation can support installation reminders, appointment updates, onboarding communication, service information, and routine questions while preserving human escalation.

### Sales Management

Dashboards, lead aging, follow-up status, appointment outcomes, rep activity, and conversion stages can improve visibility across field and centralized sales operations.
