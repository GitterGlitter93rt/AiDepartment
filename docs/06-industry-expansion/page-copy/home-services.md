## Core Opportunity Areas

### Lead Response

Acknowledge inbound inquiries, recover missed calls, route leads, and create consistent follow-up.

### Call Handling

AI-assisted intake can support common questions, data collection, scheduling, and routing where appropriate.

### Booking and Dispatch

Connect inquiry data with availability, field-service systems, internal notifications, reminders, and customer communication.

### Estimate Follow-Up

Create structured follow-up for higher-value quoted work.

### Marketing Attribution

Connect Google Ads, Meta Ads, organic search, calls, forms, appointments, jobs, and revenue.

### Database Reactivation

Identify appropriate past customers, old estimates, and dormant leads for permissioned re-engagement.

### Recruiting and Training

Support applicant intake, interview scheduling, onboarding, internal knowledge, and employee training while humans retain hiring decisions.

### Customer Retention

Support review requests, maintenance reminders, memberships, renewals, referrals, and post-service communication.

## Build vs Buy

The right solution may involve improving existing CRM or field-service software, adding AI-assisted communication, connecting advertising data, automating manual handoffs, building missing integrations, or creating custom workflows.

Do not assume every business needs to replace its technology stack.

## Human Judgment

Technical diagnosis, safety, pricing, permitting, field conditions, customer disputes, regulated work, and complex recommendations belong with qualified people.

## Who This Is For

Strong fit:
- established home-service operators
- multi-truck companies
- multi-location businesses
- private-equity-backed or rapidly growing platforms
- multi-trade organizations
- companies spending heavily on marketing
- operators with call centers, dispatchers, salespeople, or multiple departments

May not be the right fit:
- very small single-person operators
- companies with minimal lead volume
- businesses looking only for a low-cost chatbot
