## Key Opportunity Areas

### Customer Service

AI-assisted support can handle routine order questions, shipping-status requests, policy questions, and basic product information while escalating exceptions and sensitive cases.

### Lifecycle Marketing

Use customer behavior and approved marketing workflows to support welcome sequences, abandoned-cart recovery, post-purchase communication, replenishment, win-back, and reactivation.

### Merchandising

AI can assist product categorization, recommendations, collection organization, search experiences, product content, and merchandising analysis.

### Creative Operations

AI can support ad concepts, product copy, email content, image variations, campaign production, and testing workflows while preserving brand standards and factual accuracy.

### Returns and Support Triage

Collect structured information, route requests, identify common issues, and support customer-service teams.

### Marketing Attribution

Connect acquisition source, customer, order, repeat purchase, and revenue so paid-media decisions are informed by customer value rather than only front-end conversions.

### Operations and Internal Knowledge

AI can assist SOP access, reporting, inventory-related workflows, product-data enrichment, internal communication, and repetitive administrative work.

### Build vs Buy

Many e-commerce businesses already use Shopify, WooCommerce, Klaviyo, Gorgias, ERPs, analytics platforms, and advertising tools. The right architecture often connects and improves these systems rather than replacing them.

## Responsible AI

AI-generated product claims, images, pricing, availability, shipping information, and customer-facing policies should remain grounded in verified data.

AI should not invent product capabilities or materially misrepresent merchandise.
