## Positioning Section

Heading:
AI Should Improve the Hiring Process, Not Make the Hiring Decision

Copy:
The strongest recruiting use cases are often operational.

AI can help gather information, summarize candidate responses, schedule interviews, keep applicants informed, organize recruiting data, and reduce repetitive administrative work.

Hiring managers and qualified HR professionals should remain responsible for decisions that affect employment.

## Key Opportunity Areas

### Applicant Intake

Connect applications from career pages, recruiting systems, approved job platforms, referrals, and other sources into a structured process.

### Candidate Communication

Automatically acknowledge applications, explain next steps, request approved information, and keep candidates informed.

### Structured Screening

AI-assisted workflows can ask consistent, job-related questions and organize responses for human review.

Avoid collecting or using sensitive or protected information unless there is a legitimate and appropriate reason.

### AI-Assisted Phone Screening

Voice workflows can conduct approved initial screening conversations, capture responses, produce transcripts or summaries, and route the information to recruiters or hiring managers.

AI should not independently make final hiring or rejection decisions.

### Interview Scheduling

Coordinate phone screens, video interviews, in-person interviews, reminders, cancellations, and rescheduling.

### Recruiter and Hiring-Manager Handoff

Create structured candidate summaries, next-step tasks, interview notes, and status visibility.

### Onboarding

Support document checklists, training reminders, internal introductions, process guidance, and frequently asked questions.

### Employee Knowledge and HR Administration

AI can support access to approved internal policies, procedures, training information, benefits information, and repetitive HR administration where appropriate.

## ATS vs CRM

Heading:
Recruiting Usually Needs an ATS, Not Another Sales CRM

Copy:
An Applicant Tracking System is the recruiting equivalent of the system of record used to manage candidates and hiring stages.

Where an organization already has an ATS, Your AI Department can help improve the workflows around it.

Where no ATS exists, the appropriate solution may involve implementing one or building a lighter structured recruiting workflow depending on the scale and complexity of hiring.

## Human Decision Rule

AI can:

- gather information
- summarize responses
- schedule interviews
- send approved communications
- organize candidate records
- surface workflow tasks
- support recruiters

Humans should make:

- hiring decisions
- rejection decisions
- compensation decisions
- employment eligibility judgments
- accommodations decisions
- sensitive employment decisions
