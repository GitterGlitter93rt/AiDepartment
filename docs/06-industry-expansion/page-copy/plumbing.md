## Key Opportunity Areas

### Inbound Call Handling

AI-assisted intake can collect basic information, acknowledge the customer, classify the request, and route or schedule approved service types.

### Missed-Call Recovery

Create immediate recovery workflows for unanswered calls.

### Scheduling and Dispatch

Connect customer intake to appointment availability, dispatch workflows, reminders, and internal notifications.

### Estimate Follow-Up

Automate follow-up around larger replacements, repipes, water heaters, remodels, and other quoted work.

### Customer Communication

Support confirmations, technician-arrival updates, reminders, routine status messages, and post-service follow-up.

### Marketing Attribution

Connect Google Ads, calls, forms, booked jobs, completed jobs, and revenue.

### Review and Referral Workflows

Create controlled post-service requests and referral follow-up after appropriate customer milestones.
