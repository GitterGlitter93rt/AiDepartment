## Key Opportunity Areas

### Call Handling and Booking

AI-assisted phone and digital intake can support common questions, collect basic information, schedule approved appointment types, and route more complex situations to people.

### Missed-Call Recovery

Missed inquiries can trigger rapid follow-up instead of disappearing.

### Scheduling and Customer Reminders

Automate confirmations, reminders, access instructions, rescheduling workflows, and approved service communications.

### Retention and Renewal

Use customer data and workflow triggers to support renewal communication, service reminders, and retention outreach.

### Reactivation

Past customers and inactive accounts may be appropriate for controlled reactivation campaigns.

### Marketing Attribution

Connect source, call/form, booking, customer, recurring account, and revenue where systems allow.

### Internal Operations

Automation can support technician notifications, customer notes, internal handoffs, reporting, and management visibility.
