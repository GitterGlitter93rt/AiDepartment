# Where Energy Companies Should Start With AI

## Suggested Route

/resources/where-energy-companies-should-start-with-ai/

## Core Thesis

Energy organizations should start with controlled business workflows where AI can improve information access, employee productivity, document processing, reporting, and coordination without taking authority over safety-critical or regulated operations.

## Recommended Structure

### Candidate Areas

- internal knowledge
- document processing
- administrative operations
- field-support information
- project coordination
- vendor workflows
- reporting
- employee training
- customer communication

### Prioritization

Assess:

- business value
- process frequency
- manual workload
- data availability
- integration complexity
- safety consequence
- regulatory consequence
- human oversight

### Data and System Boundaries

Determine:

- what data is permitted
- where AI may run
- what systems it can access
- whether write access is needed
- who reviews outputs

### Start Narrow

Controlled use case
→ approved information
→ employee assistance
→ human review
→ measurement
→ expansion

## Human Judgment

Safety-critical operations, engineering decisions, system controls, emergency response, regulated actions, environmental decisions, and other high-consequence activities require qualified human oversight.
