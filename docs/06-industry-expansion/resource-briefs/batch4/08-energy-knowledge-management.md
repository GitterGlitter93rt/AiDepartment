# AI for Energy Knowledge Management and Internal Operations

## Suggested Route

/resources/ai-for-energy-knowledge-management-and-internal-operations/

## Core Thesis

Energy organizations often hold critical operational knowledge across manuals, policies, procedures, training materials, project files, and experienced employees.

AI-assisted knowledge systems can improve access to approved information while respecting permissions and source authority.

## Recommended Structure

### Knowledge Challenges

- information spread across repositories
- experienced employees hold undocumented knowledge
- slow internal search
- inconsistent onboarding
- difficulty finding current procedures
- different teams use different sources

### Retrieval Architecture

Approved sources
→ permissions
→ retrieval
→ AI response
→ citations/source visibility
→ employee review

### Appropriate Use

AI can assist with:

- finding procedures
- locating documents
- summarizing approved material
- comparing internal references
- onboarding support
- training support
- drafting internal documentation

### Guardrails

- respect access controls
- prefer current approved source material
- show source context where possible
- do not allow general model knowledge to override authoritative internal procedure
- route uncertainty to people
