## Editorial Rules

Every resource should:

- answer a real executive or operational question
- focus on business process and implementation, not generic AI hype
- explain data, governance, access, and human responsibility where relevant
- avoid unsupported statistics, ROI claims, fake case studies, and fake customers
- avoid regulated professional advice
- distinguish AI assistance from consequential human decisions
- use descriptive internal links
- avoid generic "Learn More" anchors
- avoid thin or mechanically templated copy
- remain specific to the operating environment of the industry
