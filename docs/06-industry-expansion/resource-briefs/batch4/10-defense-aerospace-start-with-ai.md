# Where Defense and Aerospace Companies Should Start With Enterprise AI

## Suggested Route

/resources/where-defense-and-aerospace-companies-should-start-with-enterprise-ai/

## Core Thesis

Defense and aerospace organizations should begin with approved business and knowledge workflows where security boundaries, data permissions, human accountability, and organizational value can be clearly defined.

Do not begin with the highest-consequence technical systems.

## Recommended Structure

### Appropriate Starting Areas

Potential lower-risk enterprise areas:

- internal knowledge
- employee training
- document administration
- supplier/project administration
- approved reporting
- internal research assistance
- repetitive business workflows
- enterprise productivity

### Data Classification

Before implementation, determine:

- whether information is public
- internal
- proprietary
- export-controlled
- classified
- contract-restricted
- otherwise sensitive

Do not provide legal classification guidance.

The organization must determine applicable controls.

### System Boundary

Define:

- approved model
- approved hosting/deployment
- approved data
- authorized users
- permissions
- output handling

### Human Review

Require humans before consequential actions.

### Start With a Controlled Pilot

Business workflow
→ approved data
→ defined users
→ governed AI
→ human review
→ measurement
→ controlled expansion

## Explicit Boundaries

Do not position services around:

- weapons targeting
- autonomous weapons
- offensive cyber operations
- harmful surveillance
- bypassing security controls
- other harmful capabilities
