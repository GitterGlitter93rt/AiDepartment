# AI Phone Handling and Missed-Call Recovery for Garage Door Companies

## Suggested Route

/resources/ai-phone-handling-for-garage-door-companies/

## Core Thesis

Garage door service is often high-intent and time-sensitive.

AI-assisted answering and missed-call recovery can help capture opportunities quickly while technicians remain responsible for diagnosis and repair decisions.

## Recommended Structure

### Intake

Collect:

- customer information
- address
- general issue
- door/opener category
- preferred timing
- emergency status

### Workflow

Call
→ intake
→ approved scheduling/routing
→ confirmation
→ technician

### Missed Calls

Missed call
→ customer acknowledgment
→ callback workflow
→ dispatch notification

### Human Escalation

- safety issues
- trapped vehicle/person
- structural problems
- diagnosis
- pricing
- unusual repair conditions
