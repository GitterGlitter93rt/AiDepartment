## Core Thesis

Larger plumbing opportunities often require more than one customer conversation.

Structured follow-up can support water heaters, repipes, replacements, remodels, upgrades, and other quoted work.

### Relevant Estimate Types

- repiping

- remodels

- larger replacements

### Workflow

Estimate sent
→ confirmation
→ task
→ follow-up
→ customer response
→ human conversation
→ booked work / nurture

### AI Assistance

- summarize job notes
- draft messages
- create follow-up reminders
- prioritize stale estimates
- maintain CRM status

### Human Responsibility

Pricing and technical recommendations remain with qualified people.
