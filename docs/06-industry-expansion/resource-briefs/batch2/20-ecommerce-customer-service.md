# AI Customer Service for E-commerce: What Should Be Automated?

## Suggested Route

/resources/ai-customer-service-for-ecommerce/

## Core Thesis

AI can handle many repetitive e-commerce support interactions, but the best system distinguishes routine information from exceptions requiring human judgment.

## Recommended Structure

### Good Candidates for Automation

Examples:

- order status
- shipment tracking
- common product questions
- approved policy information
- basic return-process guidance
- common account questions

### Human Escalation

Examples:

- damaged/lost orders
- angry customers
- unusual refunds
- fraud concerns
- complex product problems
- high-value customers
- policy exceptions

### Data Connection

The system should retrieve verified:

- order information
- product data
- shipping status
- policies
- customer history where appropriate

### Never Invent

AI should not fabricate:

- shipment status
- refund approval
- inventory
- delivery dates
- product capabilities
- policies
