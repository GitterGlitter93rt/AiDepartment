## Editorial Rules

Every resource should:

- solve a real business problem
- be useful even if the reader never hires Your AI Department
- start from workflow and business value, not AI hype
- include implementation realities
- explain where humans remain necessary
- avoid unsupported statistics, ROI claims, case studies, and fake results
- use descriptive internal links
- avoid generic "Learn More" anchor text
- link contextually to its primary industry page
- link to relevant service pages where appropriate
- avoid keyword stuffing and thin SEO copy
