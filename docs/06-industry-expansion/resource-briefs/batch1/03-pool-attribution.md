## Search Intent

How does a pool company connect Google Ads spend to estimates, signed projects, and revenue?

## Core Thesis

Lead counts alone do not tell management whether advertising is producing valuable customers.

The real system should connect:

campaign
→ lead
→ conversation
→ appointment
→ estimate
→ signed project
→ revenue

## Recommended Structure

### Why Lead Count Is Not Enough

Discuss differences between:

- raw form lead
- qualified inquiry
- design consultation
- estimate
- signed project

### Capture Source Correctly

Potential systems:

- website tracking
- call tracking
- UTM parameters
- CRM
- offline conversion data

### Maintain Attribution Through the Sales Process

The source should not disappear after a lead is handed to sales.

### Closed-Loop Reporting

Example management questions:

- which campaigns create estimates?
- which create signed projects?
- which markets produce higher-value work?
- which keywords produce poor-quality inquiries?

### Operational Requirement

Explain that attribution requires consistent CRM/process usage, not just another analytics dashboard.
