# How Brokerages Can Track Lead Sources to Closings

## Suggested Route

/resources/how-brokerages-can-track-lead-sources-to-closings/

## Core Thesis

Real estate organizations frequently know where leads originate but lose attribution as the relationship moves through agents, appointments, transactions, and long sales cycles.

The goal is to connect:

source
→ lead
→ appointment
→ opportunity
→ transaction
→ closing

## Recommended Structure

### Capture Source

Examples:

- portal
- Google Ads
- Meta
- organic
- referral
- open house
- website
- agent-generated

### Preserve Source Through CRM

Do not overwrite or lose attribution during assignment.

### Track Meaningful Milestones

- qualified lead
- appointment
- showing
- listing meeting
- active opportunity
- contract
- closing

### Use Reporting for Better Decisions

Understand:

- sources producing appointments
- sources producing closings
- agent/team performance
- cost of acquisition
- long-cycle lead value

Avoid unsupported revenue or ROI claims.
