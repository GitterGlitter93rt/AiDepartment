## Purpose

These resources should:

- answer real business questions
- support topical authority
- internally link to relevant commercial/industry pages
- strengthen Google Ads landing-page credibility
- avoid thin SEO content
- avoid keyword stuffing
- avoid fake statistics, ROI claims, customers, testimonials, or case studies
- preserve Your AI Department's business-transformation positioning

## Editorial Standard

Every article should:

- start from the business problem
- explain the workflow
- identify where AI/automation can help
- explain where humans still matter
- discuss implementation realities
- give practical next steps
- link contextually to relevant services/industries
- use descriptive internal anchor text
- avoid generic "Learn More" anchors
