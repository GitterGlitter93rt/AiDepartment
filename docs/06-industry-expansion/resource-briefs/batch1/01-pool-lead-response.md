## Search Intent

A pool company wants to improve response time and stop losing leads after calls, forms, or advertising inquiries.

## Core Thesis

The problem is not simply generating more leads.

Pool companies often lose opportunities because:

- calls are missed
- forms sit unanswered
- leads are assigned slowly
- salespeople follow up inconsistently
- nobody knows whether follow-up happened
- the customer waits too long for a response

AI and automation can improve the system without removing human sales judgment.

## Recommended Structure

### Why Speed Matters

Explain that high-intent prospects often contact multiple contractors and that operational delay can waste paid demand.

Do not invent industry response-time statistics.

### Where Leads Enter

Examples:

- Google Ads
- organic search
- referrals
- website forms
- phone calls
- social media
- home shows
- repeat customers

### Immediate Acknowledgment

Explain:

- confirmation SMS/email
- lead routing
- sales-team notification
- appointment scheduling options
- missed-call recovery

### AI-Assisted Qualification

Possible questions:

- project type
- location
- timing
- new build vs. renovation
- service vs. construction
- desired consultation
- relevant contact information

Do not imply AI should produce construction pricing or site-specific design advice without appropriate human review.

### Human Handoff

Sales/design staff should handle:

- project scope
- site conditions
- design
- pricing
- financing discussions
- negotiation
- complex customer questions

### Measurement

Track:

lead source
→ response
→ appointment
→ estimate
→ signed project
