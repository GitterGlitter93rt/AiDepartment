## Suggested Route

## Core Thesis

The customer-acquisition process does not end when someone agrees to service.

Scheduling, reminders, rescheduling, and installation handoff directly affect the customer experience.

## Recommended Structure

### Sales-to-Install Handoff

Customer
→ service availability
→ installation appointment
→ confirmation

### Reminders

- appointment confirmation
- advance reminder
- arrival expectations
- preparation requirements

### No-Show / Reschedule Recovery

Automated workflow should:

- identify missed appointment
- contact customer
- offer approved rescheduling path
- notify internal team
- update system

### Human Escalation

Complex property/install issues go to people.
