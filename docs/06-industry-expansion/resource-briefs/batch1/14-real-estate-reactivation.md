# How Real Estate Teams Can Reactivate Old Leads

## Suggested Route

/resources/how-real-estate-teams-can-reactivate-old-leads/

## Core Thesis

Brokerages and teams often have years of contacts that are poorly segmented and inconsistently followed up.

AI and automation can help organize and prioritize re-engagement without treating every old record as permission for unrestricted outreach.

## Recommended Structure

Potential audiences:

- past buyer inquiries
- seller prospects
- prior open-house attendees
- old website leads
- past clients
- long-term nurture contacts

Workflow:

database
→ segmentation
→ eligibility/permission check
→ personalized outreach
→ response
→ human agent
→ appointment

### AI Assistance

- summarize historical notes
- categorize contacts
- draft outreach
- identify likely next action
- create agent tasks
- maintain nurture

### Guardrails

Respect:

- consent
- opt-outs
- brokerage policies
- fair housing
- professional judgment
