# AI Follow-Up for Solar Leads

## Suggested Route

## Core Thesis

High lead volume creates little value if the follow-up process is inconsistent.

AI can support structured lead response, scheduling, reminders, and nurture while communications remain permissioned and humans retain sales judgment.

## Recommended Structure

### Sources

- paid media

- website
- events

- partner leads

- confirmation

- notifications

### Multi-Step Follow-Up

- SMS
- email
- phone
- salesperson task
- longer-term nurture

### Human Role

Humans handle:

- customer-specific economics
- financing
- system recommendations
- objections
- closing
- consumer questions requiring judgment

### Compliance Guardrail

Explicitly say automation should respect:

- consent
- opt-outs
- applicable telemarketing rules
- company policy

No mass unsolicited outreach positioning.
