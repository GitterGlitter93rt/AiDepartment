# AI for Door-to-Door Solar Sales Follow-Up

## Suggested Route

/resources/ai-for-door-to-door-solar-sales-follow-up/

## Core Thesis

The value of field canvassing drops when interested homeowners are captured inconsistently or never receive structured follow-up.

The opportunity is to connect field activity to a real system.

## Recommended Structure

### Capture the Interaction

Potential data:

- homeowner/contact
- address
- representative
- territory
- interest level
- requested next step
- permissioned communication preference

### Trigger the Next Step

Lead captured
→ confirmation
→ rep notification
→ appointment scheduling
→ reminders
→ follow-up
→ outcome

### Rep Accountability

Track:

- leads created
- appointments
- follow-up
- outcomes
- territory

### AI Assistance

AI may:

- summarize field notes
- generate contextual follow-up
- assist scheduling
- prioritize neglected leads
- support managers

Do not automate regulated or high-stakes sales representations.
