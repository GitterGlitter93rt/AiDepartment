## Core Thesis

Emergency restoration inquiries often happen outside normal business hours.

AI-assisted intake can capture structured information and route urgent inquiries faster, but it should not replace qualified human or field judgment.

## Recommended Structure

### Why Emergency Intake Is Different

Discuss:

- urgency
- distressed customer
- location
- type of loss
- active damage
- service availability
- safety concerns
- handoff quality

### What AI Can Capture

Examples:

- caller identity
- property address
- callback information
- general loss type
- time of event
- basic routing information
- insurance/carrier information where appropriate

Do not have AI interpret coverage or provide adjusting advice.

### Routing

Possible routing dimensions:

- market
- service type
- geography
- after-hours team
- estimator
- technician
- emergency escalation

### Human Escalation

Immediate human involvement where:

- safety concerns
- major loss
- unusual circumstances
- coverage questions
- complex property conditions
- distressed customer needs more support
