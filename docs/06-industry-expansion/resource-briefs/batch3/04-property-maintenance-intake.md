## Search Intent

How can AI help property managers handle maintenance requests?

## Core Thesis

Maintenance automation works best when AI organizes information and coordinates workflow rather than attempting to diagnose every property problem.

## Recommended Structure

### Maintenance Intake Problem

Requests may arrive through:

- phone calls
- email
- resident portal
- SMS
- forms
- after-hours channels

Incomplete information creates extra back-and-forth.

### Information AI Can Help Capture

Examples:

- resident/contact information
- property
- unit
- general issue category
- description
- when the issue began
- photos where the approved system supports them
- access information
- preferred contact method

### Triage and Routing

Possible categories:

- routine maintenance
- vendor-required issue
- internal maintenance
- property-manager review
- possible emergency escalation

Do not position AI as making safety-critical determinations without appropriate safeguards and human escalation.

### Vendor / Team Handoff

Request
→ structured intake
→ routing
→ assignment
→ scheduling
→ resident update
→ completion status

### Human Escalation

People should handle:

- emergencies
- safety issues
- disputes
- unclear conditions
- sensitive resident situations
- unusual property damage
- decisions with financial/legal implications
