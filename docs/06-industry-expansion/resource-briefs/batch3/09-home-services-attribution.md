# How Home Service Companies Can Track Marketing Leads to Revenue

## Suggested Route

/resources/how-home-service-companies-can-track-marketing-leads-to-revenue/

## Core Thesis

Home-service marketing should be measured beyond clicks, calls, and form fills.

The meaningful business path is:

marketing source
→ lead
→ booking
→ estimate or service call
→ completed job
→ revenue

## Recommended Structure

### Capture the Original Source

Examples:

- Google Ads
- Local Services Ads where applicable
- Meta Ads
- organic search
- referrals
- direct
- other campaigns

### Preserve Attribution

Do not lose the original marketing source when the lead moves into:

- CRM
- field-service software
- call center
- dispatch
- estimator
- salesperson

### Track Business Milestones

Depending on the business:

lead
→ booked appointment
→ dispatched call
→ estimate
→ sold job
→ completed job
→ revenue

### Closed-Loop Advertising

Where appropriate and technically supported, conversion outcomes can be returned to advertising systems to improve optimization.

### Management Questions

- which campaigns create booked work?
- which campaigns create high-value estimates?
- which sources create completed jobs?
- which keywords generate poor-quality calls?
- which markets produce stronger customers?
- where does the sales process lose good leads?

### Attribution Reality

Explain that attribution may be imperfect because:

- customers use multiple devices
- multiple marketing interactions occur
- phone calls and offline sales are involved
- privacy restrictions limit some tracking

Do not pretend every dollar can always be traced perfectly.
