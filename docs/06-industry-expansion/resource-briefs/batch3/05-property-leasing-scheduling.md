# Automating Leasing Inquiry Response and Showing Scheduling

## Suggested Route

/resources/automating-leasing-inquiry-response-and-showing-scheduling/

## Core Thesis

Leasing inquiries can arrive at any time, while leasing teams operate with limited availability.

AI-assisted workflows can improve response, approved information delivery, qualification, and scheduling while sensitive housing decisions remain human-controlled.

## Recommended Structure

### Inquiry Sources

- property website
- listing portals
- calls
- email
- advertising
- referral sources

### Immediate Response

A workflow can:

- acknowledge inquiry
- collect contact information
- identify property/unit interest
- provide approved factual information
- identify scheduling preference
- offer approved showing availability

### Showing Scheduling

Inquiry
→ approved information
→ scheduling
→ confirmation
→ reminder
→ showing
→ human follow-up

### AI Guardrails

AI should use verified property data for:

- availability
- amenities
- pricing
- policies
- location
- showing instructions

Do not invent availability, pricing, property features, concessions, or policies.

### Human Responsibility

Sensitive or consequential issues should be handled by people, including:

- application decisions
- exceptions
- disputes
- fair-housing-sensitive situations
- legal questions
- unusual accommodation requests
