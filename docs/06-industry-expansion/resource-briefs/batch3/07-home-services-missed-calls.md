## Core Thesis

Home-service companies can spend heavily generating phone calls and still lose opportunities when staff cannot answer every call.

AI-assisted answering and missed-call recovery can create a structured response without replacing technicians, dispatchers, or qualified salespeople.

## Recommended Structure

### Why Calls Get Missed

Examples:

- after-hours demand
- call spikes
- dispatchers already on calls
- field teams unavailable
- storms
- seasonal demand
- lunch/shift changes

### Immediate Recovery Workflow

Missed call
→ automated acknowledgment
→ basic information collection
→ callback or approved booking
→ internal notification
→ human follow-up

### AI-Assisted Answering

Possible tasks:

- customer/contact collection
- general service category
- property location
- preferred appointment
- routine approved information
- routing

### Human Escalation

People should handle:

- technical diagnosis
- safety
- emergencies
- pricing exceptions
- unusual customer situations
- regulated work
- complaints

### Measurement

Track:

missed call
→ recovered conversation
→ booking
→ job
→ revenue where available

Avoid unsupported recovery-rate claims.
