# How Home Service Companies Can Automate Estimate Follow-Up

## Suggested Route

/resources/how-home-service-companies-can-automate-estimate-follow-up/

## Core Thesis

Many home-service companies generate significant quoted work but lack a consistent process for following up after the estimate.

Automation can keep opportunities visible while technicians, estimators, and salespeople remain responsible for pricing, recommendations, and customer conversations.

## Recommended Structure

### Common Estimate Categories

Examples:

- HVAC replacement
- plumbing replacement
- electrical upgrades
- roofing
- pools
- outdoor living
- garage doors
- generators
- restoration-related reconstruction
- other higher-value projects

### Workflow

Estimate sent
→ confirmation
→ salesperson task
→ scheduled follow-up
→ customer response
→ human conversation
→ sold / nurture / closed-lost

### AI Assistance

AI can:

- summarize job history
- draft contextual follow-up
- surface stale estimates
- organize reasons for delay
- create tasks
- maintain follow-up cadence

### Segmentation

Differentiate:

- newly quoted
- engaged
- needs financing/timing
- waiting on another decision
- unresponsive
- future project
- closed-lost

### Human Role

Humans handle:

- pricing
- diagnosis
- design
- technical questions
- negotiation
- customer objections
- scope changes
