## Editorial Rules

Every resource should:

- answer a real business question
- focus on workflow and business value rather than AI hype
- distinguish AI assistance from human responsibility
- discuss implementation realities
- avoid unsupported statistics, ROI claims, fake clients, fake case studies, and fake results
- use descriptive internal-link anchor text
- avoid generic "Learn More" links
- avoid giving legal, employment, housing, or compliance advice
- avoid positioning AI as a replacement for required professional judgment
