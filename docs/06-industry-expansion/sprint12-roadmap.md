## SEO / Content Rule

Every page must be genuinely industry-specific.

Avoid generic "AI for X" pages with swapped nouns.

Each page should address:
- actual business workflows
- lead/customer journey
- AI opportunities
- automation opportunities
- systems/integrations
- implementation realities
- human judgment
- governance/risk where appropriate
- measurement/attribution
- relevant Resources
- commercial CTA

No fake:
- clients
- logos
- case studies
- ROI
- statistics
- testimonials

## Internal Link SEO Rule

Avoid generic CTA text such as:
- Learn More
- Read More
- Click Here

Prefer descriptive anchors such as:
- Explore AI for Pool Companies
- Explore AI for Automotive Dealer Groups
- Explore AI for Real Estate Teams
- Explore AI for Solar Companies

Run a site-wide descriptive-anchor cleanup during Sprint 12.
