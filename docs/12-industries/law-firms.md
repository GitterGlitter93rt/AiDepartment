# FAQ

## Can AI replace attorneys?

That is not the objective of our work.

AI may assist with appropriate research, administrative, communication, document, and workflow tasks, but professional judgment remains critical.

---

## Can AI answer calls for a law firm?

Potentially.

AI-assisted phone systems can handle defined intake, routing, scheduling, and information-collection workflows when implemented appropriately.

---

## Can AI qualify legal leads?

AI may assist with collecting structured information and applying criteria established by the firm.

Final legal or case evaluation should remain with qualified personnel.

---

## Can AI work with confidential legal documents?

Some systems may support secure document workflows, but confidentiality, privilege, vendor terms, security, data retention, and professional obligations must be evaluated before implementation.

---

## Can you help with Google Ads for law firms?

Yes.

Google Ads may be provided independently or as part of a broader AI Growth System.

---

## Can you help our employees learn to use AI?

Yes.

AI training and workshops can be customized for leadership, attorneys, intake teams, marketing teams, and administrative employees.
