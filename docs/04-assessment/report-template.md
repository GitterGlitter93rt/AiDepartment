# Purpose

This document defines the customer-facing report generated after someone completes the AI Department Assessment.

The report should:

- Deliver immediate value
- Explain the company's AI readiness
- Identify specific business opportunities
- Quantify opportunities where sufficient data exists
- Prioritize recommendations
- Create curiosity about deeper opportunities
- Encourage qualified prospects to schedule a strategy call

The report should feel like a business diagnostic, not a generic quiz result.

# Report Integrity Rules

1. Never invent financial savings.

2. Never guarantee revenue increases.

3. Clearly distinguish estimates from known facts.

4. Use ranges when uncertainty is significant.

5. Explain assumptions behind ROI calculations.

6. Do not recommend unnecessary services.

7. Give credit for areas where the company performs well.

8. Separate customer-facing scoring from internal sales qualification.

9. Keep the initial report understandable to a business owner.

10. Use the deeper strategy engagement for complex implementation planning.
