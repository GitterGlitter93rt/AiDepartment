Status: Draft
Version: 1.0

# Assessment Design

Target completion time:

7-10 minutes.

Target question count:

40 questions.

Questions should primarily use:

- Multiple choice
- Yes / No
- Select all that apply
- Rating scales

Free-response questions should be limited.

Each answer may affect:

- Public readiness scores
- Internal Commercial Opportunity Score
- Recommended services
- Sales priority
- Follow-up messaging

# Results Page

After completion, show:

- Overall AI Department Score
- Category Scores
- Three strongest areas
- Three biggest opportunities
- Recommended next steps
- Estimated opportunity categories
- CTA to schedule a strategy call

Do not automatically display aggressive pricing.
