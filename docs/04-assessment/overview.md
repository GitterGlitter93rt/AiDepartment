\# The AI Department Assessment



Status: Draft



Version: 1.0



\---



\# Purpose



The AI Department Assessment is the primary diagnostic and lead-generation system for Your AI Department.



Its purpose is to:



\- Help business owners understand where AI can create value

\- Identify operational inefficiencies

\- Identify revenue growth opportunities

\- Identify marketing and sales weaknesses

\- Identify employee training opportunities

\- Identify automation opportunities

\- Identify technology and integration gaps

\- Qualify prospects for consulting and implementation services

\- Recommend appropriate next steps



The assessment must provide real value even if the prospect never becomes a client.



\---



\# Assessment Structure



The public assessment should take approximately 7-10 minutes.



Target length:



35-45 questions.



The assessment is divided into the following categories:



1\. Company Profile

2\. Leadership and AI Strategy

3\. Marketing and Lead Generation

4\. Sales and Follow-Up

5\. Customer Service

6\. Operations and Automation

7\. Employees and Training

8\. Technology and Data

9\. Growth Priorities



\---



\# Public Scores



The prospect may receive the following scores:



\## Overall AI Department Score



0-100



Measures the company's overall readiness and opportunity for practical AI adoption.



\## Leadership and Strategy Score



Measures leadership alignment, urgency, ownership, planning, and understanding of AI.



\## Marketing and Growth Score



Measures lead generation, advertising, attribution, website performance, and growth systems.



\## Sales and Follow-Up Score



Measures response time, CRM usage, automation, pipeline discipline, and lead conversion processes.



\## Customer Experience Score



Measures phone handling, responsiveness, service consistency, scheduling, and customer communication.



\## Operations and Automation Score



Measures repetitive work, documentation, workflow efficiency, process consistency, and automation opportunity.



\## Employee AI Readiness Score



Measures employee adoption, training, policies, usage, and comfort with AI tools.



\## Technology and Data Score



Measures software quality, integrations, reporting, data access, security, and readiness for AI implementation.



\---



\# Internal Commercial Opportunity Score



This score is not necessarily shown to the prospect.



It is used internally to prioritize sales follow-up.



Factors may include:



\- Annual revenue

\- Number of employees

\- Number of locations

\- Monthly advertising spend

\- Monthly lead volume

\- Current software complexity

\- Amount of manual work

\- Existing AI adoption

\- Urgency

\- Decision-maker status

\- Budget willingness

\- Growth goals

\- Implementation complexity

\- Number of identified opportunities



Score range:



0-100



Suggested classifications:



0-39: Low Priority



40-59: Nurture



60-79: Qualified Opportunity



80-100: High Priority Executive Lead

\---



\# Sales Philosophy



The assessment must diagnose honestly.



Recommendations should never be manufactured purely to sell services.



If a business is not ready for a major implementation, the assessment should say so.



Credibility is more important than forcing an upsell.



The goal is to identify the highest-value and most realistic next step for each company.
