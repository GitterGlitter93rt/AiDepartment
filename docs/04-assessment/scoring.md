# AI Department Assessment Scoring

Status: Draft
Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

The scoring system should produce two different outputs:

1. Public AI Department Score
2. Internal Commercial Opportunity Score

The public score helps the prospect understand their AI readiness and business opportunities.

The internal score helps Your AI Department prioritize sales follow-up and identify high-value opportunities.

---

# Public AI Department Score

Score range:

0-100

The score should be based on seven weighted categories.

## Category Weights

Leadership and AI Strategy: 15%

Marketing and Growth: 15%

Sales and Follow-Up: 15%

Customer Experience: 10%

Operations and Automation: 20%

Employee AI Readiness: 10%

Technology and Data: 15%

Total: 100%

---

# Category Scoring Method

Each scored answer should receive a value between 0 and 4.

Suggested framework:

0 = Major weakness or no capability

1 = Early stage

2 = Developing

3 = Strong

4 = Advanced

Each category score is calculated as:

Total points earned
divided by
Total possible points

Then convert to a 0-100 score.

Example:

Marketing questions total possible points: 20

Company earns: 11

Marketing Score:

55 / 100

---

# Public Score Interpretation

## 0-24

AI Foundation Stage

Description:

The company has significant opportunities to improve basic processes, technology, reporting, and AI readiness before attempting advanced AI implementation.

Recommended focus:

- Business process review
- Executive AI education
- Technology cleanup
- SOP development
- Basic automation
- AI roadmap

---

## 25-49

AI Opportunity Stage

Description:

The company has meaningful opportunities for AI adoption but lacks consistent strategy, processes, or implementation.

Recommended focus:

- AI Department Assessment
- Executive AI Strategy
- Workflow automation
- Employee training
- Marketing and sales improvements

---

## 50-69

AI Adoption Stage

Description:

The company has begun adopting modern technology and AI but still has significant opportunities to improve productivity, automation, integration, and growth.

Recommended focus:

- AI implementation
- Department-specific automation
- AI agents
- Employee training
- Reporting and integrations
- AI Growth Systems

---

## 70-84

AI Scaling Stage

Description:

The company has strong technology and operational foundations and is ready to scale AI across multiple departments.

Recommended focus:

- Advanced automation
- Custom AI agents
- Department-wide integrations
- Executive dashboards
- Managed AI Department
- Ongoing optimization

---

## 85-100

AI Leadership Stage

Description:

The company has advanced systems, strong AI adoption, and mature processes.

Recommended focus:

- Advanced AI strategy
- Custom AI systems
- Competitive differentiation
- Advanced analytics
- AI governance
- Continuous optimization

---

# Internal Commercial Opportunity Score

Score range:

0-100

This score should never rely on only one factor.

It should estimate:

- Potential project value
- Likelihood of buying
- Urgency
- Ability to pay
- Number of opportunities
- Strategic fit

---

# Commercial Opportunity Categories

## Company Size and Financial Capacity

Maximum: 20 points

Annual revenue:

Under $500,000 = 1

$500,000-$1 million = 3

$1-$3 million = 6

$3-$10 million = 10

$10-$25 million = 14

$25-$50 million = 17

$50 million+ = 20

Employee count may be used as a secondary modifier.

---

# Advertising Opportunity

Maximum: 15 points

Monthly paid advertising spend:

$0 = 0

Under $2,500 = 2

$2,500-$5,000 = 4

$5,000-$10,000 = 7

$10,000-$25,000 = 10

$25,000-$50,000 = 12

$50,000+ = 15

Add opportunity emphasis when:

- Attribution is weak
- Conversion tracking is weak
- Lead response is slow
- Website conversion is poor

---

# Labor Automation Opportunity

Maximum: 20 points

Score based on:

- Administrative workload
- Number of support employees
- Repetitive work
- Manual data movement
- Overtime
- Planned hiring
- Accounting workload
- Sales administrative work

Suggested scoring:

Low manual workload = 2-5

Moderate workload = 6-10

High workload = 11-15

Very high workload with measurable labor cost = 16-20

---

# Sales Opportunity

Maximum: 15 points

Factors:

- Lead volume
- Slow response time
- Poor CRM usage
- Weak follow-up
- Manual sales administration
- No reactivation process
- Unknown conversion rate

Examples:

Low lead volume and mature process = 2-4

Moderate lead volume with weaknesses = 5-9

High lead volume with poor follow-up = 10-15

---

# AI Readiness and Urgency

Maximum: 10 points

Factors:

- Leadership urgency
- Lack of AI strategy
- Active interest in implementation
- Willingness to move quickly
- Existing AI experimentation

Suggested scoring:

Research only = 1-2

Interested but low urgency = 3-4

Important within 3-6 months = 5-6

Very important within 1-3 months = 7-8

Mission critical / immediate = 9-10

---

# Buying Authority

Maximum: 10 points

Owner / Founder = 10

CEO / President = 10

Executive = 8

Partner = 8

Department Leader = 6

Marketing / Operations Leader = 5

IT / Technology = 5

Employee = 2

Consultant / Advisor = 2

Other = 1-5 depending on context

---

# Budget Willingness

Maximum: 10 points

Under $2,500 = 1

$2,500-$5,000 = 2

$5,000-$10,000 = 4

$10,000-$25,000 = 6

$25,000-$50,000 = 8

$50,000-$100,000 = 9

$100,000+ = 10

Depends entirely on ROI = 7

# Opportunity Flags

In addition to scores, the system should create opportunity flags.

Examples:

MARKETING_HIGH_VALUE

Trigger when:

- Paid ad spend is significant
- Tracking is poor
- Marketing satisfaction is low

---

SALES_AUTOMATION

Trigger when:

- Lead response is slow
- Follow-up is inconsistent
- CRM adoption is weak
- Lead volume is meaningful

---

AI_PHONE_AGENT

Trigger when:

- Missed calls are significant
- After-hours calls go to voicemail
- Lead value is high
- Call volume is meaningful

---

FINANCE_AUTOMATION

Trigger when:

- Finance work is highly manual
- Internal staff spend significant time on repetitive tasks
- Reporting is slow
- Collections are manual

---

EMPLOYEE_PRODUCTIVITY

Trigger when:

- Administrative workload is high
- Employees spend significant time searching for information
- Repetitive communication is common

---

AI_TRAINING

Trigger when:

- Employee AI adoption is low
- No formal training exists
- Leadership is concerned about improper AI usage

---

INTEGRATION_OPPORTUNITY

Trigger when:

- Software systems are disconnected
- Employees manually transfer data
- Reporting is inconsistent

---

EXECUTIVE_STRATEGY

Trigger when:

- No AI roadmap exists
- Leadership considers AI important
- Company has meaningful financial capacity

---

MANAGED_AI_DEPARTMENT

Trigger when:

- Multiple opportunity flags are active
- Company size is meaningful
- Leadership has urgency
- Ongoing implementation is likely

---

# Recommendation Priority

Every recommendation should be assigned one of four priority levels.

Priority 1:

Immediate opportunity with clear business impact.

Priority 2:

High-value improvement that should follow Priority 1.

Priority 3:

Longer-term optimization.

Priority 4:

Optional or future opportunity.

---

# Recommendation Value Categories

Each recommendation should be labeled as one or more of:

Revenue Growth

Cost Reduction

Labor Efficiency

Customer Experience

Employee Productivity

Risk Reduction

Management Visibility

Competitive Advantage

---

# ROI Evidence

Whenever possible, recommendations should reference measurable business inputs.

Examples:

Employee hours saved

Labor cost avoided

Future hires avoided

Lead response improvement

Recovered missed calls

Additional leads converted

Advertising waste reduced

Accounts receivable collected faster

Reporting time reduced

Customer service response improved

---

# Important Rule

Do not present the Commercial Opportunity Score to prospects.

It is an internal sales qualification tool.

The public-facing experience should focus on:

- AI readiness
- business opportunities
- practical recommendations
- measurable improvement
