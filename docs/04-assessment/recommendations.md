# AI Department Assessment Recommendation Engine

Status: Draft
Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

The recommendation engine converts assessment findings into practical business recommendations.

It should answer four questions:

1. What problem or opportunity did we identify?
2. What is the likely business impact?
3. What should the company do about it?
4. How can Your AI Department help implement the solution?

Recommendations must be based on actual assessment findings.

The system should not recommend services simply because we sell them.

---

# Recommendation Structure

Every recommendation should contain:

## Finding

What the assessment discovered.

## Business Impact

How the issue may affect:

- Revenue
- Labor cost
- Employee productivity
- Customer experience
- Growth
- Management visibility
- Risk

## Recommended Action

What should be changed.

## Potential Solution

The technology, process, AI system, automation, training, or strategy that could address the problem.

## Your AI Department Service

Which service could help implement the recommendation.

## Priority

Priority 1 - Immediate

Priority 2 - High

Priority 3 - Medium

Priority 4 - Future

# Human Review Requirement

AI automation should not automatically eliminate human review in areas involving:

- Legal decisions
- Medical decisions
- Financial approvals
- Hiring and firing decisions
- Safety-critical operations
- Regulatory compliance
- Significant financial commitments
- Sensitive customer decisions

Appropriate human oversight should be designed into the workflow.

# Retainer Qualification

A company becomes a strong Managed AI Department candidate when:

- Three or more meaningful opportunity categories exist
- Leadership has high AI urgency
- Company has sufficient implementation capacity
- Multiple departments are involved
- Technology will require ongoing optimization
- New opportunities are likely to emerge
- Executive leadership wants ongoing guidance

The retainer should be justified by ongoing value, not simply by bundling unrelated services.

---

# Final Recommendation Principle

The assessment should ultimately answer:

Where can this company use AI, automation, better technology, or improved marketing to:

- Make more money
- Save money
- Save time
- Increase employee capacity
- Improve customer experience
- Improve management visibility
- Scale without proportional overhead

Those findings become the foundation for the strategy conversation.
