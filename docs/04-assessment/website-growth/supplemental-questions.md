# Website & Growth Assessment — Supplemental Questions

Status: Architecture Draft Authority

## Objective

Ask only questions that cannot be reliably determined from public website analysis.

Keep the assessment short.

Target:

5–10 questions for most users.

Use conditional branching where useful.

## Candidate Questions

### CRM

Where do new website leads currently go?

Options may include:

- Email inbox
- CRM
- Scheduling system
- Call center / team
- Multiple systems
- Not sure
- Other

### Lead Response

How quickly are new website inquiries typically contacted by a person?

Potential options:

- Within 5 minutes
- Within 15 minutes
- Within 1 hour
- Same business day
- Next business day or later
- Varies / not tracked
- Not sure

Do not present response-time benchmarks unless supported by approved evidence.

### Automated Acknowledgment

Do new website leads automatically receive an immediate acknowledgment?

- Yes
- No
- Sometimes
- Not sure

### Lead Assignment

Are new leads automatically assigned or routed to the correct person/team?

- Yes
- No
- Partially
- Not sure

### Follow-Up

Does your system automatically create reminders, tasks, or follow-up sequences for uncontacted leads?

- Yes
- No
- Partially
- Not sure

### Marketing Attribution

Can you identify which marketing source generated a lead after the lead enters your CRM or business system?

- Yes, reliably
- Partially
- No
- Not sure

### Revenue Attribution

Can you connect a closed customer or sale back to the original marketing source?

- Yes, reliably
- Partially
- No
- Not sure

### Phone Attribution

Are inbound phone calls attributed back to marketing source or campaign?

- Yes
- Partially
- No
- Not sure
- Phone calls are not a meaningful lead source

### Paid Media

Do you currently use paid advertising?

- Google Ads
- Meta Ads
- Both
- Other paid channels
- No

Allow multi-select if appropriate.

### Lead Reactivation

Do you systematically follow up with old leads or past customers?

- Yes, automated
- Yes, mostly manual
- Occasionally
- No
- Not sure

## Optional Industry Question

Industry may be collected at intake.

Use it to personalize examples and recommendations where appropriate.

Do not change deterministic score solely because an industry is considered "better" or "worse."

## Question Design Rules

Questions must:

- be answerable by a business decision-maker
- avoid unnecessary technical jargon
- avoid shame-oriented language
- include "Not sure" where appropriate
- not require the user to know implementation details they cannot reasonably know

## Branching

Only show questions that are relevant.

Example:

If paid advertising = No

do not ask detailed Google Ads tracking questions unless there is another reason.

If phone calls are not a meaningful lead source

do not penalize lack of call tracking.

## Scoring

Each answer that affects scoring must map to a deterministic score rule.

No free-text answer should directly determine numeric scoring.

Free text may be collected separately for context but treated as untrusted input.
