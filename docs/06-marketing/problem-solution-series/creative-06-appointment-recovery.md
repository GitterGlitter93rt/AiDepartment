# Creative 06 - Appointment Recovery

Status: Script ready
Series: YAD Business Problem Series

## Business problem

Booking the appointment is not the end of the workflow. Customers forget, schedules change, confirmations get missed, and rescheduling can create repetitive front-office work.

The employee is not the problem. The workflow may lack enough consistency around confirmations, reminders, and recovery.

## Core message

**Booking is not the finish line. Keep the appointment process moving.**

YAD can add confirmation, reminder, rescheduling, and follow-up workflows around the calendar while employees handle conversations that need a person.

## Positioning guardrails

- Do not promise lower no-show rates without verified data.
- Do not imply every missed appointment is recoverable.
- Do not suggest constant messaging or spam.
- Respect business-defined cadence, channel permissions, and escalation rules.

## Preferred 30-second narration

> Booking the appointment is only half the job. Customers forget, schedules change, and an empty slot still costs your business time. Your team can call and text reminders manually, but that gets harder as volume grows. Your AI Department can add confirmations, reminders, rescheduling, and follow-up around the appointment process, while your staff handles the conversations that need a person. Keep the calendar moving without making your front desk chase every appointment by hand.

## Visual story

### 0-6 sec - Appointment booked

- Front desk or sales rep completes booking.
- Deterministic calendar card: `Appointment booked`.

Overlay:

`Booked.`

Then:

`But not finished.`

### 6-12 sec - Schedule risk

- Customer gets busy / looks at calendar / realizes conflict.
- Deterministic status: `Needs confirmation` or `Schedule changed`.

Overlay:

`Plans change.`

### 12-17 sec - Manual burden

- Employee works through calls/messages while handling normal front-office tasks.
- No blame, no frustration acting.

Overlay:

`Confirm`
`Remind`
`Reschedule`
`Follow up`

### 17-25 sec - Better workflow

Deterministic YAD sequence:

`APPOINTMENT`
→ `CONFIRMATION`
→ `REMINDER`
→ `RESCHEDULE IF NEEDED`
→ `HUMAN HANDOFF`

Keep the cadence generic and configurable.

### 25-30 sec - Outcome

- Calendar remains organized.
- Front desk handles an in-person customer or exception.

Overlay:

`Keep the calendar moving.`

CTA:

`YourAiDepartment.ai`

## Audio direction

- Off-camera narrator.
- Subtle notification sounds only; no barrage of text pings.
- Light office ambience.
- No spoken domain.

## Primary QA gates

- No invented no-show statistic.
- No guarantee that reminders save an appointment.
- Human escalation remains available.
- Messaging cadence is presented as configurable.
- Staff are not blamed for missing manual follow-up.
