# Creative 07 - Dormant Lead Reactivation

Status: Script ready
Series: YAD Business Problem Series

## Business problem

Older inquiries, unsold estimates, and leads that went quiet are often ignored because the sales team must focus on today's active opportunities. Some records may still be worth another conversation, but reactivation takes time and consistency.

## Core message

**Your next opportunity may already be sitting in your CRM.**

YAD can help segment older opportunities, reach out through approved channels, capture responses, and hand interested contacts back to the team.

## Positioning guardrails

- Do not call the entire database revenue.
- Do not imply every old lead is still valid, contactable, or interested.
- Do not guarantee recovered sales.
- Respect consent, suppression, channel, and legal requirements.
- Human sales remains responsible for meaningful conversations and closing.

## Preferred 30-second narration

> Your next opportunity may already be sitting in your CRM. Old inquiries, unsold estimates, and leads that went quiet do not automatically mean the customer is gone forever. The problem is your sales team usually has to focus on what is new today. Your AI Department can help segment older opportunities, follow up through approved channels, capture responses, and hand interested customers back to your team. You keep selling what is active. The system keeps checking what may still be worth a conversation.

## Visual story

### 0-6 sec - Old opportunities exist

- Sales rep works today's leads.
- Deterministic CRM graphic shows older records below the active queue.

Overlay:

`Today's leads`

Then reveal below:

`Older opportunities`

### 6-12 sec - Why they stay untouched

- Rep handles active customer / call / estimate.
- Older records remain idle.

Overlay:

`The team has to work what's active.`

### 12-17 sec - Diagnosis

- Deterministic database card: `Old inquiry`, `Unsold estimate`, `Went quiet`.

Overlay:

`Not every old lead is dead.`

Use carefully; do not imply all are recoverable.

### 17-25 sec - Reactivation workflow

`SEGMENT`
→ `APPROVED OUTREACH`
→ `RESPONSE`
→ `INTERESTED`
→ `HAND TO SALES`

Optional support:

`Suppress invalid / opted-out records`

Keep small only if readable.

### 25-30 sec - Human + system

Sales rep receives an interested response while continuing active work.

Overlay:

`You sell what's active.`
`The system checks what may still be worth a conversation.`

CTA:

`YourAiDepartment.ai`

## Audio direction

- Off-camera narrator.
- Light office ambience.
- No cash-register effects or fake sales alerts.
- No spoken domain.

## Primary QA gates

- No claim that all old records are opportunities.
- No claim that database size equals revenue.
- Approved-channel language remains present.
- Human handoff is clear.
- CRM graphics use fictional/generic records only.
