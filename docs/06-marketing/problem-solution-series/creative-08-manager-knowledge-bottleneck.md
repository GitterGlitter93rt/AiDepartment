# Creative 08 - Manager Knowledge Bottleneck

Status: Script ready
Series: YAD Business Problem Series

## Business problem

Employees repeatedly interrupt the same manager or senior employee for routine operational answers because policies, procedures, pricing rules, and institutional knowledge are scattered across documents, messages, and people's heads.

The manager is valuable. The employee asking the question is not the problem. The information system is the bottleneck.

## Core message

**Keep the expertise. Remove the bottleneck.**

YAD can organize approved company knowledge into an internal AI assistant so employees can find routine answers faster while managers stay focused on judgment, exceptions, coaching, and consequential decisions.

## Positioning guardrails

- Do not imply the AI replaces managerial judgment.
- Do not present unverified or outdated company knowledge as authoritative.
- Use approved source material and clear ownership/update processes.
- Sensitive, legal, financial, HR, safety, or consequential questions should route to human review where appropriate.
- Do not promise universal accuracy.

## Preferred 30-second narration

> If every employee has to stop the same manager to ask the same operational questions, your best people become the search bar for the whole company. Policies, pricing rules, procedures, and answers may already exist, but they are scattered across documents, messages, and people's heads. Your AI Department can organize approved company knowledge into an internal AI assistant, so employees can get routine answers faster while managers stay available for judgment and exceptions. Keep the expertise. Remove the bottleneck.

## Visual story

### 0-6 sec - Repeated interruptions

- Competent manager is reviewing work or helping a customer.
- First employee approaches with a routine question.
- Manager answers briefly and returns to task.

Overlay:

`Quick question...`

### 6-12 sec - Pattern repeats

- Another employee approaches later with a different routine question.
- Manager pauses again.
- No eye rolling, sighing, or employee blame.

Overlay:

`Where's that policy?`

Then:

`What's the process?`

### 12-17 sec - Diagnosis

Deterministic visual shows knowledge spread across:

`Docs`
`Email`
`Messages`
`SOPs`
`People`

Overlay:

`The expertise exists.`
`Access is the bottleneck.`

### 17-25 sec - Better workflow

Deterministic YAD workflow:

`APPROVED COMPANY KNOWLEDGE`
→ `INTERNAL AI ASSISTANT`
→ `ROUTINE ANSWER`
→ `SOURCE / CONTEXT`
→ `ESCALATE EXCEPTION`

Do not generate fake source citations. Use generic approved-document cards.

### 25-30 sec - Human expertise protected

- Employee gets routine guidance at workstation.
- Manager handles a meaningful exception / coaching / decision.

Overlay:

`Keep the expertise.`
`Remove the bottleneck.`

CTA:

`YourAiDepartment.ai`

## Audio direction

- Off-camera narrator.
- Natural office ambience.
- Subtle interruption cues, not cartoon notification sounds.
- No spoken domain.

## Primary QA gates

- Manager does not look annoyed or replaceable.
- Employees do not look incompetent.
- AI is grounded in approved company knowledge.
- Human escalation remains clear.
- No universal-accuracy claim.
- This ad clearly expands YAD beyond phone/sales workflows.
