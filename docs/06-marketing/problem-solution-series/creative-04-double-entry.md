# Creative 04 - Double Entry

Status: Script ready
Series: YAD Business Problem Series

## Business problem

Employees repeatedly copy the same customer, lead, appointment, claim, or job information between systems that do not communicate cleanly.

The employee is not the problem. The workflow is asking a person to become the integration layer.

## Core message

**If your team is typing the same information into three systems, that may be a systems problem - not a staffing problem.**

YAD can connect appropriate workflows so routine information moves where it belongs and employees spend less time re-entering the same data.

## Positioning guardrails

- Do not promise any specific integration until technically verified.
- Do not imply all manual entry can or should be removed.
- Keep human review for exceptions, approvals, consequential actions, and unreliable source data.
- Do not say employees are wasting time; attack the workflow.

## Preferred 30-second narration

> If your team keeps typing the same customer information into multiple systems, that may not be a staffing problem. It may be a systems problem. Every copy, paste, spreadsheet update, and manual handoff pulls attention away from work that actually needs a person. Your AI Department can connect the workflow so information moves where it belongs, routine records get updated, and your team handles the exceptions. Less retyping. Fewer handoffs. More capacity from the team you already have.

## Visual story

### 0-6 sec - Same information, again

- Competent office employee receives a customer record or form.
- Types into system A.
- Opens another system and begins typing the same details again.
- No sighing / exaggerated frustration.

Overlay:

`Same customer.`
`Same information.`

### 6-12 sec - The human becomes the bridge

- Spreadsheet / CRM / scheduling / management-system switching shown through deterministic graphics.
- Employee checks one screen, then another, then paper.

Overlay:

`System A`
→ `Person copies it`
→ `System B`

Then:

`Again.`

### 12-17 sec - Diagnosis

Narration lands:

`Not necessarily a staffing problem.`

Then:

`A systems problem.`

### 17-25 sec - Better workflow

Deterministic diagram:

`SOURCE SYSTEM`
→ `YAD WORKFLOW / INTEGRATION`
→ `CRM / SCHEDULE / TASK`

Supporting steps:

`Capture once`
→ `Route appropriately`
→ `Update routine records`
→ `Human reviews exceptions`

Do not display named integrations unless actually supported and approved.

### 25-30 sec - Human capacity

Return to employee handling a customer, reviewing an exception, or completing a meaningful task.

Overlay:

`Less retyping.`
`More capacity.`

CTA:

`YourAiDepartment.ai`

## Audio direction

- Off-camera narrator.
- Natural office sounds: typing, mouse, printer/paper, light movement.
- Avoid cartoon keyboard clacking.
- No spoken domain.

## Primary QA gates

- Employee appears competent.
- The repeated-entry action is obvious even muted.
- No unsupported integration promise.
- Human review remains in the workflow.
- No staff-replacement message.
- The ad materially expands YAD beyond phone agents.
