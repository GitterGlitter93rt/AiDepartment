# Creative 05 - Attribution Blind Spot

Status: Script ready
Series: YAD Business Problem Series

## Business problem

A company spends money on marketing, gets calls and forms, and may even close business, but cannot reliably connect the original source to the final outcome. The marketing may be working; the measurement system may be incomplete.

## Core message

**A lead is not the finish line. Can you trace the ad to the sale?**

YAD can help connect the journey from source to inquiry to booked opportunity to revenue where the business's systems and data support it.

## Positioning guardrails

- Do not claim attribution is perfect.
- Do not imply all offline revenue can always be tied back automatically.
- Do not invent spend, CPL, ROAS, or revenue figures.
- Do not blame the current agency without evidence.
- Use capability language: connect, capture, pass, reconcile, report.

## Preferred 30-second narration

> Your ads can be generating leads and you still may not know what's actually making you money. A click becomes a form, a call becomes an appointment, and somewhere between the ad platform and the final sale, attribution disappears. Your AI Department can help connect the journey from source to lead to booked opportunity to revenue, so you can see which marketing is creating real business. Stop judging campaigns by clicks alone. Build the system that shows what happens after the lead.

## Visual story

### 0-5 sec - Marketing looks active

- Business owner/manager sees ad platform charts or campaign activity.
- Exact UI recreated deterministically; no real account data.

Overlay:

`Clicks are not the finish line.`

### 5-11 sec - Lead enters

Deterministic journey:

`AD`
→ `CALL / FORM`
→ `LEAD`

Narrator explains that marketing can generate activity without complete downstream visibility.

### 11-17 sec - Attribution breaks

The journey visually disconnects after lead creation.

Overlay:

`What happened after the lead?`

Then:

`Booked?`
`Sold?`
`Revenue?`

No invented values.

### 17-25 sec - Connected measurement

YAD system visual:

`SOURCE`
→ `LEAD`
→ `BOOKED`
→ `OPPORTUNITY`
→ `REVENUE`

Support copy:

`Capture source`
`Preserve attribution`
`Connect downstream outcomes`

Do not promise a specific CRM integration unless verified.

### 25-30 sec - Decision clarity

Return to owner/manager reviewing a clean dashboard.

Overlay:

`Know what creates business.`

CTA:

`YourAiDepartment.ai`

## Audio direction

- Off-camera narrator.
- Quiet office / keyboard ambience.
- No casino-like analytics sounds, cash-register effects, or exaggerated notification SFX.
- No spoken domain.

## Primary QA gates

- No fake account data.
- No fabricated performance numbers.
- The gap between lead and revenue is visually clear.
- YAD is shown as building measurement infrastructure, not merely running ads.
- Attribution limitations remain honest.
