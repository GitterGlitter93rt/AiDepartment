# Your AI Department — Structured Data Plan

Status: V1 Planning Authority

## Principle

Structured data must accurately describe visible page content.

Do not use schema to imply credentials, ratings, reviews, customers, pricing, events, or services that are not actually present.

## Organization

Use appropriate organization/business identity structured data where supported by the technical SEO specification.

Include only factual information.

## Website

Use WebSite structured data where appropriate.

## Breadcrumbs

Use BreadcrumbList on interior pages where the visible information architecture supports breadcrumbs or equivalent hierarchy.

## Services

Service structured data may be used for genuine service pages when technically appropriate.

Do not manufacture prices or geographic service areas.

## FAQ

FAQ structured data should only be considered where:

- FAQ content is visible to users
- questions and answers are real
- implementation complies with current search-engine requirements

Do not assume FAQ rich results will be displayed.

## Articles / Resources

Future educational resources may use Article or BlogPosting structured data where appropriate.

Required factual fields should be present before implementation.

Do not fabricate author credentials or publication information.

## Enterprise

Do not add enterprise customer, review, award, or client schema without factual authority.

## Review Policy

Every structured-data implementation must be validated during technical QA.
