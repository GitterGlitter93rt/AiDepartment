## Audience

Business owners, marketing leaders, sales leaders, and service businesses using Google Ads.

## Core Position

Clicks and form submissions are not the final business outcome.

A useful paid-search measurement system should connect advertising activity to qualified leads, sales activity, closed business, and revenue where the company's systems make that possible.

## Recommended Structure

### H2 — Why Lead Counts Are Not Enough

Two campaigns can generate the same number of leads while producing very different business outcomes.

What matters may include:

- lead quality
- contact rate
- appointment rate
- sales qualification
- close rate
- revenue
- profitability

### H2 — Define the Full Lead Journey

Suggested model:

Search
→ Ad
→ Landing Page
→ Conversion
→ CRM
→ Contact
→ Qualification
→ Appointment / Estimate
→ Sale
→ Revenue

### H2 — Capture the Original Marketing Source

Discuss preserving attribution information when a lead enters the CRM.

Potential fields:

- source
- campaign
- landing page
- form
- call source
- timestamp
- campaign identifier where available

Avoid prescribing a specific technical architecture universally.

### H2 — Connect Marketing and Sales Data

The marketing platform knows what generated the lead.

The CRM or business system often knows what happened after the lead arrived.

Useful attribution requires connecting those systems.

### H2 — Track Meaningful Conversion Stages

Potential stages:

- new lead
- contacted
- qualified
- appointment scheduled
- estimate/proposal
- won
- lost
- revenue

Adapt stages to the actual business.

### H2 — Phone Calls Need Attribution Too

For many service businesses, phone calls may be a major conversion path.

Discuss call tracking and CRM association conceptually.

Do not endorse a specific vendor unless approved.

### H2 — Offline Conversion Data Can Improve Decision-Making

Explain conceptually how qualified or closed outcomes can be associated back to advertising when the technical stack supports it.

Do not make guarantees about platform optimization.

### H2 — Build Reporting Around Business Outcomes

Useful reporting should help answer:

- Which campaigns create qualified opportunities?
- Which campaigns create revenue?
- Which keywords produce poor-quality leads?
- Which landing pages convert?
- Where are leads failing after acquisition?

### H2 — Attribution Is a System, Not a Dashboard

Emphasize:

Ad Platform
+ Website
+ Call Tracking
+ CRM
+ Sales Process
+ Revenue Data
