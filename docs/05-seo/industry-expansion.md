# Your AI Department — Industry Expansion Roadmap

Status: Approved Planning Direction

## Principle

Industry expansion should prioritize valuable industries where AI, automation, software integration, employee productivity, governance, and operational transformation can create meaningful business opportunities.

Do not create industry pages simply to generate SEO volume.

Each industry page must demonstrate real understanding of the industry's business workflows, risks, constraints, and decision-making environment.

## Wave 2 Industries

### Healthcare

Planned route:

/industries/healthcare/

Working search positioning:

AI Consulting & Business Transformation for Healthcare Organizations

Potential areas:

- administrative workflow improvement
- scheduling
- patient communication support
- employee productivity
- internal knowledge
- reporting
- system integration
- AI governance
- human review

Guardrails:

Do not provide medical advice.
Do not imply AI replaces clinical judgment.
Do not make unsupported HIPAA/compliance claims.
Do not imply access to protected health information unless the actual architecture supports it.

## SEO Requirement

Every new industry page must define before build:

- target decision-maker
- primary search intent
- canonical route
- SEO title
- meta description
- H1
- real business problems
- potential transformation opportunities
- human-review requirements
- claim guardrails
- relevant service links
- relevant future resource links
- CTA

Do not use a generic industry template with only the industry name replaced.
