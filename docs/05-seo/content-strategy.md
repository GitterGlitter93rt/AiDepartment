# Your AI Department — V1 Content & SEO Strategy

Status: Approved V1 Planning Authority

## Purpose

The website should build organic authority around practical AI, automation, implementation, growth systems, employee productivity, and measurable business improvement.

SEO content must support real business expertise.

The goal is not to manufacture pages simply to target keywords.

Content should:

- answer real buyer questions
- support commercial service and industry pages
- demonstrate operational understanding
- create useful internal-linking paths
- help prospects understand when AI is and is not appropriate
- support qualified lead generation
- strengthen topical authority over time

## Core Principle

More pages do not automatically create better SEO.

Do not create large quantities of thin, repetitive, substantially similar, or low-value content.

Do not create pages solely by replacing an industry or city name in an otherwise identical template.

Do not create unsupported case studies, statistics, ROI claims, testimonials, customer stories, awards, rankings, or performance claims.

## Search Intent Model

Every future page should have a defined primary search intent.

Possible intent classes:

1. Commercial
2. Problem-aware
3. Educational
4. Comparison
5. Evaluation
6. Implementation
7. Industry-specific
8. Executive / governance

Do not publish multiple pages targeting effectively the same primary intent without a clear reason.

## Content Quality Standard

Every supporting resource should do at least one of the following:

- explain a business problem
- provide a useful framework
- answer a meaningful question
- explain how a system works
- help a buyer evaluate an option
- explain implementation considerations
- identify risks or limitations
- show what should be measured
- clarify when human judgment remains necessary

## Business-Value Framing

Content should lead with business problems rather than AI novelty.

Preferred framing:

Business Problem
→ Operational Cause
→ Potential AI / Automation / Process Opportunity
→ Risks / Constraints
→ Implementation Considerations
→ Measurement

Avoid framing such as:

"AI is changing everything."

Prefer:

"Here is the operational problem, what causes it, and where AI or automation may or may not help."

## Content Production

V1 should begin with a small set of strong resources.

Recommended initial production:

3–5 resources

Then expand based on:

- Search Console data
- actual buyer questions
- sales conversations
- assessment patterns
- service demand
- industry demand

Do not publish 25 weak pages simply to create volume.

## Content Ownership

All final content must comply with:

- company positioning authority
- launch decisions
- offer documentation
- service documentation
- industry documentation
- visual identity
- technical SEO specification

When repository authorities conflict, resolve the conflict before publishing.

## No Automatic Publishing

AI may assist drafting.

AI-generated content must not be automatically published without:

- factual review
- business review
- claim review
- internal-link review
- metadata review
- human approval

## Resources Hub — Approved Direction

The public educational content section will use:

/resources/

Public label:

Resources

The section should function as a business knowledge center rather than a chronological company blog.

Primary audiences include:

- business owners
- CEOs and executives
- operations leaders
- CIOs and CTOs
- IT leaders
- marketing and growth leaders
- transformation decision-makers

Content may include:

- Guides
- Frameworks
- Founder Perspective
- Downloadable tools and checklists

Future content may include industry-specific resources supporting approved industry pages.

The Resources architecture should use Astro content capabilities rather than requiring WordPress.

Each resource must have deliberate:

- URL slug
- SEO title
- meta description
- H1
- search intent
- author
- audience
- category
- related commercial destination
- internal links
- CTA
- schema where appropriate
