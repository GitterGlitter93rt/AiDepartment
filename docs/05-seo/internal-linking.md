# Your AI Department — Internal Linking Architecture

Status: Approved V1 Planning Authority

## Principle

Internal links should help users understand relationships between problems, solutions, services, industries, and next steps.

Do not add links merely for SEO volume.

Use descriptive anchor text where practical.

## Supporting Resource Linking

Every future resource should normally link to:

1. one primary commercial page
2. one or two closely related supporting resources
3. one appropriate next-step CTA

Do not insert links into every paragraph.

## Assessment

The AI Department Assessment remains the primary SMB/mid-market diagnostic CTA.

Relevant commercial and educational pages may link to:

/ai-assessment/

Enterprise content should not force all enterprise prospects into the public assessment.

## Booking

When scheduling is implemented:

/book/

will provide centralized booking-path selection.

Do not hardcode individual Calendly URLs in article content.
